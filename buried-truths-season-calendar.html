EVENT GUIDE • SEASON CALENDAR

BURIED TRUTHS SEASON

SEPTEMBER 28 — OCTOBER 25

The official Buried Truths Season calendar is here. Four weekly event groups bring Metida, Talismans, new Skins, Relic resources, discounts and a progression path connected to the new Elarite Supertitan.

IMPORTANT — PLAN WITH THE LIVE EVENT

The official announcement states that quests and rewards may change. The calendar also does not provide every milestone quantity, shop price, drop rate or guaranteed acquisition cost. Use this guide to understand the Season structure and important dates, then compare the live requirements before spending resources.

WEEK ONE

ANCIENT AWAKENING

SEPTEMBER 28 — OCTOBER 4

The Season begins with Metida. Ancient Awakening provides ways to collect her Soul Stones and regular Skin, while a unique item for Metida's Skin+ appears among event rewards and in the Event Shop.

METIDA TAKES THE SPOTLIGHT

Before spending Event Coins, check the live requirements and the price of the Skin+ item. The Event Shop also lists Pallant and Orm Soul Stones and Artifact fragments, Primordial Skins for Hyperion, Araji and Eden, and Relic Shards for Cascade and Satori.

Firstborn Force

Rewards include Pallant and Orm Soul Stones, Titan resources, Hero Skin Stone Chests, Mastery Crystals, Metacubes, Realm packs and other items.

Quests include logging in, Fairy Dust spending, Tower, Artifact and Summoning Chests, activity or VIP points and Raid Leader boss victories.

Unstable Equilibrium

Adds Eva and Crow Soul Stones, Metida fragments, Rune Spheres, Elemental Spheres and additional resources.

Objectives include Rune Stone, Elemental Sphere and Emerald spending, Arena and Grand Arena battles, Research speedups, logins and VIP points.

Ancient Awakening

Offers Metida Soul Stones and Artifact fragments, Rigel's Biomech Skin and additional resources.

Objectives include Heroic and Outland Chests, Expeditions, Event Shop spending, Realm troop losses and quest completion in the other two event tabs.

LINKED EVENT: Check all three tabs before treating a task as finished. Progress in Firstborn Force and Unstable Equilibrium also feeds Ancient Awakening.

WEEK 1 DISCOUNTS & OFFERS

  • October 1–4: 4× Emerald offers
  • October 1–3: Outland Chests
  • October 1–2: Elemental Spheres
  • October 3–4: Heroic Chests
  • October 4: Summoning Spheres

RESOURCE TIP: Do not rush a chest or sphere quest on day one if you intend to use its matching discount. Also avoid opening or buying resources solely for a reward before seeing the actual milestone cost.

WEEK TWO

BOUNTIFUL ROADS

OCTOBER 5 — OCTOBER 11

Week Two shifts the focus toward Talismans. Event Coins from Reward Boxes can be used in the Trading Chamber to obtain them, while the Event Shop also features Tome Relic fragments and additional resources.

GUUS RELICS — OCTOBER 5

The previously announced Guus Relics arrive on October 5. The Season trailer says his heals can crit, he gives out more feathers and he weakens enemy critical hits. The complete Relic descriptions and battle performance should be judged separately once the mechanics can be tested.

Roads Unlocked

Provides Pallant and Orm Soul Stones, Rune resources, Metacubes and Realm packs.

Quests include Fairy Dust, Skin Stones, Tower Chests, Expeditions, logins, VIP points and Raid Leader boss victories.

Grand Caravan

Listed rewards include Luther and Cornelius Talismans, Eva and Crow Soul Stones, Orm fragments, Rune and Elemental resources and red item recipe fragments.

Quests include Artifact Coins and Emeralds, Artifact Chests, Arena battles, Realm troop losses, logins and VIP points.

Bountiful Roads

Offers Demonic Skins for Chabba and Mushy and Shroom, Eva and Crow Soul Stones and additional resources.

Objectives include Talisman rerolls, Energy spending, Realm speedups and completion of the other two event tabs.

TALISMAN PLANNING: Use rerolls deliberately. Check how Event Coins and Reward Boxes function when the event goes live, then compare the Trading Chamber options with the Talismans your account actually needs.

OCTOBER 9 — OCTOBER 11

SEER'S GAME RETURNS

Flip cards with Mama Chabba for a chance at Cornelius's Ocean Echo Skin. The calendar presents this as a chance-based reward and does not provide a guaranteed cost, so consider setting a limit before chasing it.

WEEK THREE

ASCENDANT GLORY

OCTOBER 12 — OCTOBER 18

Week Three turns toward Crow and a broad selection of Hero and Titan upgrades.

CROW'S OCEAN ECHO SKIN & SKIN+

The official calendar names a special offer for Crow's Ocean Echo Skin and Skin+. Do not assume these are normal Event Shop purchases. The shop instead lists Cascade and Satori Relic fragments and Artifacts for Eva, Qing Mao and Fafnir among other items.

Rising Legend

Offers Crow Soul Stones, Pallant fragments, Hero Skin Stones, Artifact resources and Titan and Realm materials.

Quests include Fairy Dust, Summoning Spheres, Chaos Cores, Hydra battles, Arena fights, VIP points and Raid Leader bosses.

Trial of Legends

Lists Soleil's Demonic Skin and Skin+, Eva and Crow Soul Stones, Pallant and Orm fragments and upgrade resources.

Objectives include Rune Stones, Titan Potions, Emeralds, Heroic Chests, activity, VIP points and a Realm speedup mechanic.

Spark of Glory

Includes Demonic Skins for Helios and Fafnir, Fafnir's Stellar Skin, Qing Mao's Lunar Skin+, Soleil's Talisman, Soul Stones and additional resources.

Quests include Outland Chests, Realm troop recruitment and completion of Rising Legend and Trial of Legends.

DO NOT TURN ARTWORK INTO CONFIRMATION

The calendar confirms Fafnir rewards during this week, but it does not identify an Ocean Echo Skin for Fafnir. The character shown in earlier promotional artwork should therefore not be treated as confirmation by itself.

OCTOBER 16 — OCTOBER 18

  • 4× Emerald offers
  • Outland Chest discount

If an Outland milestone is part of your plan, compare these dates with the live thresholds and with what you would normally spend.

WEEK FOUR

BALANCE OF POWER

OCTOBER 19 — OCTOBER 25

The Season closes with an Honor Hero focus. The Event Shop lists Luther's Ocean Echo Skin and Skin+, Cascade and Satori Relic Shards, and Eva and Crow Soul Stones and Artifacts.

LUTHER RELICS — OCTOBER 19

October 19 is the announced release date for Luther's Relics. His Stun and Omen can affect the rhythm of battle, but the complete Relic text and battle testing are still required before judging how the new Relics affect his role.

Defiant Edge

Offers Pallant and Orm Soul Stones, Titan resources, Artifact Chests and keys, Mastery Crystals, Metacubes and Fairy Dust.

Objectives include Energy, Skin Stone and Elemental Sphere spending, Arena battles, VIP points, logins and Raid Leader bosses.

Unbroken Bond

Includes Faceless's Demonic Skin and Skin+, Eva and Crow Soul Stones, Rune materials, Artifact resources and red recipe fragments.

Quests include Summoning Spheres, Titan Skin Stones, Emeralds, Tower Chests, troop recruitment speedups, Realm troop losses, logins and VIP points.

Gear & Glory

The Luther upgrade tab includes two previously released Skins, Soul Stones, an Artifact and a glove recipe. It also lists Yasmine's Demonic Skin and Phobos's Dark Depths Skin.

Its quests focus on logging in and completing objectives in Defiant Edge and Unbroken Bond.

LINKED EVENT: Work through Defiant Edge and Unbroken Bond according to your account needs, then check what that progress unlocks in Gear & Glory.
