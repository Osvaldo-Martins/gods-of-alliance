GODS OF ALLIANCE • RESOURCE STRATEGY

How to Maximize Your Rewards

01 — Prioritize Daily Progression

Complete Firstborn Force objectives consistently. Daily milestones are essential to both direct rewards and Titan's Roar progression.

02 — Combine Quest Objectives

Use Elemental Spheres earned through other quests to progress through Altar of the Elements. Avoid treating each event tab as an isolated objective.

03 — Plan Your Emerald Spending

If Skin Plus is your goal, evaluate the discounted Outland Chest offer. These purchases can advance both Outland and Emerald spending milestones.

04 — Protect Your Resources

Do not sacrifice troops or spend valuable saved resources without comparing the rewards against the cost of replacing those resources.

05 — Track Alecto's Skin

If Alecto's Primordial Skin is one of your objectives, monitor the 63-quest Elemental Tempest milestone.

06 — Make Your Final Purchases Carefully

Use the additional 24-hour Event Shop period to review your final balance and select rewards that support your account development.

IMPORTANT RESOURCE DISTINCTION

Rune Stones and Rune Spheres are different resources. Unstable Equilibrium requires spending Rune Stones, while Rune Spheres are available among the rewards.

Is Valdur's Skin Plus Worth Your Emeralds?

The answer depends on your account, available resources and long-term Titan development strategy.

Both baseline scenarios provide enough Eternal Marks for Valdur's Primordial Skin. Skin Plus, however, represents a significantly larger investment.

BASE SKIN ROUTE

100,000 Eternal Marks

A practical option for players who can complete the baseline milestones but want to preserve their Emeralds.

After purchasing the base skin, the projected remaining balance is between 170,450 and 173,950 Eternal Marks.

Those Marks can be invested in other Event Shop rewards according to your account priorities.

SKIN PLUS ROUTE

450,000 Eternal Marks

An option for players committed to Valdur's development who have sufficient resources available.

Our projected discounted Outland investment is between 111,375 and 114,750 Emeralds.

Additional optional quest rewards may reduce this cost. Evaluate the investment against your other priorities.

The base skin is achievable under both projected baseline routes. Skin Plus requires a much greater commitment.

Do not spend simply because a reward is available. Spend because that reward supports your long-term strategy.
