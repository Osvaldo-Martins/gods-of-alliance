SUBEVENT 01 • DAILY PROGRESSION

Firstborn Force

Firstborn Force is the foundation of our reward strategy. Most quests reset daily, making consistent progression across all seven days extremely important.

QUEST 01

Game Login

Log in every day to collect Eternal Marks, Titan Skin Stones and Brawl Tokens.

Seven logins provide 3,500 Titan Skin Stones and 90 Brawl Tokens.

2,100 Eternal Marks
QUEST 02

VIP

Rewards include Titan Skin Stones, Rune Spheres, Season Points, Adventure Coins and Elemental Spheres.

VIP rewards are excluded from our baseline calculation.

Daily milestones range from 250 to 4,500 activity points.

GODS OF ALLIANCE • STRATEGY

How to Maximize Your Rewards

01 — Prioritize Daily Progression

Complete your Firstborn Force objectives consistently. Daily milestones are essential to both direct rewards and Titan's Roar progression.

02 — Combine Quest Objectives

Use Elemental Spheres obtained from other quests to progress through Altar of the Elements. Avoid treating each event tab as an isolated objective.

03 — Plan Your Emerald Spending

If Skin Plus is your goal, evaluate the discounted Outland offer. These purchases can advance both Outland and Emerald spending milestones.

04 — Protect Your Resources

Do not sacrifice troops or spend valuable saved resources without evaluating the rewards and the cost of replacing them.

05 — Track Alecto's Skin

If Alecto's Primordial Skin is one of your objectives, monitor the 63-quest Elemental Tempest milestone.

06 — Make Your Final Purchases Carefully

Use the additional 24-hour Event Shop period to review your final balance and select rewards that support your account development.

FINAL ANALYSIS

Is Valdur's Skin Plus Worth Your Emeralds?

The answer depends on your account, available resources and long-term Titan development strategy. Both baseline scenarios provide enough Eternal Marks for the Primordial Skin, but Skin Plus represents a significantly larger investment.

BASE SKIN ROUTE

100,000 Eternal Marks

A practical option for players who can complete the baseline milestones but want to preserve their Emeralds.

After purchasing the base skin, the projected remaining balance is between 170,450 and 173,950 Eternal Marks.

Those Marks can be invested in other Event Shop rewards according to your account priorities.

SKIN PLUS ROUTE

450,000 Eternal Marks

An option for players committed to Valdur's development who have sufficient resources available.

The projected discounted Outland investment is between 111,375 and 114,750 Emeralds, before additional optional quest rewards.

Evaluate the investment against your other Titan and account development priorities.

GODS OF ALLIANCE VERDICT
The base skin is achievable under both projected baseline routes. Skin Plus requires a much greater commitment.

Do not spend simply because a reward is available. Spend because that reward supports your long-term strategy.
