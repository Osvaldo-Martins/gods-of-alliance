Ascended Glory

Valdur's Primordial Skin, Skin Plus & Maximum Rewards

Ascended Glory — Complete Event Overview

Ascended Glory brings together three interconnected subevents, offering players an opportunity to collect Eternal Marks, develop their Titans and obtain valuable rewards from the Event Shop.

This guide covers every quest category in Firstborn Force, Unstable Equilibrium and Ancient Awakening, alongside two practical progression routes and the estimated Emerald investment required to obtain Valdur's Primordial Skin Plus.

Primordial Skin 100,000 Eternal Marks
Skin Plus Upgrade 350,000 Additional Eternal Marks
Total Skin Investment 450,000 Eternal Marks

Event Shop — Rewards and Priorities

Valdur's Primordial Skin costs 100,000 Eternal Marks, while Skin Plus requires an additional 350,000 Eternal Marks.

The Event Shop also offers Titan fragments and Arachne Relic Shards, giving players alternative ways to invest their event currency.

The shop remains available for 24 hours after the event ends. Use this additional time to evaluate your final balance before purchasing your rewards.

Important: Titan Brawls contributes another 1,500 Eternal Marks to the projections used in this guide.

1. Firstborn Force

Most Firstborn Force quests reset daily. Consistency across all seven days is essential to maximizing rewards and progressing through Titan's Roar.

Game Login

Seven daily logins provide 2,100 Eternal Marks, 3,500 Titan Skin Stones and 90 Brawl Tokens.

VIP

Daily milestones range from 100 to 4,500 VIP Points. Rewards include Eternal Marks, Titan Skin Stones, Rune Spheres, Season Points, Adventure Coins and Elemental Spheres.

VIP rewards are excluded from the baseline projections.

Guilds' Activity

Daily milestones range from 250 to 4,500 activity points. Rewards include Pallant Fragments, Adventure Energy, Adventure Coins, Titan Potions, Fairy Dust, Mastery Crystals and Elemental Spheres.

Save the Elemental Spheres for Unstable Equilibrium progression when appropriate.

Fairy Dust

Spend Fairy Dust to complete daily milestones up to 10,000. Rewards include Metida Fragments, Sacred Guard Recipe Fragments, Titan Skin Stones, Rune Spheres, Meta Cubes and other valuable resources.

Some milestones also return Fairy Dust.

Hero Skin Stones

Daily milestones range from 500 to 10,000 Hero Skin Stones spent. Rewards include Eternal Marks, Titan Skin Stones, Adventure Coins, Sacred Guard Recipe Fragments, Mastery Crystals and Rune Spheres.

Check your available inventory before committing to all seven days.

Tower Chests

Open Tower Chests to complete daily milestones. Opening 11 chests reaches the 10-chest milestone, while opening 15 reaches the next milestone.

The difference between these two routes is 3,500 Eternal Marks across the event.

Valkyrie's Chests

The baseline projection includes opening 40 Artifact Chests per day. Rewards include Eternal Marks, Adventure Energy, Adventure Coins, Pallant Fragments, Fairy Dust, Season Points and Meta Cubes.

Hold a Raid

Defeat bosses as Raid Leader. The daily route includes up to 10 defeats, providing Realm resources, Elemental Spheres, Titan Skin Stones, Fairy Dust, Titan Potions and General Speedups.

11 Tower Chests Per Day 205,450 Eternal Marks
15 Tower Chests Per Day 208,950 Eternal Marks

2. Unstable Equilibrium

Unlike the daily Firstborn Force quests, Unstable Equilibrium milestones accumulate throughout the event.

Game Login

Seven daily logins provide 2,100 Eternal Marks and Titan Skin Stones.

VIP

The cumulative VIP track offers up to 47,000 Eternal Marks across the available milestones, alongside Eva Soulstones, Red Gear Fragments, Rune Spheres and additional resources.

Emeralds

Milestones range from 1,000 to 200,000 Emeralds spent. Completing the entire track provides 36,650 Eternal Marks.

Outland Chest purchases made with Emeralds can contribute to this track and the Ancient Awakening Outland quest simultaneously.

Research Speedup

Use research speedups to progress through milestones ranging from 60 minutes to 41 hours. The complete track provides 8,100 Eternal Marks, alongside Realm resources, Titan development materials and Hero Soulstones.

Altar of the Elements

Open Elemental Spheres to complete milestones. Opening 35 Spheres provides 1,000 Eternal Marks in our baseline. The full track reaches 875 openings.

Rune Stones

Spend Rune Stones to advance through milestones offering a total of 58,600 Eternal Marks across the full track.

Additional rewards include Titan Skin Stones, Red Gear Fragments, Meta Cubes, Hero Soulstones, Rune Spheres and Elemental Spheres.

Arena

Five daily battles provide 35 battles over seven days, earning 1,850 Eternal Marks in the baseline.

The final milestone is at 60 battles. Additional attempts are required to progress beyond the baseline.

Grand Arena

Completing 35 battles provides 2,850 Eternal Marks. The full track reaches 60 battles.

Evaluate the additional rewards before purchasing extra attempts.

Baseline Eternal Marks 7,800
Completed Quests 24 Elemental Tempest Progress

3. Ancient Awakening

Ancient Awakening combines repeatable quests, Event Shop spending and progression from both Firstborn Force and Unstable Equilibrium.

Game Login

Seven logins provide another 2,100 Eternal Marks and Titan Skin Stones.

Lose Troops

Lose level 6 or higher troops to advance through this repeatable quest. Each completed cycle provides 5,300 Eternal Marks.

Always consider the cost of rebuilding your troops before pursuing additional cycles.

Event Coins

Spend Eternal Marks in the Event Shop to unlock additional Titan Potions, Titan Skin Stones and Season Points.

Heroic Chest

Open Heroic Chests to complete repeatable milestones. Each full cycle provides 8,500 Eternal Marks, alongside Titan Potions and Elemental Spheres.

Outland

Each complete cycle requires 150 Outland Chest openings and provides 6,675 Eternal Marks.

Openings beyond the current cycle carry forward into the next one, making discounted batches particularly useful for players pursuing Skin Plus.

Expeditions

Begin Expeditions to advance through milestones up to 56. The complete track provides 7,600 Eternal Marks, alongside Fairy Dust, Titan Potions, Adventure Coins, Adventure Energy and Season Points.

Titan's Roar

Complete Firstborn Force quests to progress through Titan's Roar. Rewards include Valdur Fragments, Season Points, Titan Skin Stones and Rune Spheres.

Both baseline routes reach the 340-quest milestone, providing 50,350 Eternal Marks.

Elemental Tempest

Complete Unstable Equilibrium quests to earn Zorval's Call Fragments and other valuable resources.

Reaching 63 completed quests unlocks Alecto's Primordial Skin.

The baseline route provides 3,250 Eternal Marks from this track.

Ancient Awakening Baseline 55,700 Eternal Marks

How to Maximize Your Rewards

Complete Daily Quests Consistently

Prioritize Firstborn Force daily milestones to maximize Eternal Marks and Titan's Roar progression.

Combine Event Objectives

Use Elemental Spheres earned from other quests to progress through Altar of the Elements. Emerald purchases in Outland can also contribute to multiple event tracks.

Use the Outland Discount

If Skin Plus is your objective, plan your Emerald spending around the discounted Outland Chest offer.

Protect Your Resources

Do not spend valuable resources or sacrifice troops without comparing the rewards against the cost of completing those milestones.

Wait Before Final Shop Purchases

Use the additional 24-hour Event Shop availability to review your final Eternal Marks balance and select your rewards carefully.

Final Verdict — Is Valdur's Skin Plus Worth It?

Both baseline progression routes provide enough Eternal Marks to purchase Valdur's Primordial Skin, while leaving a substantial balance for additional Event Shop rewards.

However, pursuing Skin Plus requires a considerably larger investment. Our projections estimate an additional Emerald cost of approximately 111,375 to 114,750 Emeralds through discounted Outland Chest purchases.

For players with limited resources, obtaining the base skin and investing the remaining Eternal Marks in other valuable rewards may be a more practical approach.

Players committed to developing Valdur can evaluate Skin Plus against their available Emeralds, Titan development priorities and remaining event quests.

Plan your investment carefully. The best reward is the one that supports your long-term account progression.
