REALM MAJOR UPDATE

A major Realm roadmap is coming. New buildings, Level 30 progression, expanded research, inter-server Guild Versus PvP and a new Server Capital Presidency system. Explore the announced features and their expected release windows.

END OF OCTOBER NOVEMBER COMING AFTERWARD
01 / THE OFFICIAL ROADMAP

A New Era for the Realm.

The developers have revealed a substantial roadmap for the Realm in Hero Wars: Alliance, introducing changes planned for the end of October, throughout November and shortly afterward.

The roadmap goes beyond visual improvements. It introduces new structures, expanded building progression, additional research, Faction buildings, inter-server Guild Versus battles and a new competition for control of the Server Capital.

Some details have already been announced. Others, including exact upgrade requirements, combat rules and certain rewards, have not yet been revealed.

This Gods of Alliance breakdown separates announced features from details still awaiting confirmation, so Guardians can understand what is coming without treating speculation as established mechanics.

06 / NOVEMBER — MAJOR GAMEPLAY FEATURE

Realm PvP Comes to Guild Versus.

One of the biggest announcements is a new Realm PvP Day, introducing direct competition against rival Guilds across different servers.

Inter-Server Realm Warfare

Players will be able to teleport to the opposing Guild's server and attack enemy Realms. This expands Guild Versus beyond its current activities into a new battlefield involving attacking, defending and coordination.

Guild organization becomes central to this system. Members will need to coordinate movements and attacks while also protecting their own Realms.

The roadmap also confirms new Guild Versus quests with opportunities to earn significantly more points toward the highest rewards.

07 / REALM PVP DEFENSE

Protect Your Realm. Defend Your Resources.

The new Realm PvP system includes defensive mechanics as well as opportunities to capture resources.

01

Shields

Shields are confirmed as part of the new Realm PvP features. They will contribute to Realm defense, although their exact activation rules and limitations have not been detailed.

02

Wall Garrison

Players will be able to station troops as a Garrison on the Wall.

This introduces an important defensive component to Realm battles.

03

Resource Capture

Players will be able to capture resources during Realm PvP battles.

The exact capture conditions, quantities and restrictions remain unannounced.

04

Guild Defense Coordination

Defending a Realm becomes part of the broader Guild Versus competition.

Communication and organized preparation could become increasingly valuable as the new system develops.

09 / COMING SHORTLY AFTERWARD

The Server Capital. A New Seat of Power.

A new Fort will become the Capital of the server, introducing a competition for control and a new Presidency system.

♛

Who Will Rule the Server?

Players will be able to compete for control of the Server Capital and the opportunity to become President of their server. The position will offer powerful bonuses for the President and their allies.

SERVER PRESIDENCY Compete for the position of President through the new Capital system.
PRESIDENT BONUSES Unlock bonuses for the President and their allies.
MINISTER APPOINTMENTS Appoint Ministers from any Guild on the server.
01

Ministers From Any Guild

The President will be able to appoint Ministers from any Guild on the server.

These Ministers can receive additional bonuses, creating opportunities for cooperation between different Guilds.

02

A New Political Dimension

The Capital system could encourage competition, alliances and cooperation across Guild boundaries.

How this develops will depend on the final rules and the decisions made by players.

Not yet confirmed: The developers have not explained the exact Fort capture rules, how the President will be determined, the numerical bonuses or the final release date.
10 / INFORMATION STILL PENDING

What We Still Need to Know.

The roadmap confirms several major features, but some important details are still missing. These should not be treated as established mechanics until the developers provide more information.

Level 30 Upgrade Costs

Exact resource requirements, upgrade times and building benefits have not been announced.

Research & Faction Bonuses

The exact statistics, research branches, progression requirements and Faction building effects remain unknown.

Realm PvP Combat Rules

Detailed attack rules, Shield restrictions, Garrison interactions and resource capture conditions are pending.

Server Capital & Presidency

The exact Fort capture mechanics, Presidency selection rules, bonus values and release date have not been confirmed.

11 / GODS OF ALLIANCE — FINAL OVERVIEW

The Realm Is Entering a New Chapter.

At the end of October, the Realm is expected to receive a visual makeover, more convenient game-mode access and new buildings.

During November, building progression expands to Level 30, the Research Center receives additional branches, new Faction buildings arrive and Guild Versus introduces inter-server Realm PvP.

Shortly afterward, the Server Capital and Presidency system are planned to introduce another layer of competition and cooperation.

This roadmap has the potential to make Guild communication, planning and teamwork more important than ever. But the final impact will depend on the mechanics and numerical details that are still awaiting confirmation.

Gods of Alliance will continue following these developments as new official information becomes available.

Editorial note: This article is based on the developer roadmap. Release windows refer to the announced schedule and may change. Features without published numerical details are intentionally described without invented values.
