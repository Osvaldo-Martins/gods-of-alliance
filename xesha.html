GODS OF ALLIANCE • HERO GUIDE

XE'SHA

MAGE • WAY OF CHAOS • MIDDLE LINE

Xe'Sha is an explosive Magic Damage dealer who turns allied Health sacrifice into offensive power. Her abilities can target vulnerable enemies, empower magic-based allies and permanently increase her own Magic Attack as friendly skills cause teammates to lose Health.

WATCH FULL GUIDE →
ABILITIES

SKILLS

Xe'Sha's kit revolves around sacrifice, Magic Attack growth, priority-target burst and offensive support for her allies.

SKILL 01

Deadly Ray

Xe'Sha sacrifices 3% of the Health of every ally above 25% Health, then attacks the enemy with the lowest Magic Defense. Damage scales with her Magic Attack and the Health sacrificed. If the target dies, every sacrificed ally immediately recovers twice the Health they lost.

SKILL 02

Spiritual Power

Xe'Sha sacrifices a small portion of her allies' Health and grants every ally a powerful Magic Attack bonus for four seconds. Chaos heroes receive double the bonus.

SKILL 03

Crimson Mist

Xe'Sha covers the battlefield in crimson fog for four seconds, causing enemy basic attacks to miss completely. Its strength comes from the skill itself rather than Xe'Sha's Magic Attack.

SKILL 04 • PASSIVE

Soaring Might

Whenever an allied skill causes one of Xe'Sha's teammates to lose Health, Xe'Sha permanently gains additional Magic Attack. Repeated allied sacrifice allows her offensive power to continue growing throughout the battle.
