Xe'Sha — The Exiled Witch

Xe'Sha is one of the most explosive Magic Damage dealers in Hero Wars Alliance. The Exiled Witch transforms allied Health sacrifice into overwhelming offensive power while becoming increasingly dangerous as the battle continues.

MAGEPrimary class
CHAOSHero faction
MIDDLEBattle line
Abilities

Skills

Xe'Sha's abilities revolve around allied Health sacrifice, Magic Attack growth, priority-target burst and offensive support.

SKILL 01 • ULTIMATE

Deadly Ray

Xe'Sha sacrifices 3% of the Health of every ally above 25% Health before unleashing a devastating beam against the enemy with the lowest Magic Defense.

The damage scales with both her Magic Attack and the amount of Health sacrificed by her allies. If the target dies, every sacrificed ally immediately recovers twice the Health they lost.

SKILL 02

Spiritual Power

Xe'Sha sacrifices a small portion of her allies' Health. In return, every ally receives a powerful Magic Attack bonus for four seconds.

Chaos heroes receive double the bonus.

SKILL 03

Crimson Mist

Xe'Sha covers the battlefield in crimson fog for four seconds, causing enemy basic attacks to miss completely.

Crimson Mist does not scale with Magic Attack. Its strength comes from the skill itself.

SKILL 04 • PASSIVE

Soaring Might

Whenever an allied skill causes one of Xe'Sha's teammates to lose Health, Xe'Sha permanently gains additional Magic Attack.

Repeated allied sacrifice allows her offensive power to continue growing throughout the battle.

Core interaction

Chaos Sacrifice Synergy

Xe'Sha becomes especially dangerous alongside heroes who cause allied Health loss. Aidan is a key example: Phoenix's Ritual and Bonds of Fire repeatedly sacrifice Health, triggering Soaring Might and permanently increasing Xe'Sha's Magic Attack. Xe'Sha can then share that increased Magic Attack back with the team through Spiritual Power.

Build priority

Glyphs, Artifacts & Gift of the Elements

Glyphs

Bring every Glyph to around Level 40 before pushing further, unless you are specifically progressing Hero Classes.

  1. Magic Penetration

    Primary offensive priority.

  2. Health

    Helps Xe'Sha survive long enough to benefit from Soaring Might.

  3. Magic Attack

    Improves Deadly Ray and Spiritual Power.

  4. Intelligence

    Adds offensive power and Magic Defense.

  5. Magic Defense

    Lowest immediate priority.

Artifacts

  1. Weapon — Magic Attack

    Grants the entire team a Magic Attack bonus whenever Deadly Ray activates.

  2. Book — Magic Penetration + Magic Attack

    Provides two major offensive stats for Xe'Sha.

  3. Ring — Intelligence

    Valuable primary-stat development after Weapon and Book.

GIFT OF THE ELEMENTS

MAX IT

Xe'Sha benefits from every possible stat increase to remain alive while continuously scaling throughout the battle.

Build options

Talismans

TALISMAN 01

Intelligence + Magic Attack

Easier to obtain through the Eternal Frontier Shop and provides excellent offensive value.

TALISMAN 02

Armor + Magic Penetration

More difficult to acquire, but its Magic Penetration is valuable against heavily protected enemies.

Matchups

How to Counter Xe'Sha

Xe'Sha can be disrupted through Intelligence targeting, burst protection, target redirection, control, Energy disruption and resistance to Magic Damage.
