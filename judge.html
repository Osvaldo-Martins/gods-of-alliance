HERO GUIDE

JUDGE

CONTROL • WAY OF PROGRESS • MIDDLE LINE

Judge is one of the most underrated Control Heroes in the Way of Progress. The Guardian of the Universe can stun entire enemy teams, protect key allies with powerful shields and disrupt the rhythm of battle through repeated control effects.

HERO IDENTITY

What Is Judge?

Judge is a Progress Control Hero whose greatest strength is controlling the pace of the battlefield. His abilities combine repeated Magic Damage, protective shields, slowing effects and constant stuns. When supported by the right allies, Judge can prevent enemies from executing their strategies while giving his own team more time to attack.

SKILLS

Judge's Skill Kit.

Judge's true potential comes from the interaction between his abilities. His fourth Skill transforms the other three into additional sources of control, making repeated interruption the defining mechanic of his kit.

SKILL 01 • ULTIMATE

Ion Cyclone

Judge unleashes a massive electrical storm that strikes every enemy four times, dealing Magic Damage with each hit.

The damage scales with Judge's Magic Attack. This makes Ion Cyclone the foundation of his offensive contribution.

However, without enough Magic Penetration, the damage can struggle against enemies with strong magical defenses.

Orion becomes an excellent partner by providing additional Magic Penetration. Polaris contributes further control and Magic Critical Hit Chance, while Byrna supplies Energy generation and additional Magic Penetration.

KEY MECHANIC: Ion Cyclone strikes all enemies four times. With Enhanced Generator active, every strike also becomes a source of stun.
SKILL 02

Force Field

Judge places powerful shields on the leftmost and rightmost allies.

These shields absorb every type of damage, and their strength scales with Judge's Magic Attack.

The positioning is particularly important. Judge can help protect the frontline against heavy burst damage while also defending vulnerable backline Heroes.

This can be valuable against dangerous threats such as Yasmine, Jhu and Xe'Sha, especially when those extra seconds allow an important damage dealer to activate an Ultimate.

KEYWORD: SHIELD. Force Field protects two strategically important positions and becomes an additional source of stun through Enhanced Generator.
SKILL 03

Spacial Rift

Judge opens a dimensional rift in the middle of the enemy formation, slowing nearby enemies for seven seconds.

This ability depends only on Skill Level.

Slowing enemies disrupts their attack rhythm, delays Energy generation and can postpone important Ultimates.

This becomes especially effective alongside Julius, who accelerates the allied team while Judge simultaneously slows the opposition.

TIMING MATTERS. Judge slows the enemy while Julius accelerates allies, creating a powerful tempo advantage.
SKILL 04 • PASSIVE

Enhanced Generator

Enhanced Generator upgrades every one of Judge's other abilities.

Ion Cyclone: Every strike now stuns enemies.

Force Field: The shields stun nearby enemies.

Spacial Rift: The rift applies another stun when it disappears.

This passive depends only on Skill Level.

Judge's enemies are no longer simply taking damage or being slowed. They are repeatedly interrupted, losing opportunities to attack, cast abilities and build momentum.

SIGNATURE MECHANIC: Enhanced Generator transforms Judge into a continuous crowd-control specialist. Repeated stuns are the foundation of his battlefield value.
DEVELOPMENT

Skins & Glyphs.

Judge needs enough Magic Attack to strengthen his damage and shields, but he also needs sufficient durability to stay alive and continue applying control. For Skins, Magic Attack comes first.

Skin Priority

  1. Magic Attack
    First priority. Strengthens Ion Cyclone and the shields from Force Field.
  2. Health
    Improves survivability and gives Judge more time to control the battle.
  3. Default Skin — Intelligence
    Strengthens his magical development and provides additional Magic Defense.
  4. Magic Defense
    Helps Judge survive against magic-heavy compositions.
  5. Second Magic Attack Skin
    Valuable later, after the earlier development priorities.
FUTURE PRIORITY: If Judge receives another Health Skin, it immediately becomes the highest priority in this build.

Glyph Priority

  1. Health
  2. Magic Attack
  3. Magic Defense
  4. Armor
  5. Intelligence
Bring all five Glyphs evenly to Level 40. Beyond Level 40, further investment should depend on your objectives, including Skill Class progression.
ARTIFACTS

Artifact Priority.

Judge's development should begin with the Artifact that improves his two most important personal requirements: Magic Attack and Health.

PRIORITY 01

Artifact Book

Provides Magic Attack and Health. This combination improves Judge's offensive abilities, shield strength and survivability simultaneously.

PRIORITY 02

Artifact Ring

Provides Intelligence, further strengthening Judge's magical development while increasing his personal Magic Defense.

PRIORITY 03

Artifact Weapon

Activating Ion Cyclone provides the entire team with a Magic Attack bonus. Useful in magical compositions, but third when prioritizing Judge's personal development.

GIFT OF THE ELEMENTS

No Compromise Here.

RECOMMENDED DEVELOPMENT MAX IT

Judge needs solid fundamental stats to survive and repeatedly activate his abilities. Health and Magic Attack are central to his overall development, supporting survivability, shield strength and damage while allowing his control abilities to remain relevant throughout the battle.

SYNERGIES

Heroes That Work With Judge.

Judge becomes especially dangerous when his allies improve his Magic Penetration, accelerate his Energy generation or add even more crowd control.

Julius

Julius accelerates the allied team while Judge slows enemies. This creates a strong tempo advantage and allows the Progress team to maintain pressure.

Orion

Orion provides additional Magic Penetration, helping Judge's Ion Cyclone deal more effective damage against enemies with strong magical defenses.

Polaris

Polaris adds further control while increasing the team's chance of Magic Critical Hits, complementing Judge's repeated stuns and magical offense.

Byrna

Byrna provides Energy generation and additional Magic Penetration, supporting more frequent Judge Ultimates and improving their overall impact.

COUNTERS

How Can Judge Be Countered?

Judge depends on repeatedly applying control effects. The most effective answers protect against stuns, punish control, silence his abilities or eliminate him despite his defenses.

Sebastian

Ballad of Tenacity protects allies against stuns, dramatically reducing the impact of Judge's strongest control mechanic.

Soleil

Soleil punishes central enemies when Judge applies control effects, potentially turning Judge's own strategy against him.

Drayne

Once Drayne's maximum Health has scaled sufficiently high, he can execute Judge regardless of Judge's protective shields.

Thea

Thea can silence Judge, preventing him from casting the magical abilities that make his kit dangerous.

Crow

Crow's silence can interrupt Judge's spellcasting and reduce his ability to maintain battlefield control.

Mushy and Shroom

Silence provides another way to disrupt Judge and limit the repeated ability activations his strategy depends on.

FINAL ANALYSIS

Is Judge Worth Building?

Absolutely. Judge is one of the most complete Control Heroes in the Way of Progress.

His Force Field protects important allies. Spacial Rift disrupts enemy timing. Ion Cyclone provides repeated Magic Damage. And Enhanced Generator transforms those abilities into a continuous source of crowd control.

Judge also benefits from outstanding interactions with Julius, Orion, Polaris and Byrna, allowing him to support powerful Progress compositions.

His greatest strength is not simply how much damage he deals. It is his ability to control the enemy's opportunities to respond.

Gods of Alliance Verdict: Judge is an outstanding investment for players who value strategic control, defensive support and battlefield tempo.
