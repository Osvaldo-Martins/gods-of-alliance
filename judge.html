HERO GUIDE

JUDGE

CONTROL • WAY OF PROGRESS • MIDDLE LINE

Judge is one of the most underrated Control Heroes in the Way of Progress. The Guardian of the Universe can stun entire enemy teams, protect key allies with powerful shields and disrupt the rhythm of battle through repeated control effects.

HERO IDENTITY

What Is Judge?

Judge is a Progress Control Hero whose greatest strength is controlling the pace of the battlefield. His abilities combine repeated Magic Damage, protective shields, slowing effects and constant stuns.

SKILLS

Judge's Skill Kit.

Judge's potential comes from the interaction between his abilities. His fourth Skill transforms the others into additional sources of control, making repeated interruption the defining mechanic of his kit.

SKILL 01 • ULTIMATE

Ion Cyclone

Judge unleashes an electrical storm that strikes every enemy four times, dealing Magic Damage with each hit.

Damage scales with Judge's Magic Attack. Without enough Magic Penetration, the damage can struggle against strong magical defenses.

Orion supplies Magic Penetration. Polaris adds control and Magic Critical Hit Chance, while Byrna contributes Energy generation and additional Magic Penetration.

KEY MECHANIC: Ion Cyclone strikes all enemies four times. With Enhanced Generator, every strike also becomes a source of stun.
SKILL 02

Force Field

Judge places powerful shields on the leftmost and rightmost allies.

These shields absorb every type of damage, and their strength scales with Judge's Magic Attack.

This helps protect the frontline and vulnerable backline Heroes against threats such as Yasmine, Jhu and Xe'Sha.

KEYWORD: SHIELD. Force Field protects two important positions and becomes another source of stun through Enhanced Generator.
SKILL 03

Spacial Rift

Judge opens a dimensional rift in the middle of the enemy formation, slowing nearby enemies for seven seconds.

This ability depends only on Skill Level.

Slowing enemies disrupts their attack rhythm, delays Energy generation and can postpone important Ultimates.

Alongside Julius, who accelerates the allied team, Judge creates a strong tempo advantage.

TIMING MATTERS: Judge slows the enemy while Julius accelerates allies.
SKILL 04 • PASSIVE

Enhanced Generator

Enhanced Generator upgrades every one of Judge's other abilities.

Ion Cyclone: Every strike now stuns enemies.

Force Field: The shields stun nearby enemies.

Spacial Rift: The rift applies another stun when it disappears.

This passive depends only on Skill Level.

Enemies are repeatedly interrupted, losing opportunities to attack, cast abilities and build momentum.

SIGNATURE MECHANIC: Enhanced Generator transforms Judge into a continuous crowd-control specialist.
DEVELOPMENT

Skins & Glyphs.

Judge needs Magic Attack to strengthen his damage and shields, along with durability to stay alive and maintain control. Magic Attack comes first.

Skin Priority

  1. Magic Attack
    Strengthens Ion Cyclone and Force Field.
  2. Health
    Improves survivability and control uptime.
  3. Default Skin — Intelligence
    Strengthens magical development and provides Magic Defense.
  4. Magic Defense
    Helps Judge survive magic-heavy compositions.
  5. Second Magic Attack Skin
    Useful after the earlier priorities.
FUTURE PRIORITY: If Judge receives another Health Skin, it becomes the highest priority.

Glyph Priority

  1. Health
  2. Magic Attack
  3. Magic Defense
  4. Armor
  5. Intelligence
Bring all five Glyphs evenly to Level 40. Further investment depends on your objectives, including Skill Class progression.
ARTIFACTS

Artifact Priority.

Judge's development begins with the Artifact that improves his two most important personal stats: Magic Attack and Health.

PRIORITY 01

Provides Magic Attack and Health, improving damage, shield strength and survivability.

PRIORITY 02

Artifact Ring

Provides Intelligence, supporting magical development and Magic Defense.

PRIORITY 03

Artifact Weapon

SYNERGIES

Heroes That Work With Judge.

Judge becomes dangerous when allies improve his Magic Penetration, accelerate Energy generation or add more crowd control.

Julius

Accelerates allies while Judge slows enemies, creating a strong tempo advantage.

Orion

Provides Magic Penetration, helping Ion Cyclone deal more effective damage.

Polaris

Adds control and Magic Critical Hit Chance, complementing Judge's repeated stuns.

Byrna

Provides Energy generation and Magic Penetration, supporting more frequent and effective Ultimates.

COUNTERS

How Can Judge Be Countered?

Judge depends on repeated control effects. Effective answers protect against stuns, punish control, silence his abilities or bypass his defenses.

Sebastian

Ballad of Tenacity protects allies against stuns, reducing Judge's strongest control mechanic.

Soleil

Punishes control effects, potentially turning Judge's strategy against him.

Drayne

After sufficient maximum Health scaling, Drayne can execute Judge despite his shields.

Thea

Silence prevents Judge from casting important magical abilities.

Crow

Silence can interrupt Judge's spellcasting and reduce his battlefield control.

Mushy and Shroom

Silence can disrupt Judge and limit the repeated ability activations his strategy depends on.

FINAL ANALYSIS

Is Judge Worth Building?

Absolutely. Judge is a powerful Control Hero in the Way of Progress.

Force Field protects important allies. Spacial Rift disrupts enemy timing. Ion Cyclone provides repeated Magic Damage. Enhanced Generator transforms those abilities into a continuous source of crowd control.

Judge benefits from excellent interactions with Julius, Orion, Polaris and Byrna, allowing him to support powerful Progress compositions.

His greatest strength is not simply how much damage he deals. It is his ability to control the enemy's opportunities to respond.

Gods of Alliance Verdict: Judge is an outstanding investment for players who value strategic control, defensive support and battlefield tempo.
