JUDGE FULL GUIDE

CONTROL • WAY OF PROGRESS • MIDDLE LINE

Master Judge's constant stuns, protective shields, battlefield disruption and powerful Progress synergies. Discover the best build priorities, artifacts, talismans and counters for this underrated control Hero.

Judge is a Way of Progress Control Hero positioned on the middle line. Known as the Guardian of the Universe, he specializes in interrupting enemy teams with repeated stuns, slowing their momentum and protecting vulnerable allies through powerful shields. His value comes from controlling the pace of combat while contributing Magic Damage and defensive support.

1. Judge Overview

Judge is not a conventional damage dealer. His strength comes from combining area Magic Damage, defensive shields, movement disruption and repeated crowd-control effects.

His fourth skill upgrades the rest of his kit, turning ordinary attacks and support abilities into additional sources of stun.

This makes Judge particularly effective alongside Heroes who can strengthen his Magic Damage, accelerate Energy generation or add even more control.

2. Judge Skills Explained

First Skill — Ion Cyclone

Judge unleashes an electrical storm that strikes every enemy four times, dealing Magic Damage with each hit.

The damage scales with his Magic Attack. Because Ion Cyclone hits multiple enemies repeatedly, it becomes the foundation of Judge's offensive contribution.

Magic Penetration is important for improving its effectiveness against enemies with strong Magic Defense.

Orion is an excellent partner because he can provide additional Magic Penetration, allowing Judge's attacks to become more effective.

Polaris adds control and increases the team's chance of Magic Critical Hits, while Byrna contributes Energy generation and Magic Penetration.

Second Skill — Force Field

Judge places protective shields on the leftmost and rightmost allies. These shields absorb every type of damage.

Their strength scales with Judge's Magic Attack.

Key Mechanic — Shield

Force Field protects important allies on opposite ends of the formation. This can help the frontline survive heavy burst damage while shielding the backline from dangerous single-target threats.

This protection can be especially useful against Heroes such as Yasmine, Jhu and Xe'Sha.

Keeping an important damage dealer alive for even a few additional seconds can provide enough time to activate an Ultimate and change the outcome of the battle.

Third Skill — Spacial Rift

Judge opens a dimensional rift in the middle of the enemy formation, slowing nearby enemies for seven seconds.

This ability depends on Skill Level.

Slowing enemies disrupts their attack rhythm, reduces the pace of Energy generation and can delay the activation of important abilities.

Julius creates a particularly useful combination by accelerating Judge's allies while Judge slows the opposing team.

The result is a substantial tempo advantage, allowing the Progress team to act more frequently while the enemy struggles to maintain its rhythm.

Fourth Skill — Enhanced Generator

Enhanced Generator is the passive ability that transforms Judge into a powerful control specialist.

It improves all three of his other skills:

  • Ion Cyclone: Each strike can stun enemies.
  • Force Field: Stuns nearby enemies.
  • Spacial Rift: Applies another stun when the rift disappears.

Enhanced Generator depends on Skill Level.

Key Mechanic — Continuous Control

Judge does not rely on a single stun. His enhanced abilities create repeated interruptions, preventing enemies from attacking, casting skills and building momentum as effectively.

Combined with Heroes such as Polaris, Orion, Julius and Byrna, Judge can help create teams focused on sustained battlefield control supported by Magic Damage.

6. Artifact Priority

Judge's Artifacts provide Magic Attack, Health and Intelligence.

Priority Artifact Benefit
1st Book Magic Attack and Health
2nd Ring Intelligence
3rd Weapon Team-wide Magic Attack when Ion Cyclone activates

First Priority — Book

The Book provides both Magic Attack and Health. This improves Judge's damage, shields and survivability at the same time.

Second Priority — Ring

Intelligence further strengthens Judge's abilities and increases his personal Magic Defense.

Third Priority — Weapon

The Weapon grants Magic Attack to the entire team when Ion Cyclone is activated. It is useful in Magic Damage compositions, but this build prioritizes strengthening Judge himself first.

9. How to Counter Judge

Judge is extremely disruptive when his control abilities activate repeatedly. The most effective counters either protect allies from control, punish control effects, disable Judge or remove him from battle.

Sebastian — Protection Against Stuns

Sebastian's Ballad of Tenacity protects the team from stuns, dramatically reducing Judge's greatest strength.

Soleil — Punishing Control Effects

Soleil can punish central enemies whenever Judge applies control effects, turning Judge's own strategy against him.

Drayne — Execution Potential

Drayne can eventually execute Judge regardless of his shields once Drayne's maximum Health has scaled sufficiently high.

Thea, Crow and Mushy and Shroom — Silence

These Heroes can silence Judge, preventing him from casting his magic skills.

Without access to his abilities, Judge loses much of the control and protection that make him dangerous.

Final Verdict — Is Judge Worth Building?

Absolutely. Judge is one of the most complete control Heroes in the Way of Progress.

His shields protect important allies, his repeated stuns interrupt enemy strategies, and his slow effects create opportunities for the rest of the team.

With powerful synergies involving Julius, Orion, Polaris and Byrna, Judge can become the foundation of a highly disruptive Progress composition.

His greatest strength is not simply dealing damage. It is controlling when and how the opposing team can respond.

Gods of Alliance Verdict: Judge is an excellent investment for players who value battlefield control, defensive support and strategic team synergy.
