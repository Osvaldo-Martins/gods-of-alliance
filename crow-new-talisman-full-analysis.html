WHY ONLY +2,120?

Compare the Complete Build.

8,000 Physical Attack Does Not Mean +8,000 Final Attack

Talisman of Verdict gives Crow 8,000 Physical Attack as its main bonus. But it replaces Talisman of Deduction, which already contributes to Crow's attack through its 2,000 Agility.

That is why the actual displayed difference in this build is approximately +2,120 Physical Attack, rather than +8,000.

Health and Armor Penetration remain unchanged in this comparison. The real decision is therefore centered on offense and Critical Hit Chance versus considerably stronger physical protection.

CRITICAL HIT CHANCE

The Most Important New Stat.

With Verdict equipped, Crow's displayed Physical Crit Hit Chance reaches 6,440 in this build. The Talisman's tested raw rolls contribute 5,600 of that value.

Rating — Not a Fixed Percentage

Physical Crit Hit Chance is a rating, not a fixed percentage. Crow's actual probability of landing a critical hit depends on the target's main stat.

Different opponents can therefore produce different critical-hit probabilities.

Confirmed Sebastian Interaction

Sebastian's Hunter's Tune increases allies' Critical Hit Chance for five seconds, but excludes heroes who do not possess that stat.

Verdict now gives Crow Physical Crit Hit Chance directly. That makes Crow eligible for Hunter's Tune while Verdict is equipped.

This demonstrates why the value of the new stat goes beyond the number displayed on Crow's character sheet: it can also change which support effects he can benefit from.

CROW'S KIT

Why Physical Attack Matters.

Crow combines physical pressure with Silence. Physical Attack appears throughout his damage formulas, so the offensive direction of Verdict supports several parts of his kit.

Silent Judgment

Attacks enemies in front.

Holy Grenade

Targets the enemy with the highest Intelligence and deals area damage.

Swift Retribution

Speeds up Crow's skill recharge and gives his basic attacks an additional hit against silenced enemies.

Final Verdict

Adds damage when Crow reapplies Silence to an enemy already affected by it, while extending the effect.

FIRST VERDICT

Which Crow Talisman Should You Use?

Talisman of Verdict gives Crow a clear offensive upgrade path. More Physical Attack strengthens the damage formulas across his kit, while Physical Crit Hit Chance introduces a new offensive stat and enables confirmed interactions with support buffs that require that stat.

For an aggressive Crow build, Verdict is the preferred choice after these first tests.

But Talisman of Deduction has not become useless. Its Agility and substantially higher Armor remain valuable when Crow needs more physical protection.

The correct choice therefore depends on what the battle demands: Verdict for greater offensive potential; Deduction when physical survivability matters more.

These are still first examples. More teams, opponents, repeated attempts and direct Talisman comparisons are needed to measure the upgrade's contribution more precisely.
