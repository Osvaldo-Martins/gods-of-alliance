Crow — Deduction vs Verdict

Physical Attack, Critical Hit Chance or Armor? A closer look at Crow's two Talismans, his four skills and the offensive potential of his second Talisman.

Crow's second Talisman changes the direction of his build. His original Talisman of Deduction emphasizes Agility and Armor, while the Talisman of Verdict introduces Physical Attack and Critical Hit Chance. The question is not simply which Talisman provides more stats — it is which one better supports the job Crow is expected to perform.

Two Talismans. Two Different Purposes.

Crow's identity revolves around Physical Damage and Silence. His abilities can disrupt Intelligence-based enemies, while repeated Silence application contributes to his offensive pressure.

The original Talisman provides defensive protection. The second Talisman places greater emphasis on dealing damage. Neither direction should be judged by Power alone: the correct choice depends on Crow's role and the enemy composition.

Verdict takes Crow in a more aggressive direction. Physical Attack supports the damage scaling of his abilities, while Critical Hit Chance adds offensive potential to eligible physical hits.

The trade-off is the loss of the Armor provided by the original Talisman's secondary attributes.

Why Physical Attack Matters

The most important detail in this comparison is how Crow's abilities scale. All four skill damage values shown in our analysis depend on Physical Attack.

That gives the Talisman of Verdict a direct connection to Crow's existing kit. The additional Physical Attack is not simply a generic offensive stat — it contributes to the damage calculations associated with the skills we examined.

Critical Hit Chance is a separate consideration. It introduces the potential for critical physical hits, but we should not assume that every individual skill damage instance can critically hit.

103,104 Physical Damage

This creates a connection between repeated ability use, maintaining Silence and continuing offensive pressure.

SKILL ANALYSIS 04

Final Verdict

157,656 Physical Damage

When Crow applies Silence to an enemy who is already Silenced, that enemy receives additional damage.

Its damage depends on Physical Attack, making this another ability supported by Verdict's offensive main stat.

The Silence Connection

Crow's abilities do not operate independently. Silent Judgment and Holy Grenade apply Silence. Swift Retribution provides faster skill recharge and additional attacks against Silenced enemies. Final Verdict rewards applying Silence to an enemy who is already affected.

Together, these mechanics explain why Crow benefits from remaining active and repeatedly applying pressure.

The second Talisman does not introduce a new Silence mechanic. Instead, it supports the offensive side of the abilities Crow already possesses.

What Does Crow Gain — and Lose?

Stronger Physical Attack Scaling

Verdict's additional Physical Attack supports the displayed damage scaling across Crow's four skills.

Critical Hit Potential

The three Critical Hit Chance rolls add offensive potential, although critical-hit eligibility still matters.

Reduced Armor Investment

Moving away from Deduction means losing its secondary Armor rolls. That can matter against strong physical attackers.

Greater Focus on Crow's Role

Verdict prioritizes damage and offensive contribution, complementing Crow's repeated attacks and Silence-based control mechanics.

When Is Deduction Still Useful?

The original Talisman should not be dismissed. Its additional Armor can be useful when Crow is exposed to significant Physical Damage.

A Crow who cannot survive long enough to use his abilities effectively may gain more practical value from defensive protection than from additional offensive stats.

That is why matchup context matters. Verdict may be the preferred general build, but Deduction remains a reasonable alternative when survivability is the priority.

Our Final Verdict

GODS OF ALLIANCE ANALYSIS

Talisman of Verdict

Our preferred general choice for Crow is the Talisman of Verdict.

Its Physical Attack has a direct relationship with the damage scaling of Crow's abilities, while Critical Hit Chance adds another offensive dimension.

Deduction remains valuable when Crow needs additional protection against Physical Damage. However, when our priority is strengthening Crow's offensive contribution alongside his Silence-based control, Verdict is the more appealing direction.

The first Talisman helps Crow survive the battle. The second Talisman helps Crow do the job we brought him into the battle to do.
