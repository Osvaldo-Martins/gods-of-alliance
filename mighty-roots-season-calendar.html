SEASON OVERVIEW

Everything Coming This Season

Mighty Roots runs from October 26 through November 22. The announced schedule includes multiple event groups, daily gift calendars, major resource-spending opportunities, new skins, Talismans and anniversary rewards.

The goal of this guide is to help you identify which activities and resources matter during each event, where quests overlap, and when the announced discounts take place.

Important: Event quests, rewards and offers may change during the Season. Always confirm the final requirements inside the game when each event opens.

EVENT GROUP 01 • TALISMANS & TRADING

Bountiful Roads

OCT 26 — NOV 1

Three connected events focus on Talismans, resources and Event Coins. The Trading Chamber allows players to purchase Talismans using Event Coins obtained from Reward Boxes. Yasmine's Relic Shards and other resources are also announced for the shop.

Discount Schedule

  • October 29 – November 1: Big Sale with 4× Emerald offers.
  • October 29: Summoning Spheres discount.
  • October 30: Artifact Chest discount.
  • October 31 – November 1: Elemental Spheres discount.
  • November 1: Heroic Chest discount.

1. Roads Unlocked

Announced rewards: Metida and Pallant Soul Stones, Rune Spheres, Rune Stones, Metacubes and Realm resource packs.

Quest requirements: Log in, spend Fairy Dust and Skin Stones, start Expeditions, open Tower Chests, earn VIP Points and defeat bosses as a Raid Leader.

2. Grand Caravan

Announced rewards: Eva and Crow Soul Stones, Pallant Fragments, Oya and Mojo Talismans, Ignis' Biomechanical Skin, red equipment recipe fragments, Rune Spheres, Rune Stones, Metacubes, Mastery Crystals, Elemental Spheres, Realm resource packs and speedups.

Quest requirements: Log in, spend Artifact Coins and Emeralds, complete Realm troop-loss objectives, earn VIP Points, open Artifact Chests and start Arena battles.

The October 30 Artifact Chest discount may be relevant to the chest-opening quests.

3. Bountiful Roads

Announced rewards: Eva, Crow, Julius and Yasmine Soul Stones; Julius' Demonic Skin Plus; Yasmine's Cybernetic Skin Plus; Artemis' Demonic Skin; Sebastian's Dark Depths Skin; red equipment recipe fragments, Rune Spheres, Rune Stones, Mastery Crystals and Realm resources.

Quest requirements: Log in, reroll Talismans, use Realm speedups, spend Energy and complete quests from Roads Unlocked and Grand Caravan.

GODS OF ALLIANCE — PLANNING TIP

Progress through Roads Unlocked and Grand Caravan also contributes to objectives in Bountiful Roads. Review all three event requirements before committing your resources.

HALLOWEEN CELEBRATIONS

Holiday Every Day + Bingo

OCT 26 — NOV 1

Holiday Every Day

Log in daily and open the calendar windows to receive gifts. Announced rewards include Emeralds, Large Skin Stone Chests, Energy and other items.

Halloween Bingo

Complete quests to unlock Bingo cells. Rewards are available for individual cells, completed rows or columns, and completion of all Bingo quests.

Announced rewards: Emeralds, Adventure Coins, Adventure Energy, a spooky avatar and frame.

Witch's Gifts: Random rewards may include Rune Stones, World Energy, Skin Stones and Energy.

PLANNING TIP

Compare Bingo objectives with Bountiful Roads quests. Activities with overlapping requirements may help you progress in more than one event.

EVENT GROUP 02 • HEROES & TITANS

Balance of Power

NOV 2 — NOV 7

Balance of Power focuses on Hero development and Titan resources. The event shop is announced to include Mushy and Shroom's Crimson Grove Skin and Skin Plus, Relic Shards for Phobos and Dorian, and Eva and Crow Soul Stones and Artifacts.

1. Defiant Edge

Announced rewards: Pallant and Metida Soul Stones, red equipment fragments, Titan Skin Stones, Titan Potions, Artifact Chests, Mastery Crystals, Metacubes, Elemental Spheres, Artifact Chest Keys and Fairy Dust.

Quest requirements: Log in, spend Energy, Skin Stones and Elemental Spheres, earn VIP Points, start Arena battles and defeat a boss as a Raid Leader.

2. Unbroken Bond

Announced rewards: Jhu's Demonic Skin and Skin Plus, Eva and Crow Soul Stones, Rune Spheres, Rune Stones, Artifact Resource Chests, red equipment recipe fragments, Mastery Crystals and Artifact Chest Keys.

Quest requirements: Log in, spend Summoning Spheres, Titan Skin Stones and Emeralds, earn VIP Points, open Tower Chests, speed up troop recruitment and complete Realm troop-loss objectives.

3. Gear and Glory

Announced rewards: Mushy and Shroom's second Talisman, Gloves, Tribal Skin and Cybernetic Skin; Jorgen and Krista's Demonic Skins; Rune Stones and red equipment recipe fragments.

Quest requirements: Log in and complete quests from Defiant Edge and Unbroken Bond.

GODS OF ALLIANCE — PLANNING TIP

Mushy and Shroom players should review this week's shop and Gear and Glory rewards carefully. Progress in Defiant Edge and Unbroken Bond also contributes to the third event.

TENTH ANNIVERSARY

Holiday Every Day

NOV 2 — NOV 15

A second daily gift calendar celebrates the game's tenth anniversary. Open the calendar windows every day to collect the announced gifts.

Rewards include Emeralds, Skin Stone Chests, Adventure Coins, Adventure Energy, Seer's Orbs, Energy and other surprises.

TENTH ANNIVERSARY • GUILD CELEBRATION

Dominion Day

NOV 8 — NOV 15

Dominion Day introduces a Guild-wide anniversary activity. Complete event quests to earn Holiday Particles and work together to grow a giant birthday cake. Larger Guild cakes unlock greater rewards.

The shop is announced to include Mojo's Crimson Grove Skin and Skin Plus, Soul Stones and Artifact Fragments for Eva, Crow and Mojo, Skin Stones and other items.

Anniversary Discounts

  • November 13 – 15: Big Sale with 4× Emerald offers.
  • November 13 – 15: Outland Chests discount.

1. Dominion Day

Rewards: Seer's Orbs and Holiday Particles.

Quest requirements: Speed up Realm activities, recruit and spend troops, use Honor Marks and defeat other players' troops.

2. Grand Feast

Rewards: Seer's Orbs and Holiday Particles.

Quest requirements: Spend Artifact Coins, Relic Shards, Rune Spheres, Hero Skin Stones, Titan Skin Stones, Emeralds, Chaos Cores and Energy.

GODS OF ALLIANCE — RESOURCE STRATEGY

If an upgrade can reasonably wait until Grand Feast, using its required resources during the event may contribute to your anniversary progress and Guild cake. Prioritize upgrades that benefit your account.

ANNIVERSARY SPECIAL

Seer's Game

NOV 8 — NOV 16

Every Card Wins — No Black Card!

Mama Chabba returns with a special anniversary edition of Seer's Game.

The official announcement confirms that this edition has no black card. Every card is a winning card, so revealing a card will not end the round because of a black card.

Seer's Orbs are among the announced rewards from Holiday Every Day, Dominion Day and Grand Feast. These are important sources to watch during the celebration.

IMPORTANT DATE

Seer's Game continues through November 16, one day after Dominion Day and the anniversary Holiday Every Day calendar are scheduled to end.

FINAL EVENT GROUP

Ascendant Glory

NOV 16 — NOV 22

The final event group of Mighty Roots brings new development rewards and resource-spending quests.

Byrna's Crimson Grove Skin and Skin Plus are announced through special offers in the event shop. The shop also lists Relic Fragments for Phobos and Dorian, Artifacts for Eva and Crow, and other items.

November 20 – 22 Offers

Special offers on Summoning Spheres and Elemental Spheres are announced. Check the terms and active event requirements when these offers appear.

1. Rising Legend

Announced rewards: Crow Soul Stones, Pallant Fragments, red equipment recipe fragments, Rune Spheres, Hero Skin Stones, Artifact Resource Chests, Metacubes, Mastery Crystals, Elemental Spheres, Titan Skin Stones, Titan Potions and Realm resource packs.

Quest requirements: Log in, spend Fairy Dust, Summoning Spheres and Chaos Cores, earn VIP Points, fight the Hydra, participate in Arena battles and defeat bosses as a Raid Leader.

2. Trial of Legends

Announced rewards: Eva Soul Stones, Pallant and Metida Fragments, red equipment recipe fragments, Rune Spheres, Titan Skin Stones, Titan Potions, Hero Skin Stones, Metacubes and Mastery Crystals.

Quest requirements: Log in, spend Rune Stones, Titan Potions and Emeralds, earn Activity Points and VIP Points, open Heroic Chests and speed up any Realm activity.

3. Spark of Glory

Announced rewards: Mojo's Cybernetic and Lunar Skins; Crow's Talisman, helmet and Soul Stones; Metida and Pallant Soul Stones; Rune Spheres, Skin Stones and Realm resource packs.

Quest requirements: Log in, open Outland Chests, recruit Realm troops and complete quests from Rising Legend and Trial of Legends.

IMPORTANT RESOURCE DISTINCTION

Trial of Legends requires spending Rune Stones. Grand Feast earlier in the Season requires Rune Spheres. These are different resources.

GODS OF ALLIANCE — PLANNING TIP

If Crow is one of your development priorities, watch Spark of Glory for his announced Talisman, helmet and Soul Stones. Progress through Rising Legend and Trial of Legends also contributes to Spark of Glory.

SEASON STRATEGY

How to Prepare Your Resources

Before October 29 – November 1: Review the Bountiful Roads objectives and compare them with the announced Summoning Sphere, Artifact Chest, Elemental Sphere and Heroic Chest discounts.

Before November 2 – 7: Examine your Hero and Titan upgrade plans, particularly if you want Mushy and Shroom's announced rewards.

Before November 8 – 15: Check Grand Feast's resource requirements and coordinate with your Guild for the anniversary birthday cake.

During November 8 – 16: Collect available Seer's Orbs and remember that the anniversary Seer's Game has no black card.

Before November 16 – 22: Review Rising Legend and Trial of Legends before spending resources, since their completion contributes to Spark of Glory.

FINAL REMINDER

The announced calendar helps with preparation, but full quest milestones and final requirements should be checked when each event becomes available.

GODS OF ALLIANCE • FINAL VERDICT

A Season Built Around Preparation

Mighty Roots brings a wide range of activities, from Talisman rewards and Hero development to Halloween Bingo and the tenth anniversary celebrations.

The biggest opportunities come from understanding which quests overlap, when resource discounts occur, and how progress through connected events contributes to additional rewards.

Above all, the anniversary Seer's Game stands out because every card wins and there is no black card.

Plan your upgrades, collect the daily gifts, coordinate with your Guild and spend with intention.
