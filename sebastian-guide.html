HERO GUIDE

SEBASTIAN

SUPPORT • WAY OF PROGRESS • MIDDLE LINE

Sebastian is the Dominion's legendary bard, a Support Hero who can protect allies from Stuns and Curses, amplify Critical Hits and add devastating Pure Damage to physical compositions. His strength is not measured by personal damage alone. It is measured by how much stronger he makes his team.

HERO IDENTITY

The Bard Who Controls the Battle.

Sebastian is a Way of Progress Support Hero whose abilities combine anti-control protection, Critical Hit amplification, Armor reduction and additional Pure Damage. His entire skill set benefits from Physical Attack.

SKILLS

Sebastian's Four Skills.

Sebastian's skills are designed to work together. His protection keeps allies active, while his offensive abilities turn Critical Hits into a much greater threat.

SKILL 01 • ULTIMATE

Ballad of Tenacity

Sebastian conjures a shield in front of his team, protecting allies from Stun and Curse effects.

The shield loses health whenever the team takes damage, and its strength depends on Sebastian's Physical Attack.

Stuns and Curses can interrupt crucial abilities and completely disrupt a team's timing. Ballad of Tenacity helps prevent that.

Heroes such as Isaac and Julius can benefit greatly. Isaac can deploy his drones, while Julius can establish his shields without constantly being interrupted.

KEY MECHANIC: Ballad of Tenacity protects the team's battle rhythm. Its timing matters because the shield is not necessarily active immediately.
SKILL 02

Hunter's Tune

Sebastian increases the Critical Hit Chance of his allies for five seconds.

This buff applies only to heroes who already possess the Critical Hit stat. Its strength depends on Sebastian's Physical Attack.

Heroes can possess Critical Hit Chance naturally or obtain it from other sources. Jet can provide it through Inordinate Fury. Certain Talismans and rank items can also grant the stat.

Examples include Dante, Qing Mao, Kayla, Galahad, Astrid and Lucas, Ginger and Peech, depending on their development.

Yasmine is an especially dangerous partner because her Ultimate allows her to reach extremely high Critical Hit Chance.

IMPORTANT: Five seconds may sound short, but that window can be enough for a Critical Hit composition to decide the battle.
SKILL 03

Curse Song

At the beginning of battle, Sebastian curses the nearest enemy, reducing that enemy's Armor.

The curse remains active until Sebastian dies.

Because the nearest enemy is usually the tank, Curse Song can immediately weaken the opponent's frontline.

Physical attackers benefit from the reduced Armor. Heroes such as Cascade can exploit these weakened defenses to put greater pressure on the enemy tank.

TACTICAL VALUE: Curse Song can weaken the enemy frontline from the beginning of battle, but keeping Sebastian alive is essential.
SKILL 04 • PASSIVE

Rhythm of War

Whenever Sebastian or his allies perform a Critical Hit, they deal additional Pure Damage.

The additional damage depends on Sebastian's Physical Attack.

This is where Hunter's Tune becomes especially dangerous. One skill increases Critical Hit Chance, while the other rewards successful Critical Hits with additional Pure Damage.

Because Pure Damage bypasses Armor, the combination can be devastating against vulnerable enemies.

Yasmine is one of the clearest examples of a hero who can take advantage of this interaction.

SIGNATURE COMBINATION: Hunter's Tune increases Critical Hit opportunities, and Rhythm of War adds Pure Damage when those Critical Hits occur.
DEVELOPMENT

Skins & Glyphs.

Sebastian needs Physical Attack because all four skills benefit from it. He also needs survivability to maintain his support abilities throughout the battle.

Skin Priority

  1. Physical Attack Strengthens all four skills and should be the first priority.
  2. Default Skin — Agility Improves his main stat and contributes additional Armor.
  3. Health Keeps Sebastian alive to maintain Curse Song and support his allies.
  4. Toughness Helps reduce incoming damage and counter enemy Crushing.
  5. Magic Defense Improves survivability against magical threats.
  6. Critical Hit Chance Useful, but lower priority than the stats supporting his main role.
FUTURE PRIORITY: A new Physical Attack or Health Skin would become an important investment.

Glyph Priority

  1. Health
  2. Physical Attack
  3. Agility
  4. Magic Defense
  5. Critical Hit Chance
Bring all five Glyphs to Level 40 before investing further, unless you are specifically pursuing Skill Class progression.
ARTIFACTS

Artifact Priority.

Sebastian's Artifacts improve his own development and provide valuable support to physical compositions. Prioritize Ring, then Book, then Weapon.

PRIORITY 01

Artifact Ring

Provides Agility, strengthening Sebastian's main stat and contributing to his physical survivability.

PRIORITY 02

Artifact Book

Provides Physical Attack and Critical Hit Chance. Physical Attack strengthens his abilities, while Critical Hit Chance supports his offense.

PRIORITY 03

Artifact Weapon

Grants Physical Attack to the entire team when Ballad of Tenacity activates. The Artifact bonus applies before the Ultimate effect resolves, allowing the shield itself to benefit from the increased Physical Attack.

GIFT OF THE ELEMENTS

Keep the Bard Alive.

RECOMMENDED DEVELOPMENT MAX IT

Focus on Agility and Physical Attack. Sebastian's middle-line position does not make him immune to damage. He needs enough durability to survive and enough Physical Attack to maximize the value of his support abilities.

SYNERGIES

Heroes That Shine With Sebastian.

Sebastian is especially effective alongside heroes who benefit from Critical Hit Chance, additional Pure Damage and protection from control.

Yasmine

Her high Critical Hit Chance after activating her Ultimate combines dangerously with Hunter's Tune and Rhythm of War.

Jet

Jet can grant Critical Hit Chance to allies, allowing more heroes to benefit from Sebastian's offensive support.

Isaac

Ballad of Tenacity helps protect Isaac from disruptive control effects, allowing him to deploy his drones.

Julius

Sebastian's anti-control shield can help Julius establish his defensive abilities without being interrupted.

Dante

When Dante possesses Critical Hit Chance, he can benefit from Hunter's Tune and the additional Pure Damage provided by Rhythm of War.

Kayla

With Critical Hit Chance available through her development, Kayla can benefit from Sebastian's critical-focused support.

COUNTERS

How Can Sebastian Be Countered?

Sebastian can protect and amplify powerful physical teams, but he has weaknesses. Effective counters can punish Critical Hits, delay his protection or reduce physical damage.

Iris

Sebastian's buffs can give Iris opportunities to punish the enemy through her mechanics, making her dangerous against teams dependent on his support.

Helios

Flaming Retribution punishes enemies when they perform Critical Hits. Helios needs sufficient Magic Penetration to remain effective against strong Magic Defense. His first Talisman or support from Orion can help.

Jorgen

Jorgen can interfere with Sebastian's Energy generation before Ballad of Tenacity becomes active, delaying his protection and creating a window for control effects.

Somna

Somna can strongly reduce physical damage, making her useful against the physical Critical Hit compositions where Sebastian normally excels.

FINAL ANALYSIS

Is Sebastian Worth Building?

Absolutely — especially for teams built around Critical Hits.

Sebastian provides protection against Stuns and Curses, increases Critical Hit Chance, reduces enemy Armor and adds Pure Damage to successful Critical Hits.

His abilities are strongest when combined. Hunter's Tune creates more opportunities for Critical Hits, while Rhythm of War turns those attacks into additional Pure Damage.

He also provides defensive value. Ballad of Tenacity can prevent enemy control effects from disrupting the team's most important abilities.

However, Sebastian is not automatically the best choice for every composition. His value depends on whether his teammates can take advantage of his mechanics and whether the enemy has effective counters.

Gods of Alliance Verdict: Sebastian is an outstanding Support investment for well-designed Critical Hit compositions. He protects the team's timing, amplifies physical pressure and rewards players who understand the mechanics behind their team combinations.
