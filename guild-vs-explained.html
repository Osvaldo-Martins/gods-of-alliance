01 • THE BASICS

What Is Guild Versus?

Guild Versus is a cross-server competition built around four-week Seasons. From Monday to Saturday, players earn Guild Points through daily activities and planned upgrades. Sunday concludes the weekly contest and delivers the corresponding rewards.

EVENT STRUCTURE
4 Weeks

Each Season lasts four weeks. Tasks take place Monday through Saturday.

DAILY RESET
02:00 UTC

Daily quests and personal chest progress reset at 02:00 UTC.

ENTRY REQUIREMENT
Level 15

Participation begins at team level 15 after the opening tutorials.

How Does the Guild Competition Work?

Players can earn personal rewards individually. Guild membership unlocks the full competition. In Bronze, Silver and Gold, your guild faces another guild each week.

Guild Points count toward your personal daily chests and your guild's daily total. The guild with the highest daily score wins the day and earns Victory Points.

Tiebreaker: If the daily totals are equal, the guild that reached that score first wins.

What Happens at 4.5 Million Points?

If all three tiers are unlocked, 4,500,000 daily points reaches all nine personal chest milestones. You do not need to add the nine targets together.

Collecting all nine chests provides 4 Universal Relic Shards and 7 Relic Caskets, alongside other rewards.

Unclaimed personal rewards arrive in your mail at the end of the day. The Emerald number shown above a chest represents its equivalent value, not Emeralds contained inside.

05 • REWARD CONTENTS

What Can You Actually Earn?

Rewards include Energy, Gold, Titan Potions, Rune Stones, Honor Marks, Research Speedups, Troves, Universal Relic Shards and Relic Caskets. Some Troves let you choose the reward that best supports your account.

Universal Relic Shards & Caskets

Universal Relic Shards can support Relic progression. Relic Caskets give a random hero's Relic Shard.

Fully upgraded Relics stop receiving those random drops. If all Relics are maxed, Caskets provide Universal Relic Shards instead.

Normal Heroes Troves

Choose one reward per Trove from options including:

  • Artifact Essence, Scroll or Metal Chests
  • Meta Cubes
  • Sparks of Power
  • Big EXP Potions

Normal Titans Troves

Choose one reward per Trove from options including:

  • Titan Artifact Chests
  • Water, Fire or Earth Artifact Chests
  • Titan Skin Stones
  • Fairy Dust

Rare Heroes Troves

Options include:

  • Artifact Boxes and Artifact Coins
  • Rune Stones
  • Mastery Crystals and Meta Cubes
  • Sparks of Power and Huge EXP Potions

Rare Titans Troves

Options include:

  • Titan Artifact Chests
  • Light and Dark Artifact Chests
  • Titan Potions and Titan Skin Stones
  • Fairy Dust

Final Tier Three Chest

The final Tier Three chest includes 5 Rare Heroes Troves and 5 Rare Titans Troves.

Choose rewards based on the development priorities of your own account.

06 • CONTRIBUTION REQUIREMENTS

Three Targets You Must Not Confuse.

Personal chest milestones are separate from the participation requirements for daily, weekly and Season rewards.

DAILY PARTICIPATION
300,000

Earn 300,000 personal points in one day to qualify for Daily Participation Rewards.

WEEKLY PARTICIPATION
2,000,000

Earn two million points during the week to qualify for Weekly Victory Rewards if your guild wins, or Weekly Effort Rewards if it loses. You receive one package, not both.

SEASON REQUIREMENT
8,000,000

The League screen recorded in the guide requires eight million personal points across the Season.

Weekly Reward Timing

Weekly rewards are distributed on Sunday at 02:00 UTC. Packages improve with your league and may contain Artifact Coins, Honor Marks, Rune Stones, Troves and Skin Stone Chests.

Weekly Victory packages also include Universal Relic Shards. Each Skin Stone Chest lets you choose 10 Intelligence, Strength or Agility Skin Stones.

07 • LEAGUES

From Qualifying to Gold.

Promotion, Demotion & Season Rewards

In the Bronze standings shown in the guide, the top four guilds advance to Silver, while the bottom five positions are marked at risk of demotion to Qualifying.

League Rewards arrive at Season's end and depend on the guild's final league position and each player's individual ranking within the guild.

The reward tables group players into positions 1–10, 11–20 and 21–30. Higher placements and stronger leagues offer better packages.

The recorded leading rewards also include Fafnir's Ocean Echo Skin. Qualifying Rewards are distributed separately to guilds outside the main leagues, according to their final placement and member ranking.

08 • CHANGING GUILDS

What Happens If You Move?

Moving to another guild does not erase your personal progress, but it affects which guild receives your contribution and when you qualify for new guild rewards.

01

Your Old Guild Keeps Its Contribution

The points you already contributed remain with your previous guild. Your personal progress continues.

02

New Guild Rewards Have Requirements

Daily and weekly rewards from your new guild require the minimum points earned there and 72 hours of membership.

03

Season Rewards Follow the New League

Qualifying personal points count regardless of where they were earned. The Season reward package depends on your new guild's league.

09 • STRATEGY

How to Play Guild VS Efficiently.

The strongest strategy is not spending the most. It is using resources when they improve your account and generate valuable event points.

01

Prepare Your Resources

Save useful upgrades for their corresponding day. Titan upgrades on Monday, Skins on Tuesday, research on Wednesday, Hero development on Thursday, training on Friday and Enemy Hunt activities on Saturday.

02

Set a Personal Milestone

Choose a chest target that fits your account. You do not need to spend beyond your means simply to reach the highest tier every day.

03

Complete Supporting Tasks

Tower chests, Expeditions, Daily Quests, VIP Points and other repeatable activities can help you reach your chosen milestone.

04

Coordinate With Your Guild

Track the weekly Victory Point total. A coordinated guild can decide when additional resource spending is worthwhile.

05

Combine Events When Possible

When another event rewards the same action, consider completing both objectives together. Useful progression can deliver multiple rewards.

GODS OF ALLIANCE • FINAL VERDICT

Plan the Week. Win With Purpose.

Guild Versus is a competition built around planning, coordination and efficient progression. Every day offers opportunities to improve your account while supporting your guild.

Understand the six daily themes. Track your personal chest milestones. Respect the daily, weekly and Season requirements. And coordinate your spending with the guild's objectives.

The goal is not random spending. The goal is useful progression that earns rewards and helps your guild win.
