REALM BOSS LEVEL 30

The ultimate battle guide: how to combine percentage-based damage, Hero synergy, troop development and rally mechanics to challenge one of the most demanding Realm encounters.

Defeating a Level 30 Realm Boss is not simply a matter of selecting the five Heroes with the highest combat power. Troop development, rally participation, Realm Skills, positioning and the way Heroes combine their damage mechanics all influence the result. This Gods of Alliance guide examines a particularly aggressive composition built around Julius, Qing Mao, Jhu, Mojo and Eva.

1. Strategy Overview

One of the most frustrating situations in Realm combat occurs when players have powerful Heroes but relatively underdeveloped Realm Skills. Their Hero teams may perform extremely well elsewhere, yet struggle against the enormous troop strength of a Level 30 Realm Boss.

This strategy approaches the problem differently. Rather than relying exclusively on maximizing troop bonuses, it attempts to inflict enormous damage on the boss as early as possible, before the battle becomes overwhelmingly unfavorable.

The central concept is to combine attacks that scale with the boss's Health, offensive team bonuses, permanent Armor reduction and additional damage triggered by other Heroes.

JULIUS QING MAO JHU MOJO EVA

In the scenario examined here, Julius and Jhu are the two Heroes in this composition with Realm Skills. The other three contribute through their regular combat mechanics and synergy.

2. Troop Requirements and Rally Setup

A strong Hero composition does not remove the importance of troop development. A successful attempt still benefits significantly from maximizing the quality and quantity of troops participating in the rally.

Level 9 Troops

Infantry, Lancers and Shooters should all be developed to Level 9, the maximum troop level described in this strategy.

This provides the strongest possible starting troop level. Additional troop bonuses can also be obtained through research at the Research Center.

Build a Full Rally

Ideally, the Rally Leader should receive assistance from five complete teams, creating a full rally.

The objective is to bring the attacking troop count as close as possible to the boss's defending troop count at the beginning of combat. The boss's troops are also at maximum level, so a poorly filled rally can begin at a significant disadvantage.

Important: The composition can work with an average number of troops, but troop level, troop capacity, Hero power, research and rally participation remain important. This is not a guaranteed victory for every account.

3. Understanding the Realm Boss Mechanics

Attack and Defense Bonuses

During Realm combat, Attack and Defense bonuses are displayed in the upper corners of the battle screen. These bonuses can change rapidly as troops are defeated and as the battle progresses.

If the attacking troops are overwhelmed, the boss can receive an enormous bonus of up to 1,000% Attack and Defense.

Once those bonuses are active, the boss becomes considerably harder to damage and defeat. This is why the opening stage of the battle is so important.

Boss Attack Range and Hero Positioning

The boss's attacks cover a large area, affecting Heroes positioned in the front and middle lines. In the scenario described by this strategy, the back line remains outside the boss's main attack area.

This makes Eva's back-line positioning particularly valuable. While other Heroes are exposed to the boss's attacks, Eva can continue contributing substantial damage from a safer position.

4. Hero-by-Hero Analysis

Julius — Frontline Protection and Team Support

Julius is the frontline foundation of this composition. His role combines tanking, protection, support and healing. He also possesses Realm Skills that contribute to the rally's troop combat capabilities.

His shields help protect allies and can restore Health when destroyed, with additional benefits for Progress allies.

Julius can also grant a team-wide Critical Hit Chance bonus for nine seconds. This is especially useful for the physical damage dealers, while magical Heroes can also benefit through their physical basic attacks.

Qing Mao — Health-Based Pure Damage and Armor Reduction

Qing Mao is one of the key reasons this composition can perform so effectively against a boss with an enormous Health pool.

Her third skill, Dragon Claw, can inflict Pure Damage equal to 20% of the target's Health.

Her first skill provides a team-wide Armor Penetration bonus lasting nine seconds. When Qing Mao deals Physical Damage, she also steals Armor from her target. Her ability to Blind an enemy can further reduce the threat posed by physical attacks.

Most importantly for prolonged combat, her fourth skill permanently reduces the target's Armor. This creates an additional advantage for the team's physical attackers as the battle continues.

Jhu — Percentage-Based Pure Damage

Jhu is another essential damage dealer. His second skill, I Will Take Your Life, deals additional Pure Damage equal to 7% of the enemy's current Health.

Against a boss with a very large Health pool, this percentage-based mechanic becomes particularly valuable during the early stages of combat.

His first skill also grants a nine-second Critical Hit Chance bonus to the entire team, supporting the composition's aggressive offensive approach.

Eva — Maximum-Health Damage and Marksman Support

Eva contributes powerful damage and an important synergy with Jhu.

Her first skill, Impossible Shot, attacks the nearest enemy and deals Physical Damage plus additional Physical Damage equal to 25% of the target's maximum Health.

At the same time, Eva activates a nine-second team-wide Critical Hit Chance bonus.

Her fourth skill can increase her own damage and the damage of allied Marksmen, potentially reaching 400% of their original damage.

Jhu benefits directly from this Marksman synergy. Combined with Eva's protected back-line position, this creates an especially valuable partnership against the Realm Boss.

Mojo — Hex and Additional Damage

Mojo may initially seem unusual in a composition focused heavily on physical damage, but his fourth skill, Hex, provides an important additional source of damage.

Hex activates every two seconds and can inflict damage based on up to 80% of the damage received by the target, subject to a limit determined by a percentage of Mojo's current Health.

As Qing Mao, Jhu and Eva deliver their attacks, Mojo's Hex can contribute further damage, reinforcing the team's opening offensive pressure.

5. Why the Damage Synergy Works

The defining strength of this composition is not simply that every Hero deals damage. It is that several damage mechanics interact with the boss's enormous Health pool while the remaining abilities strengthen the overall offensive output.

The Most Important Mechanic: The Rally Leader Attacks First

The Rally Leader's Hero team attacks first. This means Julius, Qing Mao, Jhu, Mojo and Eva begin attacking while the boss still has its full Health.

That timing is critical. Percentage-based damage has the opportunity to remove a large portion of the boss's Health immediately, before the battle becomes more difficult.

In successful tests described by this strategy, the boss can lose most of its Health before the battle timer reaches the halfway point.

This does not mean every attempt will produce the same result. The effectiveness depends on Hero development, troop strength, rally size, Realm Skills and the overall battle conditions.

6. Recommended Battle Plan

  1. Prepare Level 9 troops. Develop Infantry, Lancers and Shooters to their maximum troop level.
  2. Improve research. Use the Research Center to strengthen troop-related bonuses.
  3. Organize a full rally. Aim for the Rally Leader plus five complete assisting teams.
  4. Use the recommended Hero composition. Julius, Qing Mao, Jhu, Mojo and Eva provide the central offensive synergy.
  5. Exploit the opening attack. The Rally Leader's Heroes attack first, allowing Health-based damage mechanics to become particularly effective.
  6. Watch the boss's Health. The objective is to remove as much Health as possible before troop losses allow the boss to become significantly stronger.
  7. Benefit from Armor reduction. Qing Mao's permanent Armor reduction can help the remaining physical attackers continue dealing damage later in the battle.

7. What Happens After Your Troops Are Defeated?

One of the most interesting aspects of this strategy is that the Hero team may still be able to finish the boss even after the attacking troops have been overwhelmed.

Normally, losing troops and allowing the boss to receive up to 1,000% Attack and Defense would make victory extremely difficult.

However, if the initial Hero attacks have already removed most of the boss's Health, the remaining Heroes may only need to inflict a relatively small amount of additional damage.

Qing Mao's permanent Armor reduction becomes especially important here. As the boss loses Armor, the team's surviving physical attackers can become more effective against it.

Eva's back-line positioning also matters, because she may avoid the boss's main front- and middle-line attack area.

Key distinction: The strategy does not make troop losses irrelevant. Instead, it attempts to deal enough damage early that the surviving Heroes may still complete the fight despite those losses.

8. Why Defeat Level 30 Realm Bosses?

Level 30 bosses are the highest-level Rally Bosses described in this guide. Defeating them allows players to obtain the maximum daily amount of small Skin Stone chests.

These encounters also provide the highest chance of obtaining valuable items associated with Leonel, including:

  • Red Gear Fragments
  • Weapon Artifact Fragments
  • Specific Skin Stones
  • Soul Stones

For players focusing on long-term progression, improving the consistency of their Level 30 boss victories can therefore be valuable.

9. Important Limitations

This composition is especially interesting for players who already possess powerful Heroes but have not yet fully developed their Realm Skills.

Nevertheless, the team is not a universal solution that ignores account development. Results still depend on several factors:

  • Hero combat power and development
  • Hero troop capacity
  • Troop level and troop count
  • Research Center bonuses
  • Rally participation
  • Realm Skill development
  • Battle timing and Hero survival

Players should treat this composition as a strong strategy worth testing, rather than a guaranteed victory under every possible condition.

Final Verdict — Gods of Alliance

Julius, Qing Mao, Jhu, Mojo and Eva form an aggressive Realm Boss composition centered on rapid Health-based damage and powerful Hero synergy.

Julius supplies frontline protection and support. Qing Mao delivers Pure Damage and permanent Armor reduction. Jhu attacks the enemy's current Health. Eva contributes maximum-Health damage and strengthens allied Marksmen. Mojo adds further damage through Hex.

The central objective is simple: remove an enormous portion of the boss's Health during the opening phase, before troop losses and the boss's escalating bonuses make the encounter significantly more difficult.

With appropriately developed troops, a well-supported rally and sufficiently powerful Heroes, this composition offers an interesting approach to defeating the Level 30 Realm Boss.
