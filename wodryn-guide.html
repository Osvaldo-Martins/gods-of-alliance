Hero overview

More Than a Frontline Tank

Master the Nature Tank's defensive support, Critical Hit mechanics, Energy generation, optimal build priorities and powerful hero synergies.

Wodryn is more than a Tank who stands at the front and absorbs damage. He combines cleansing, Critical Hit Chance support, Oak Bark protection, additional Energy generation and damage reduction.

His abilities create an unusual relationship between offense and defense: Critical Hit Chance can help allies deal more damage while also influencing their probability of reducing incoming damage.

This guide covers Wodryn's core mechanics, recommended build priorities and five promising hero partnerships.

Skill breakdown

Wodryn's Core Skills & Mechanics

Wodryn's identity revolves around frontline protection, Critical Hit Chance support and additional Energy generation.

SKILL 3

Deepwood Might

Increases Critical Hit Chance for allies positioned behind Wodryn.

This can improve offensive consistency while also interacting with the team's defensive mechanics.

SKILL 4

Unbowed Oak

Uses each hero's Critical Hit Chance to determine the probability of reducing incoming damage.

Critical Hit Chance therefore has both offensive and defensive value in Wodryn's teams.

SKILL 2

Bark Renewal

Provides cleansing against Debuffs and Control Effects.

This is a cleansing mechanic, not permanent immunity to effects that can be reapplied.

MECHANIC

Oak Bark & Energy

Wodryn gains additional Energy when taking damage while Oak Bark is active.

This creates potential interactions with heroes who benefit from allied Energy generation.

Important: Wodryn's cleanse should not be confused with continuous protection against negative effects. Enemies that repeatedly apply Debuffs or Control Effects can affect the team again after cleansing.

Wodryn's Relics — Explained and Tested

Wodryn has received his Relics, and we upgraded him to level ten to unlock Wildwood Fury and Bark Renewal. Below are all four effects, then nine battles with three attacking teams. The level twenty and level thirty effects are discussed from their descriptions; they are not unlocked or tested in these battles.

How his skills support the Relics

Crown of WrathPlaces a crown over the enemy team, dealing physical damage over time. These attacks always hit and cannot be evaded.
Bark RenewalRemoves debuffs and control effects from Wodryn and allies behind him, while healing Wodryn and giving him Oak Bark.
Deepwood MightUses those Oak Bark layers to increase Critical Hit Chance for allies behind him, and his own.
Unbowed OakGives Wodryn and allies behind him a chance to reduce incoming damage, based on each hero's Critical Hit Chance — so that stat has a defensive role as well as an offensive one.
RELIC LEVEL 1 · NEW SKILL

Wildwood Fury

TESTED

Lasts for nine seconds and is described as activating every fifteen seconds. While it is active, Critical Hits from Wodryn and his allies deal additional physical damage, based on the Magic Defense of the hero landing the Critical Hit.

The benefit is not limited to Wodryn: a teammate who lands a Critical Hit during the effect also adds damage, and Deepwood Might already raises the Critical Hit Chance of allies behind him. Different heroes add different amounts, and the Critical Hit still has to happen while the effect is active.

Core mechanic: Team Critical Hits add physical damage, scaled by each hero's Magic Defense
RELIC LEVEL 10 · SKILL UPGRADE

Bark Renewal — Thorned Bark

TESTED

While Oak Bark is active, Wodryn deals physical damage to enemies in front of him after every six hits he takes. If Oak Bark expires, the hit counter resets.

This gives the front line another reason to keep Wodryn active: he needs Oak Bark and needs to take hits. It adds damage during the fight, but the final damage panel won't show how much came from this Relic alone.

Upgrade: Every six hits taken with Oak Bark → damage to enemies in front
RELIC LEVEL 20 · SKILL UPGRADE

Unbowed Oak — More Bark

NOT TESTED YET

Whenever damage received by Wodryn himself is reduced through this Skill, he gains two layers of Oak Bark.

Those extra layers could strengthen the connection between his protection, his Energy gain and Deepwood Might. The reduced damage must be received by Wodryn himself.

Upgrade: Damage reduction on Wodryn → +2 Oak Bark layers
RELIC LEVEL 30 · NEW SKILL

Law of the Forest

NOT TESTED YET

While Wodryn is alive, enemy Pure Damage dealt to his team is converted into Magic Damage.

That is a change of damage type, not immunity: the team can still take damage, and keeping Wodryn alive remains essential. A potentially important upgrade against teams that rely on Pure Damage, but it needs its own practical testing.

Core mechanic: Enemy Pure Damage → Magic Damage while Wodryn lives
Relic level 10 · nine battles

Three attack teams tested

Wodryn, Byrna, Crow and Guus form the shared core. Guus is particularly interesting because Golden Feather can increase allied Critical Hit Chance — supporting heroes who land Critical Hits and the protection from Wodryn's Unbowed Oak. Mushy & Shroom, Luther and Tristan each change the lineup around that core.

TEAM 1 · BATTLES 1–3

Wodryn · Mushy & Shroom · Byrna · Crow · Guus

Crown of Wrath adds pressure while the rest of the team keeps attacking. Wodryn and Guus both support Critical Hit Chance, while Byrna and Guus bring healing. Critical Hit Chance helps Crow land Critical Hits and also feeds Unbowed Oak's damage reduction for eligible allies.

TEAM 2 · BATTLES 4–6

Wodryn · Luther · Byrna · Crow · Guus

Luther replaces Mushy & Shroom. We see Crown of Wrath early and Under the Wing later in the fight. Guus raises allied Critical Hit Chance, while the level ten Relic adds another source of physical damage when Wodryn takes enough hits with Oak Bark active.

TEAM 3 · BATTLES 7–9

Wodryn · Tristan · Byrna · Crow · Guus

Tristan adds his own physical damage with Purifier's Wrath, and Wodryn leads the damage on the final panel in a fast finish. Tristan can gain Energy when an eligible ally in front of him gains Energy, and Wodryn gains extra Energy when hit with Oak Bark active — an interaction worth further testing.

First impressions

  • Level 1: an additional physical damage effect tied to Critical Hits — especially interesting with heroes who raise Critical Hit Chance or land Critical Hits themselves, such as Guus and Crow.
  • Level 10: another way to deal damage while he has Oak Bark and takes hits. Both effects fit naturally with his kit, though the replays cannot show exactly how much damage each Relic added.
  • Levels 20 and 30: more accessible Oak Bark, and Pure Damage converted to Magic Damage while Wodryn is alive. Promising on paper, not tested here.

How to use these teams

Wodryn, Byrna, Crow and Guus gave a consistent core across three different teams. These are first impressions, not a claim that one lineup is best against every defense.

Test the combinations against the teams you face, and pay attention to how often Wodryn stays active at the front.

Unlocking the Relic: The first Relic level needs Wodryn at 4 stars and level 50. Level 10 needs 6 stars and a higher hero rank, and level 20 needs a higher rank again.
Skins & build priority

Recommended Skin Priority

Our recommended priority is to develop Wodryn as a reliable frontline Tank before investing in additional offensive potential.

DEFAULT SKIN

Strength — First Priority

Strength is Wodryn's main attribute. It improves Health and Physical Attack. Because several abilities scale with Physical Attack, this is a particularly valuable investment.

CRIMSON GROVE SKIN

Health — Second Priority

Wodryn needs to survive long enough to cleanse allies, activate defensive mechanics, generate Oak Bark and provide Critical Hit Chance support.

GOLDEN CROWN SKIN

Armor Penetration — Third Priority

His Ultimate deals Physical Damage, so Armor Penetration can help overcome enemy Armor. However, frontline durability takes priority over turning him into a damage dealer.

Glyph development & artifact progression

Glyphs, Artifacts & Gift of the Elements

Recommended Glyph Priority

Our recommended Glyph order focuses on durability and the attributes that directly support Wodryn's abilities.

  1. Strength

    Supports his main attribute, survivability and overall development.

  2. Health

    Essential for keeping Wodryn active as a frontline Tank.

  3. Physical Attack

    Contributes to Wodryn's damage, healing and the Critical Hit Chance bonuses provided by Deepwood Might.

  4. Critical Hit Chance

    Supports offensive potential and interacts with the defensive probability of Unbowed Oak.

  5. Magic Defense

    Adds protection against magical threats, although our current priority favors attributes more directly connected to his core kit.

Artifacts — Best Upgrade Order

  1. Ring of Strength

    Our first priority. Strength provides a solid development foundation and improves Physical Attack, supporting several Wodryn abilities.

  2. Grimoire of Thorns — Artifact Book

    Increases Critical Hit Chance and Magic Defense. Critical Hit Chance interacts with Unbowed Oak's damage reduction probability, while Magic Defense improves survivability.

  3. Rootbreaker — Artifact Weapon

    Provides Magic Defense to the entire team for nine seconds when activated. Valuable against magical threats, although our general build recommendation places it third.

Build flexibility: Artifact priorities may change depending on the enemy composition and the role Wodryn needs to perform.
HERO DEVELOPMENT

Gift of the Elements — Recommended: Maximize When Committed

Our recommendation is to maximize Wodryn's Sparks of Power as your resources allow.

He benefits from overall stat development, especially because he needs sufficient durability to remain active and continue providing protection, cleansing and support.

Talisman analysis

Talisman of Two Forms

Strength + Toughness

Wodryn's Talisman of Two Forms provides Strength as its main attribute and Toughness as its secondary stat.

Strength supports his overall development, while Toughness improves resistance to incoming damage when his Health falls below 50%.

This is a useful defensive combination for a Tank exposed to constant frontline pressure.

Team synergies

Five Powerful Hero Synergies

Wodryn opens several team-building possibilities. These five partnerships highlight different ways to benefit from his Critical Hit Chance, defensive support and Energy mechanics.

Guus — Critical Hit Support

Guus is a natural partner for Wodryn's Critical Hit mechanics. Through Golden Feathers, Guus can increase Critical Hit Chance, strengthen Critical Hit Damage and help allies recover Health.

Together, they can create stronger offensive pressure while improving the conditions for Unbowed Oak's damage reduction.

Positioning matters: Guus provides Golden Feathers to allies in front of him, while Wodryn supports allies positioned behind him.

Leonel — Energy Disruption

Leonel's Mark of Fate allows his Critical Hits to apply marks that drain enemy Energy. These marks can stack.

By increasing Leonel's Critical Hit Chance, Wodryn can create more opportunities to apply these marks and disrupt enemy Energy generation.

Leonel also contributes through Royal Roar, increasing Critical Hit Chance for himself and the ally with the highest Physical Attack.

Adding Guus introduces additional Critical Damage support.

Crow — Silence & Magic Protection

Crow brings Silence against Intelligence-based enemies through Silent Judgment. Meanwhile, Wodryn provides teamwide Magic Defense through Rootbreaker and cleansing through Bark Renewal.

This creates a combination of offensive disruption and defensive support against certain magical compositions.

Limitation: This combination is not guaranteed to counter every magic team. Continuously reapplied negative effects can return immediately after Wodryn cleanses them.

Tristan — Energy & Physical Attack

Tristan's Blessed Vanguard allows him to benefit from Energy gained by the ally directly in front of him, subject to the skill's conditions.

Wodryn gains additional Energy when taking damage while Oak Bark is active. Positioning Wodryn directly in front of Tristan therefore creates a potential Energy interaction.

Tristan's Righteous Zeal also increases Physical Attack for himself and allies positioned in front of him. This can strengthen Wodryn's damage, healing and Critical Hit Chance support.

Yasmine — Critical Assassination

Yasmine specializes in Critical Hits and aggressive attacks against vulnerable enemies. Her Dance of Death targets the enemy with the lowest Armor, while Assassin's Instinct increases her Critical Hit Chance and Dodge.

Wodryn's Deepwood Might can improve her offensive consistency. Meanwhile, Unbowed Oak can benefit from Yasmine's own Critical Hit Chance.

Adding Guus creates another potential layer of Critical Hit Chance, Critical Damage and healing support.

Counters & limitations

Wodryn — Counters & Limitations

Wodryn has promising defensive mechanics, but his effectiveness depends on the specific opponents and how their abilities interact with his own.

Repeated Debuffs

Bark Renewal can remove negative effects, but enemies that continuously reapply them may affect the team again.

Control Effects

His cleansing abilities should be evaluated against different forms of Control through actual battle testing.

Positioning Dependencies

Several of Wodryn's most interesting synergies depend on whether allies are positioned in front of or behind him.

Unconfirmed META Status

His long-term competitive value will depend on how consistently his mechanics perform against real opponents.

Testing note: This guide does not claim that Wodryn automatically counters Iris, Phobos, or other heroes who apply negative effects. His cleansing performance requires matchup-specific testing.
Gods of Alliance • Final verdict

Wodryn — A Tank Built to Protect

Wodryn is a Nature Tank with an unusual combination of protection, offensive support and Energy generation.

Our current recommendation is to prioritize Strength and Health, followed by the Physical Attack and Critical Hit Chance attributes that support his core mechanics.

Guus strengthens Critical Hit support and survivability. Leonel adds Energy disruption. Crow contributes Silence. Tristan creates Energy and Physical Attack interactions. Yasmine offers aggressive assassination potential.

However, Wodryn is still a developing hero. Future Relics and additional battle testing may change how we evaluate his builds and competitive value.

Build with intention. Master the mechanics. Control the fight.
