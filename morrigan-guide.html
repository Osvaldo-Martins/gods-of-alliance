01 / HERO OVERVIEW

The Undead Princess. An Army of Protection.

Morrigan is a middle-line Healer from the Way of Eternity. Her abilities combine skeleton summons, healing, defensive buffs, damage sharing and resurrection control.

Unlike a conventional healer, Morrigan's value depends heavily on the interaction between her summoned skeletons and the heroes fighting alongside them. Her army creates additional frontline targets while helping important Eternity allies withstand incoming damage.

Magic Attack is central to her skill scaling, while Health and defensive statistics help her survive long enough to maintain that support.

This Gods of Alliance guide covers her four skills, upgrade priorities, artifacts, talismans, team compositions and practical counters.

02 / COMPLETE SKILL BREAKDOWN

Four Skills. One Defensive System.

Morrigan's abilities are interconnected. Her skeleton army supports the healing, protection and damage-sharing mechanics that make her particularly valuable in Eternity teams.

01

Army of Graven

Summons six frontline skeletons. Morrigan can control a maximum of ten skeletons at once.

The summons are treated as Eternity Heroes. They inherit Morrigan's Armor and Magic Defense when summoned, while Magic Attack determines the skill's scaling.

The skeletons provide additional frontline targets, helping redirect enemy pressure away from allies.

02

Bone Armor

Summons two skeletons and grants Armor, Magic Defense and healing for eight seconds.

Eternity allies receive the full effect. Heroes from other factions receive half the effect.

This creates a clear advantage for Eternity-based compositions, although Morrigan can still contribute to mixed teams.

03

Empathy of Eternity

Summons two skeletons and applies Empathy to allied Eternity Heroes for ten seconds.

During this period, part of the damage those allies receive is distributed evenly among Morrigan's skeletons.

Keeping the skeleton army alive is therefore essential to maintaining her defensive value.

04

Necromancy

Whenever an enemy dies, Morrigan summons a skeleton from their body. She also prevents resurrection attempts on the battlefield.

An enemy resurrection attempt grants her a Soul Essence charge. When an ally dies, Morrigan can spend a charge to resurrect them.

Resurrection is conditional: Morrigan needs Soul Essence and does not automatically revive every fallen ally.

Important mechanic: Morrigan's resurrection control is especially valuable against enemies that attempt to revive. Without enemy resurrection attempts, that part of her kit has fewer opportunities to contribute.
03 / UNDERSTANDING THE MECHANICS

Why the Skeleton Army Matters.

Summon. Sustain. Protect.

Army of Graven establishes Morrigan's frontline skeleton presence. Bone Armor adds defensive bonuses and healing, while Empathy of Eternity turns her summons into a damage-sharing system for allied Eternity Heroes.

The skeletons are not simply extra attackers. They help distribute incoming pressure that would otherwise remain on the team's important heroes.

The summons can also receive certain shields from allies such as Julius, Judge and Isaac. Those interactions may help the army remain active for longer.

MAGIC

Magic Attack Scaling

Magic Attack supports the effectiveness of Morrigan's abilities. Improving this statistic strengthens her contribution beyond ordinary damage.

SURVIVAL

Defensive Investment

Health and defensive statistics help Morrigan remain active. A support hero who falls too early cannot maintain her army, healing or protection.

Faction consideration: Morrigan naturally favors Way of Eternity teams because Bone Armor gives those allies its full effect, and Empathy specifically protects allied Eternity Heroes.

08 / TEAM BUILDING & SYNERGIES

Best Allies for Morrigan.

Morrigan works best when her summons, defensive support and faction interactions contribute to the team's overall win condition. Protection alone is not enough: the composition must still defeat its opponent.

CORVUS

The Eternity Core

Corvus and Morrigan form a natural Way of Eternity foundation.

Corvus holds the frontline, applies pressure through Altar of Souls and supports compatible allies through Unity of the Damned.

Morrigan contributes healing, defenses, skeleton summons and damage sharing.

IRIS

Offensive Pressure

Iris adds damage to a composition supported by the Corvus–Morrigan core.

The additional protection can give the team more time to apply offensive pressure.

DRAYNE

Execution Potential

Drayne is another offensive candidate. Keeping him active creates more opportunities to bring vulnerable enemies into Final Verdict's execution range.

Corvus contributes additional pressure against enemy defenses.

SHIELDS

Julius, Judge & Isaac

Mixed compositions can benefit from shield interactions involving Morrigan's skeletons.

Longer-lasting summons can continue contributing to her defensive system. However, Bone Armor is less effective on allies outside Way of Eternity.

Build Around a Win Condition

Corvus and Morrigan provide a strong Eternity-oriented defensive foundation, but the remaining heroes must supply sufficient damage and appropriate answers to the opposing team.

Faction synergy matters, but it should never replace an evaluation of damage output, survival and enemy mechanics.

09 / HOW TO COUNTER MORRIGAN

Turn Her Strengths Against Her.

Morrigan relies on defensive buffs, healing and summoned units. The most interesting counters target those mechanics rather than simply attempting to overpower her skeleton army.

01

Iris — Punishing Buffs

Morrigan's Bone Armor applies defensive buffs. Those buffs can interact with Iris's Inner Fire mechanic.

Iris can exploit the defensive support Morrigan is trying to provide, creating pressure through an interaction within her own kit.

02

Celeste — Healing Interference

Celeste can interfere with Morrigan's healing.

When healing is blocked and converted into magical damage, the recovery Morrigan intends to provide can become a liability.

03

Jet — Healing & Armor Pressure

Jet can interfere with healing while also reducing Armor from a high-Armor target.

These effects can weaken the defensive structure Morrigan is trying to maintain.

04

Jhu — Sustained Activity

Jhu's increased attack speed and ability to avoid death while enraged may help him withstand certain situations involving Morrigan's skeletons.

Remaining active under pressure can give his team additional time to respond.

Counter warning: None of these heroes guarantees victory. Morrigan's effectiveness depends on her entire composition, including her skeletons, healing, defensive buffs and allied support. Identify which part of that system your team needs to disrupt.

11 / GODS OF ALLIANCE — FINAL STRATEGY

Master the Army. Control the Fight.

Morrigan's strength comes from how her abilities work together. Her skeleton army creates frontline targets and helps Eternity allies share incoming damage. Bone Armor adds healing and defensive buffs, while Necromancy provides powerful resurrection control in the right matchup.

Develop Magic Attack to strengthen her support abilities, but maintain enough Health and defenses to keep her active throughout the battle.

Corvus is a natural partner, while heroes such as Iris and Drayne can help convert that protection into offensive results. Shield interactions with Julius, Judge and Isaac create additional possibilities, with faction trade-offs to consider.

Use these upgrade priorities as a starting point. Your battles should determine whether the next investment needs to improve her skill scaling, survivability or the overall team's damage.

We don't build randomly. We build with intention. Master the mechanics. Control the fight.
