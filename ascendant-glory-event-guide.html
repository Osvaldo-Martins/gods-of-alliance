EVENT OVERVIEW

Three Connected Subevents.

Ascendant Glory runs for seven days and connects three separate quest boards. Your progress in Rising Legend and Trial of Legends also unlocks additional rewards through Spark of Glory. Understanding these connections is essential when planning your Ascension Crest budget.

More Than Just Crow's Skin.

Skin Plus doubles the Skin's bonus. The Champion's Gallery also offers Cascade and Satori Relic Shards, Soul Stones for Eva, Fafnir and Qing Mao, artifact fragments, Hero Skin Stones, speedups, World Energy, resource chests and purple and orange equipment.

The shop remains available for 24 hours after the event ends. The main event also includes Glory Pass, Reward Cascade, bundles and Emerald offers. Our calculations focus on quest rewards, excluding those purchase options.

SUBEVENT 01 • EIGHT TABS

Rising Legend.

Rising Legend contains eight quest tabs. Login and Hydra milestones accumulate throughout the event. The other six tracks reset daily, meaning full weekly completion requires progress every day.

TAB 01

Day After Day

Log in daily throughout the seven-day event. Additional rewards include Skin Stone Chests, Crow Soul Stones, and Artifact Essence, Scroll and Metal Chests.

TAB 02

Generous Soul

Requires daily VIP Points and is counted separately from our doable baseline. Rewards include normal and Large Skin Stone Chests, Titan Skin Stones, Pallant Soul Stones, Season Points, Meta Cubes, Elemental Spheres and Adventure Coins.

TAB 03

Chaos Cores

Spend Chaos Cores on useful Artifact upgrades. This contribution remains separate from the baseline. Other rewards include Season Points, Skin Stone Chests, Adventure Coins, Artifact Chest Keys, Whisper Ring Recipe fragments and Orm Soul Stones.

TAB 04

Summoning Titans

Full completion requires 50 Titan summons every day. This is a separate saved-resource contribution. Additional rewards include Orm Soul Stones, Season Points, Titan Skin Stones, Mastery Crystals, Artifact Chest Keys, Adventure Coins and other resources.

TAB 05

Fairy Dust

Reaching every milestone requires 10,000 Fairy Dust daily. This is another separate resource contribution. Rewards include Orm and Pallant Soul Stones, Titan Skin Stones, Adventure Energy, Titan Potions, Adventure Coins, Skin Stone Chests and Whisper Ring Recipe fragments.

TAB 06

Taming Hydra

Three participations daily reach 21 across the event. This is included in our doable baseline. Other rewards include Titan Potions, Rune Spheres, Large Skin Stone Chests, Season Points, Artifact Chest Keys, Adventure Energy and Titan Skin Stones.

TAB 07

Arena

Arena counts battles, not victories. Five free attempts daily form our baseline. Full completion requires 15 battles daily.

The first extra pack of five battles costs 50 Emeralds and the next costs 100. That is 150 Emeralds daily, or 1,050 Emeralds over seven days. This spending also progresses Wise Exchange in Trial of Legends.

TAB 08

Hold a Raid

Requires boss victories as the Raid leader. Our doable scenario includes 10 daily victories, using World Energy instead of Emeralds. Additional rewards include Food, Wood, Stone and Metal.

SUBEVENT 02 • EIGHT TABS

Trial of Legends.

Trial of Legends also contains eight quest tabs, but its milestones accumulate across the seven-day event. Emerald spending, VIP Points and saved resources must be planned separately.

TAB 01

Day After Day

Daily login rewards, including Titan Skin Stones alongside Ascension Crests.

TAB 02

Generous Soul

Rewards cumulative VIP Points and stays separate from the baseline. Additional rewards include Crow and Eva Soul Stones, Pallant and Orm Soul Stones, Season Points, Adventure Coins and Adventure Energy.

TAB 03

Wise Exchange

Rewards Emeralds spent during the event. Arena purchases, Outland Chests and other eligible Emerald spending all contribute to the same counter.

Count the Emerald expenditure only once, even when it unlocks rewards across connected subevents.

TAB 04

Guilds' Activity

Depends on your activity and resources. Glyph upgrades and equipment exchanges in the Forge can help reach higher milestones. Maximum completion is not accessible to everyone, so this tab stays separate.

TAB 05

Rune Stones

The final spending milestone requires 750,000 Rune Stones. This contribution is counted separately. Rewards include Soul Stones, recipe fragments, Season Points, Meta Cubes, Elemental Spheres, Mastery Crystals and a Skin Shop Certificate.

TAB 06

Accelerations

Full completion requires 41 hours of speedups. Keep this separate because players have different reserves. Rewards include Titan Skin Stones, Crow Soul Stones, Rune Spheres, Season Points, Adventure Energy and Artifact Chest Keys.

TAB 07

Titan Potion

Requires spending Titan Potions. Upgrading Titans or buying Golden Horns of Summoning in the Titan Soul Shop can advance the milestones. Your available potions determine your contribution.

TAB 08

Heroic Discovery

Rewards opening Heroic Chests. Two free openings daily provide 14 openings across the event, completing milestones up to 10. Higher milestones reach 100 openings and remain separate.

SUBEVENT 03 • FIVE TABS

Spark of Glory.

Spark of Glory connects progress from the first two subevents while adding Outland and Training objectives. Its connector milestones are essential for understanding the complete acquisition strategy.

TAB 01

Day After Day

Daily login rewards, including Skin Stone Chests alongside Ascension Crests.

TAB 02

Outland

Opening Outland Chests awards Ascension Crests. Our baseline assumes no free openings. A complete sequence of 150 chests awards 6,850 Crests, and the sequence can repeat.

From October 16–18, the announced sale offers a 55% Outland Chest discount and ×4 Emerald offers.

Our scenario uses discounted packs of 100 chests, costing 3,375 Emeralds per pack. This spending also advances Wise Exchange.

TAB 03

Training

Requires training troops of level six or higher. A complete sequence needs 50,000 troops and awards 7,100 Crests. The sequence is repeatable.

Additional rewards include Food, Wood, Stone, Metal, General Speedups, Construction Speedups and Rune Spheres.

Important limitation: Players already exceeding their troop limit may be unable to progress this tab by training additional troops.

TAB 04

Rising Legend Connector

Rewards quests completed in Rising Legend. Our baseline completes 84 quests, unlocking 4,100 extra Crests. Higher milestones reach 97,600 Crests in total.

Additional rewards include Crow, Pallant, Orm and Fafnir Soul Stones, Adventure Energy, Holy Grenade fragments for Crow's Artifact weapon, Skin Shop Certificates and a Red Recipe Box.

TAB 05

Trial of Legends Connector

Rewards quests completed in Trial of Legends. Our baseline completes 10 quests, unlocking 1,250 extra Crests. The highest connector milestone reaches 95,750 Crests.

Two special rewards deserve attention: Qing Mao's Lunar Skin Plus at 45 quests, and Soleil's Talisman of Scholar at 75 quests. The talisman provides Magic Reflection and Health.

Important: Nine Packs Are Not Enough.

With nine discounted Outland packs, the same Arena routine and no other Emerald spending, the example reaches only 384,850 Crests, below the 390,000 target.

VIP rewards, additional eligible Emerald spending, Chaos Cores, Rune Stones beyond 200,000 and Training can reduce the number of Outland packs needed. These contributions depend on your own resources and progress.

FINAL STRATEGY

Plan Before You Spend.

Ascendant Glory rewards planning across all three subevents. The normal Crow Skin is a smaller target, while Skin Plus requires substantial saved resources and additional progress.

Knowledge. Precision. Control.

Complete daily tasks, monitor the next connector milestone and consider the Outland discount only if it fits your plan. Keep the doable baseline separate from VIP, Emerald spending and saved resources.

Don't build randomly. Build with intention.
