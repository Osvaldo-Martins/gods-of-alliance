01 / NEW RELIC OVERVIEW

Luther's Relics. A New Dimension of Control.

Luther is a Tank from the Way of Honor, known for entering the opposing formation and disrupting enemies from inside their ranks.

His new Relics expand that identity with accumulated Physical Damage, recurring area control, conditional Armor Penetration and nearby Magic Attack reduction.

In our initial Gods of Alliance showcase, Luther reaches Relic Level 10. This unlocks the first two Relics, allowing us to examine their interaction with his existing abilities.

The Level 20 and Level 30 Relics remain locked in these tests. Their announced mechanics are explained here, but their practical battle performance requires future testing.

03 / UNDERSTANDING LUTHER

His Original Skills Explain the Relics.

Before evaluating the new upgrades, we need to understand Luther's existing role: entering the enemy formation, applying nearby control and strengthening his resistance to magic.

01

Sentence

Deals damage and stuns nearby enemies. Its effectiveness depends on Luther's position and which targets are within range.

02

Omen

Moves Luther deep into the opposing team, dealing damage to nearby enemies. This movement is central to his identity.

03

Gehenna

After Luther lands following Omen, a fiery wave deals Physical Damage and stuns nearby enemies for two seconds.

04

Doctrine of Faith

Increases Luther's Magic Defense. Each time he takes Magic Damage, he gains a further Magic Defense increase for four seconds.

Core mechanic: Luther needs to survive inside the enemy formation long enough to use his nearby control and new Relic effects. His positioning determines which enemies can be affected.
04 / FIRST RELIC — LEVEL 1

Righteous Retribution. Accumulated Retaliation.

Righteousness

Every 12 seconds, Luther begins accumulating a charge during an 8-second window.

The initial charge equals 20% of his Physical Attack. During the accumulation window, incoming damage increases the charge by 10% of his current Magic Defense.

When the timer expires, or if Luther dies while holding a charge, he deals Physical Damage equal to the accumulated amount to enemies around him.

Understanding the Charge

The Relic uses two different attributes: Physical Attack establishes the starting charge, while current Magic Defense determines the additional amount triggered by incoming damage.

Initial charge = 0.20 × Physical Attack
Increase per damage trigger = 0.10 × Current Magic Defense

The description does not state that Luther stores a percentage of the actual damage received. Therefore, a larger incoming hit should not automatically be interpreted as creating a proportionally larger charge.

SYNERGY

Doctrine of Faith

Doctrine of Faith increases Magic Defense, including a temporary bonus when Luther receives Magic Damage.

Because Righteousness accumulation depends on current Magic Defense, this creates an important interaction between his original skill and new Relic.

POSITION

Omen

Omen places Luther inside the opposing formation. If enemies are nearby when the accumulated charge releases, several targets may be within range.

The release is local: it is not described as a battlefield-wide attack.

Damage distinction: Righteous Retribution deals Physical Damage. The accumulated amount describes the attack before enemy defenses and other applicable effects determine the final damage received.
05 / SECOND RELIC — LEVEL 10

Gehenna. Recurring Area Control.

Gehenna Activates Every 11 Seconds

The Level 10 Relic upgrades Gehenna, causing the skill to trigger every 11 seconds.

The original Gehenna already deals Physical Damage and attempts to stun nearby enemies for two seconds after Luther lands.

This upgrade gives Luther a recurring activation to examine throughout the battle, extending his potential control beyond the initial movement.

What the upgrade does not confirm: The description does not announce increased damage or a longer stun. Its stated improvement is the periodic trigger. Enemy protections may also affect whether the attempted control succeeds.
06 / THIRD RELIC — LEVEL 20

Doctrine of Faith. Armor Penetration.

Magic Damage Triggers Offensive Power

At Level 20, Doctrine of Faith gains an additional effect. When Luther receives Magic Damage, he also increases his Armor Penetration for four seconds.

The bonus equals 10% of his Physical Attack + 400.

Armor Penetration Formula

Bonus Armor Penetration = (0.10 × Physical Attack) + 400

The example shown in the source build displays 5,442 Armor Penetration. That is a build-dependent example, not a fixed value for every Luther.

DAMAGE

Physical Damage Synergy

Temporary Armor Penetration can help Luther's Physical Damage overcome enemy Armor. This is relevant to Gehenna and Righteous Retribution's release.

SEPARATE

Not Additional Righteousness

Armor Penetration does not directly increase the Righteousness charge. Magic Defense contributes to charge accumulation, while Armor Penetration helps Physical Damage deal with Armor.

Testing limitation: The description gives a four-second duration but does not establish whether repeated triggers stack the Armor Penetration bonus or refresh its duration. This Relic was locked during the showcased battles.
07 / FOURTH RELIC — LEVEL 30

Unshakable Faith. Magic Attack Suppression.

Weaken Nearby Enemies

While Luther is alive, enemies around him have their Magic Attack reduced by an amount equal to Luther's Physical Attack.

The example displayed in the source build shows a reduction of 50,422 Magic Attack. That value depends on Luther's build.

This is a flat Magic Attack reduction, not a percentage reduction to all incoming damage.

OMEN

Positioning Becomes More Valuable

Omen moves Luther deep into the enemy formation, potentially placing him near opponents whose abilities depend on Magic Attack.

This gives his positioning another strategic purpose beyond damage and control.

RANGE

Local and Survival-Dependent

The effect applies to nearby enemies, not the entire battlefield.

It also lasts only while Luther is alive. Its actual coverage and impact require Level 30 testing.

Not tested in this showcase: Level 30 remained locked. The practical effect of Unshakable Faith will need to be evaluated in future gameplay tests.

11 / GODS OF ALLIANCE — FINAL ANALYSIS

Luther's Relics Strengthen His Identity.

Luther already stood out because he could enter the opposing formation and bring control into enemy ranks. His new Relics build directly on that battlefield role.

Righteous Retribution adds accumulated Physical Damage, while the Level 10 Gehenna upgrade introduces recurring area control. These were the two upgrades available during our first battle tests.

At Level 20, Luther gains temporary Armor Penetration after receiving Magic Damage. At Level 30, he reduces the Magic Attack of nearby enemies while alive. Both remain important subjects for future gameplay testing.

Our initial results are encouraging: two attacking teams, six selected victories and all five attackers surviving in every showcased battle. They provide a useful starting point, not a guarantee against other compositions.

We will continue exploring Luther with additional teams and matchups to understand how these mechanics perform across different situations.
