Metida Full Guide

How to build the Elarite Super Titan: Physical Attack, survivability, artifacts, skins, upgrade priorities and the resources required to develop her.

Metida's build revolves around Physical Attack and survivability. Physical Attack strengthens her damage and several effects from Supreme Harmony, while Health helps keep her alive long enough to continue providing control, healing, damage and team support.

Why Physical Attack Matters

Physical Attack is central to Metida because it scales Crystal Vortex and multiple effects provided by Supreme Harmony.

Crystal Vortex

Traps the central enemy in a whirlwind for five seconds. While trapped, the target cannot move, attack or use skills. The whirlwind deals damage once per second, with every tick scaling at 100% of Metida's Physical Attack.

Current reference: 717,470 damage per tick

Fire — Healing

For every surviving Fire Titan, Supreme Harmony heals all allies once per second. Each healing tick equals 10% of Metida's Physical Attack.

Current reference: 71,747 healing per tick

Water — Physical Attack

For every surviving Water Titan, Metida increases all allies' Physical Attack by 20% of her own Physical Attack.

Current reference: +143,494 Physical Attack per Water Titan

Elarite — Additional Hit

For each surviving Elarite Titan, Metida applies an effect to one ally, prioritizing Titans with the highest Attack. Whenever an affected ally deals damage, Metida lands an additional hit equal to 20% of her Physical Attack.

Current reference: 143,494 additional damage

Earth — Speed

Earth behaves differently. Each surviving Earth Titan provides 10% increased speed. This percentage does not scale with Metida's Physical Attack.

Supreme Harmony's bonuses depend on surviving Titans. Increasing Metida's attributes strengthens the effects, but the team must remain alive to continue providing the elemental contributions.

Default Skin — Physical Attack

Maximum Bonus: +196,800 Physical Attack

At maximum level, the Default Skin adds 196,800 Physical Attack. Because several of Metida's effects scale directly from this attribute, the skin contributes to more than her personal damage.

Health — Primordial Skin & Skin+

Primordial Skin

Without the Primordial Skin, the reference build has 10,027,050 Health. At maximum level, the normal Primordial Skin adds 2,627,975 Health.

Total Health: 12,655,025

Primordial Skin+

Skin+ doubles the Primordial Skin's bonus to 5,255,950 Health. It doubles the skin bonus — not Metida's entire Health pool.

Calculated Total Health: 15,283,000
In our tests, the additional Health sometimes appeared to slightly delay Crystal Vortex's activation. However, the comparisons tested with the normal Primordial Skin were never worse. The increased survivability appeared to compensate for the occasional timing delay.

Build Priorities

PRIORITY 01

Titan Level

Take Metida from level 1 to 120. This establishes the foundation of her stats and is the first upgrade path we recommend completing.

PRIORITY 02

Stars

Fullstar is the ideal target. If all Soul Stones are not yet available, aim for at least five stars and continue toward Fullstar.

PRIORITY 03

Artifacts & Skins

Develop her artifacts with particular attention to the Seal, prioritize the Default Skin's Physical Attack, then build the Primordial Skin for survivability.

Artifact Priorities

Zorval's Amulet

Provides a bonus specifically against Light and Dark Titans. When its artifact bonus activates, the effect extends to the entire team for nine seconds.

Initial target: Level 100–105

Elarite Crown

Provides additional defense against Light and Dark Titans, reinforcing Metida's role when fighting teams built around those elements.

Initial target: Level 100–105

Chthonic Balance Seal

Increases both Physical Attack and Health. Physical Attack strengthens multiple parts of Metida's kit, making this artifact particularly important to her overall build.

Initial target: Level 105–110

Final Artifact Goal

For all three artifacts, obtain the highest stars available to your account and continue developing them beyond the practical starting targets.

Final goal: Level 120

Skin Priority

1. Default Skin Physical Attack
2. Primordial Skin Health
3. Primordial Skin+ Larger Health Bonus

Physical Attack comes first because it strengthens Crystal Vortex damage, Fire healing, the Water attack bonus and Elarite additional hits. The Primordial Skin then improves survivability, with Skin+ providing the larger Health bonus for players able to make the additional investment.

The Elarite Team Is Not Complete Yet

The Elarite Summoner is coming soon. A complete Elarite team could significantly change Metida's role and how her Supreme Harmony effects are used. Until that lineup can be tested, her connections with Water, Earth and Fire remain important areas for further gameplay testing.

Build Metida With Intention

Metida has a clear build direction. Physical Attack strengthens both her damage and several parts of her team support. Her artifacts reinforce her attributes and her role against Light and Dark Titans, while the Primordial Skin provides additional survivability.

Build her level first, continue toward Fullstar, develop her artifacts with particular attention to the Chthonic Balance Seal, maximize the Default Skin's Physical Attack, and then invest in the Primordial Skin according to the resources available to your account.
