Metida Full Guide

How to build the Elarite Control Super Titan: skills, Physical Attack, survivability, artifacts, skins, upgrade priorities and the resources required to develop her.

Metida is a Control Super Titan. Her value is not limited to dealing damage or reaching Crystal Vortex as quickly as possible. Her build must balance the Physical Attack that powers several parts of her kit with enough survivability to remain on the battlefield and continue controlling the fight through Supreme Harmony.

SKILL 1

Crystal Vortex

Five Seconds of Battlefield Control

Crystal Vortex traps the central enemy in a whirlwind for five seconds and moves that enemy around the battlefield.

While trapped, the target cannot move, attack or use skills. The whirlwind also deals damage once per second.

Each damage tick scales at 100% of Metida's Physical Attack. Increasing Physical Attack therefore increases Crystal Vortex's damage, but it does not extend the five-second control duration.

Current Reference Build 717,470 damage per tick
SKILL 2

Supreme Harmony

Supreme Harmony empowers Metida's allies for nine seconds. The bonuses depend on the number of surviving Titans of each element, meaning the composition around Metida directly changes what the skill contributes to the battle.

FIRE • HEALING

Keep the Team Alive

For every surviving Fire Titan, Metida heals all allies once per second. Each healing tick equals 10% of Metida's Physical Attack.

71,747 healing per tick per surviving Fire Titan
EARTH • SPEED

Accelerate the Team

Each surviving Earth Titan provides 10% increased speed. Unlike Fire, Water and Elarite, this percentage does not scale with Metida's Physical Attack.

+10% Speed per surviving Earth Titan
WATER • PHYSICAL ATTACK

Increase Offensive Pressure

For every surviving Water Titan, Metida increases all allies' Physical Attack by 20% of her own Physical Attack.

+143,494 Physical Attack per surviving Water Titan
ELARITE • ADDITIONAL HIT

Additional Damage

For each surviving Elarite Titan, Metida applies an effect to one ally, prioritizing Titans with the highest Attack. Whenever an affected ally deals damage, Metida lands an additional hit.

143,494 additional damage
Supreme Harmony depends on surviving Titans. Fire, Earth, Water and Elarite are therefore not simply four fixed bonuses — their contribution changes according to which Titans remain alive during the fight.

Why Physical Attack Matters

Physical Attack is one of Metida's most important development stats because it affects both her personal damage and multiple support effects inside Supreme Harmony.

One Stat — Multiple Effects

Crystal Vortex 100% of Physical Attack per damage tick

Water 20% of Physical Attack as team Physical Attack
Elarite 20% of Physical Attack as additional hit

Default Skin — Physical Attack

Maximum Bonus: +196,800 Physical Attack

Health — Primordial Skin & Skin+

Health creates an important development question for Metida because additional survivability can affect the timing of Energy gained from incoming damage. The key is to judge that timing together with what Metida provides while she remains alive.

Primordial Skin

Without the Primordial Skin, the reference build has 10,027,050 Health. At maximum level, the normal Primordial Skin adds 2,627,975 Health.

Total Health: 12,655,025

Primordial Skin+

Skin+ doubles the Primordial Skin's bonus to 5,255,950 Health. It doubles the skin bonus — not Metida's entire Health pool.

Calculated Total Health: 15,283,000

Health vs Crystal Vortex Timing

In our tests, additional Health sometimes appeared to slightly delay Crystal Vortex's activation. That difference is worth understanding, but Crystal Vortex is not Metida's entire purpose.

Metida also needs to remain alive to continue influencing the battle through Supreme Harmony. While she survives, Fire can continue contributing healing, Earth speed, Water Physical Attack and Elarite additional hits.

Across the different development stages we tested, we did not find a reason to sacrifice Metida's survivability simply to pursue a slightly earlier Crystal Vortex.

The objective is balance: do not overbuild Metida unnecessarily, but do not underbuild her purely because more Health can sometimes alter Crystal Vortex timing.

Build the Control Titan — Not Just the Ultimate

Metida should not be developed as though her only purpose is to activate Crystal Vortex as quickly as possible.

Crystal Vortex is excellent Control, but Metida also influences the rhythm of her own team through Supreme Harmony. Her value comes from surviving, controlling the enemy and continuing to strengthen the different elements around her.

The goal is therefore not simply to chase the largest possible stat total or the earliest possible ultimate. It is to give Metida enough development to perform her complete role effectively.

Build Priorities

PRIORITY 01

Titan Level

Take Metida from level 1 to 120. This establishes the foundation of her stats and is the first upgrade path we recommend completing.

PRIORITY 02

Stars

Fullstar is the ideal target. If all Soul Stones are not yet available, aim for at least five stars and continue toward Fullstar.

PRIORITY 03

Artifacts & Skins

Develop her artifacts with particular attention to the Seal, prioritize the Default Skin's Physical Attack, then build the Primordial Skin for survivability.

Artifact Priorities

Zorval's Amulet

Provides a bonus specifically against Light and Dark Titans. When its artifact bonus activates, the effect extends to the entire team for nine seconds.

Initial target: Level 100–105

Elarite Crown

Provides additional defense against Light and Dark Titans, reinforcing Metida's role when fighting teams built around those elements.

Initial target: Level 100–105

Chthonic Balance Seal

Increases both Physical Attack and Health. Physical Attack strengthens multiple parts of Metida's kit, while Health helps her remain active long enough to continue providing Control and Supreme Harmony support.

Initial target: Level 105–110

Final Artifact Goal

For all three artifacts, obtain the highest stars available to your account and continue developing them beyond the practical starting targets as your resources allow.

Final goal: Level 120

Skin Priority

1. Default Skin Physical Attack
2. Primordial Skin Health
3. Primordial Skin+ Larger Health Bonus

Physical Attack comes first because it strengthens Crystal Vortex damage, Fire healing, the Water attack bonus and Elarite additional hits. The Primordial Skin then improves survivability, with Skin+ providing the larger Health bonus for players able to make the additional investment.

The Elarite Team Is Not Complete Yet

The Elarite Summoner is coming soon. A complete Elarite team could significantly change Metida's role and how her Supreme Harmony effects are used.

Until that lineup can be tested, her connections with Water, Earth and Fire remain important areas for further gameplay testing, while the complete Elarite interaction remains something we will need to evaluate when the faction is complete.

Build Metida With Intention

Metida's build has a clear direction. Physical Attack strengthens Crystal Vortex and multiple parts of Supreme Harmony. Health gives her the survivability required to stay in the fight and continue influencing the team around her.

Build her level first, continue toward Fullstar, develop her artifacts with particular attention to the Chthonic Balance Seal, maximize the Default Skin's Physical Attack, and then invest in the Primordial Skin according to the resources available to your account.

Most importantly, do not judge Metida only by how quickly she reaches Crystal Vortex. She is a Control Super Titan whose complete value comes from controlling the enemy while continuing to support Fire, accelerate Earth, strengthen Water and interact with Elarite allies.
