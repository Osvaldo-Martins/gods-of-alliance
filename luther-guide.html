HERO OVERVIEW

More Than a Frontline Tank

Luther is a frontline Tank from the Way of Honor. His greatest strength is his ability to disrupt enemy positioning and create opportunities for his allies.

Instead of remaining in his original frontline position, Luther jumps deep into enemy territory, attracting enemy attention and potentially redirecting attacks and abilities away from their intended targets.

His stuns, positioning disruption and Magic Defense allow him to interfere with opposing strategies. However, his effectiveness depends heavily on survivability, timing and team composition.

SKILL BREAKDOWN

Luther's Four Skills Explained

SKILL 01 • SENTENCE

Sentence

Luther deals Physical Damage to nearby enemies and stuns them. The damage scales with Physical Attack, and the skill's effectiveness depends on its level. The chance to stun decreases against targets above Level 120.

This ability cannot be copied.

Why it matters

A successful stun interrupts enemy actions and can delay important abilities, allowing Luther's teammates to take advantage of the opening.

Artemis is a particularly interesting partner because her Smart Arrow can react to stunned enemies, converting Luther's control into offensive pressure.

SKILL 02 • OMEN

Omen

Luther jumps into the enemy formation and deals Physical Damage to nearby opponents. Although the damage scales with Physical Attack, the main value of Omen is positioning disruption.

Why it matters

When Luther lands behind the enemy frontline, heroes in the second and third positions may turn around to attack him. This can leave the opposing frontline more exposed to heroes such as Qing Mao, who can apply Armor reduction.

If Krista occupies the second position, or Jorgen occupies the second or third, the jump can redirect important abilities away from their intended targets.

If Dorian occupies the third position, the resulting separation can move the two backmost heroes outside his Vampirism aura, limiting their access to its support.

SKILL 03 • GEHENNA

Gehenna

After landing from Omen, Luther releases a wave of fire that deals Physical Damage and stuns enemies in front of him.

Damage scales with Physical Attack, while stun effectiveness depends on the skill's level. The chance to stun decreases against enemies above Level 120.

Why it matters

Omen and Gehenna create a sequence of positioning disruption followed by control. This can interrupt enemy actions, create opportunities for Artemis' Smart Arrow and potentially interfere with backline heroes such as Polaris.

Timing remains essential: Luther can create a powerful opening, but a poorly timed jump may leave him isolated.

SKILL 04 • DOCTRINE OF FAITH

Doctrine of Faith

This passive ability increases Luther's Magic Defense. Whenever he receives Magic Damage, his Magic Defense increases further for four seconds.

The additional effect scales with Physical Attack. Repeated Magic Damage, including damage-over-time effects, can refresh the defensive effect.

Why it matters

Doctrine of Faith helps Luther withstand repeated magical pressure, especially after entering the opposing formation.

However, Magic Defense alone is not enough. Luther still needs Health, Armor and overall survivability to remain effective.

1. ARMOR → 2. HEALTH → 3. STRENGTH → 4. MAGIC DEFENSE → 5. PHYSICAL ATTACK

Armor & Health

The two highest priorities. Armor protects against physical damage, while Health increases Luther's general ability to withstand attacks.

Strength

Supports Health and Physical Attack, reinforcing durability and skills that scale with Physical Attack.

Magic Defense

Helps Luther survive magical pressure, complementing Doctrine of Faith.

Physical Attack

Useful for skill scaling, but placed last because Luther's primary job is control and survival.

INITIAL DEVELOPMENT TARGET

Bring all five glyphs to Level 40 as an initial goal, unless progressing further for skill-class requirements.

TALISMAN ANALYSIS

Luther's Two Talisman Options

First Talisman — Strength & Health

Attributes: Strength and Health.

Availability: Eternal Frontier Shop.

Cost: 700,000 Frontier Coins.

This is a strong defensive option for Luther, reinforcing his Health pool and primary attribute.

Particularly attractive when the main goal is to help him survive extended pressure after entering the enemy formation.

Second Talisman — Physical Attack & Toughness

Attributes: Physical Attack and Toughness.

Availability: Special events.

Physical Attack improves scaling for several of Luther's skills, while Toughness adds another defensive component.

This option may appeal to players looking for a combination of ability scaling and resilience.

WHICH SHOULD YOU CHOOSE?

Prioritize the first Talisman for a Health-and-Strength defensive foundation. Consider the second when Physical Attack scaling and Toughness better match your intended build.

TEAM SYNERGIES

Heroes Who Benefit from Luther

Artemis — Stun Synergy

Sentence and Gehenna can stun enemies, creating opportunities for Artemis' Smart Arrow mechanic to contribute additional offensive pressure.

Qing Mao — Exposed Frontline

Luther's jump can draw enemy attention away from the opposing frontline, allowing Qing Mao to exploit the exposed frontmost target and apply Armor reduction.

TEAM-BUILDING PRINCIPLE

Luther needs allies capable of taking advantage of the disruption he creates. He also requires enough durability to survive while separated from his team's original frontline.

POSITIONING & COUNTERPLAY

How to Counter Luther

Luther's greatest advantage is his ability to remain behind the enemy frontline, redirecting attention and interfering with targeting.

A powerful counterstrategy is to push, throw or otherwise force him back toward his own team.

Faceless — Power Throw

Faceless can throw Luther toward the center of his team and stun him, disrupting his positioning-based strategy.

Cascade — Tidal Wave

When Cascade is positioned fourth or fifth, Tidal Wave can push Luther back toward his original formation.

Somna — Transformation

From the fourth or fifth position, Somna can transform Luther into a sheep, causing him to walk back toward his team.

Fox — Heavy Caliber

Fox can use displacement to push Luther back toward his own side of the battlefield.

Maya — Poisonous Bonds

Maya's Poisonous Bonds can entangle enemies and help return Luther toward his original formation.

Counterplay Principle

The objective is not necessarily to eliminate Luther immediately. Removing his positional advantage can greatly reduce his ability to distract and disrupt your heroes.

GODS OF ALLIANCE • FINAL VERDICT

Luther — A Tank Built to Disrupt

Luther is not a conventional Tank. His greatest strength is the ability to interfere with enemy positioning, redirect attacks and create control opportunities.

Sentence and Gehenna provide stuns. Omen disrupts enemy formations. Doctrine of Faith strengthens his resistance against magical pressure.

His recommended build emphasizes Armor, Health, Toughness and appropriate magical protection before prioritizing extra damage.

Against the right opponents, Luther can create significant problems. Against teams capable of immediately pushing him back, his greatest advantage may disappear.

The key to using Luther effectively is not simply making him durable. It is building a team capable of exploiting the disruption he creates.
