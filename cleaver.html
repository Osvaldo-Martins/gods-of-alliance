CLEAVER

TANK • WAY OF CHAOS • FRONT LINE

Survive the frontline, destroy enemy positioning and turn Rusty Hook into an opportunity for your entire team to eliminate priority targets.

Cleaver is a Chaos Tank positioned on the front line. His defining strength is his ability to disrupt enemy positioning with Rusty Hook while surviving long enough to control the battlefield.

RELICS — CURRENT CONTEXT

Current information discussed in this guide suggests Cleaver may receive Relics in the next season. This guide does not treat that release as confirmed. His future value may change if and when those Relics are officially released.

CLEAVER'S KIT

SKILLS

Rusty Hook

POSITIONING CONTROL

Cleaver throws his hook at the furthest enemy, dealing Physical Damage before dragging that hero directly to the front line. The damage scales with his Physical Attack.

A protected backline hero can suddenly find themselves standing directly in front of Cleaver's team, creating an opportunity for physical pressure and burst damage to eliminate the target before the enemy can recover.

Putrefaction

PURE DAMAGE

Cleaver continuously deals Pure Damage to nearby enemies while also damaging himself. Unlike several of his other abilities, Putrefaction scales with his Health.

Its rapid sequence of Pure Damage hits can pressure shields, frontline barriers and heroes such as Lucas.

Mutilation

CONTROL

Cleaver attacks enemies in front of him, dealing damage while stunning everyone caught in the attack. The skill scales with Physical Attack and Skill Level.

After Cleaver pulls a priority target forward, Mutilation can help keep that hero under control while the team focuses them down.

Heavyweight

PASSIVE

Heavyweight permanently increases Cleaver's Strength, contributing more Health, survivability and additional scaling across his kit.

CHAOS INTERACTIONS

SYNERGIES & MECHANICS

Aidan

Putrefaction damages Cleaver himself. That allied Health sacrifice can create additional value for Aidan inside Chaos compositions.

Xe'Sha

Cleaver's self-sacrifice through Putrefaction can also benefit Xe'Sha, turning Cleaver's self-damage into additional offensive value for a Chaos team.

DISRUPTING CLEAVER

COUNTERS & KEY INTERACTIONS

HOOK INTERACTION

Dorian

If Cleaver pulls Dorian forward, Dorian's Vampirism Aura can begin supporting frontline allies instead, potentially making them considerably harder to eliminate.

DISPLACEMENT PROTECTION

Andvari

Living Earth prevents displacement effects, stopping Cleaver from pulling protected heroes with Rusty Hook.

REFLECTED PRESSURE

Peppy + Aurora

Peppy's shield together with Aurora's Rainbow Halo can reflect large amounts of Magic Damage back into Cleaver, creating a dangerous interaction for him.

CHARM

Lian

Lian can interrupt Rusty Hook by charming Cleaver when he targets her, breaking the hook interaction.

TRANSFORMATION

Somna

Somna can transform Cleaver into a sheep when he attempts to hook her, disrupting one of his defining mechanics.

BLIND

Electra

Electra can blind Cleaver, causing several of his damaging attacks to miss.

SURVIVABILITY

Martha

Martha can survive surprisingly well after being pulled, potentially buying enough time for her team to recover from Cleaver's positioning disruption.

ENERGY CONTROL

Jorgen

Torment of Powerlessness can prevent Cleaver from gaining Energy, delaying Rusty Hook activations and reducing his battlefield impact.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

CONTROL THE BATTLEFIELD

Cleaver's defining value comes from combining durability with positioning disruption.

Rusty Hook can remove the protection normally provided by the enemy formation. Putrefaction applies repeated Pure Damage while creating Chaos interactions through Cleaver's self-damage. Mutilation adds frontline control, while Heavyweight reinforces his durability through Strength.

Build his defenses first. Give him the Armor and Health necessary to survive, then allow his disruptive mechanics to create opportunities for the rest of the team.

If Cleaver does receive Relics in a future season, his development and team interactions will need to be reevaluated once their actual mechanics are known.
