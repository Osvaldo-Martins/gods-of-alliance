HERO OVERVIEW

Cleaver — More Than a Frontline Tank

Cleaver is a frontline Tank from the Way of Chaos. His defining abilities combine exceptional durability, enemy displacement, crowd control and damage at close range.

Following his rework, Cleaver has gained a much stronger offensive identity. Pure Damage plays a larger role in his abilities, while the Relic system introduces healing reduction, additional area pressure, cleansing interactions and damage amplification.

His ability to pull protected enemies into the frontline remains one of his most recognizable mechanics. With the right team, that disruption can change the direction of an entire battle.

UPDATED HERO KIT

Cleaver — All Four Skills Explained

The reworked Cleaver keeps his signature hook while increasing his ability to pressure enemies directly. His four skills provide a combination of disruption, persistent area damage, control and Strength-based durability.

Rusty Hook

Cleaver throws his hook toward the farthest enemy, pulling that hero into the frontline and dealing Pure Damage.

This remains one of Cleaver's most important tactical abilities because it allows him to disrupt the opposing formation and expose heroes who would otherwise remain protected at the back.

Following the rework, Cleaver's basic attacks also deal Pure Damage after the relevant ability interaction, giving him additional offensive pressure.

Core mechanic: Backline displacement + Pure Damage

Strategic advantage: A successful hook can pull a vulnerable support or damage dealer away from the safety of the enemy backline, creating opportunities for Cleaver and his teammates to eliminate that target.

Putrefaction

Putrefaction deals repeated Pure Damage to Cleaver himself and enemies standing nearby over several seconds.

The Legendary upgrade increases its offensive importance, turning Cleaver into a source of continuous close-range area pressure.

Its self-damage is especially important in Chaos compositions. Heroes who benefit from allied Health sacrifice can gain value from Cleaver repeatedly damaging himself.

Core mechanic: Repeated area Pure Damage + Self-sacrifice

Strategic advantage: Putrefaction can provide offensive value even while Cleaver is primarily protecting the frontline. It also creates important synergies with Aidan and Xe'Sha.

Mutilation

Cleaver strikes enemies directly in front of him, dealing Pure Damage and stunning nearby targets.

Its control component is particularly useful when an enemy has just been pulled into the frontline by Rusty Hook.

The ability's level-dependent stun interaction can be less reliable against enemies above the relevant skill threshold, but its combination of damage and disruption remains valuable.

Core mechanic: Frontline Pure Damage + Stun

Strategic advantage: Cleaver can disrupt an enemy's formation and then prevent nearby targets from immediately responding through additional control.

Heavyweight

Heavyweight passively increases Cleaver's Strength.

Strength improves his Health and Physical Attack, reinforcing his ability to remain active on the frontline while increasing the offensive attributes supporting his abilities.

Unlike activated skills, the passive bonus helps Cleaver from the beginning of the battle.

Core mechanic: Permanent Strength increase

Strategic advantage: A stronger base Health pool helps Cleaver survive incoming pressure and creates more opportunities to use his offensive and control abilities.

Understanding Cleaver: His rework combines Pure Damage and control with his traditional frontline role. Rusty Hook opens the formation, Putrefaction pressures nearby enemies, Mutilation disrupts them and Heavyweight supports his durability.
CLEAVER RELIC SYSTEM

Relics — Four Important Milestones

Cleaver's Relics strengthen his ability to pressure grouped enemies, reduce healing, remove negative effects and deal more damage when surrounded.

RELIC LEVEL 1

Miasma

Miasma applies an effect to the central enemy for 7 seconds.

While that effect remains active, it deals damage to enemies surrounding the affected target every 0.5 seconds.

This introduces another source of area pressure and allows Cleaver to affect enemies beyond the immediate frontline.

7-second duration · Damage every 0.5 seconds

Important interaction: When Putrefaction is upgraded through the relevant Relic, enemies affected by Miasma can also be affected by healing reduction.

RELIC LEVEL 10

Putrefaction — Healing Reduction

This upgrade applies 60% healing reduction to enemies affected by Putrefaction or Miasma.

If an opponent is affected by both abilities simultaneously, the healing-reduction effect can be doubled according to the Relic description.

This gives Cleaver another form of utility by making it harder for healing-dependent opponents to recover during the battle.

Healing reduction: 60% per qualifying effect

Strategic advantage: Healing suppression can become especially valuable against teams relying heavily on regeneration, sustained healing and long battles.

LEGENDARY RELIC · LEVEL 20

Heavyweight — Cleansing Interaction

Heavyweight gains an additional cleansing interaction through its Relic upgrade.

According to the original guide's description, whenever Cleaver or one of his allies takes damage from an enemy hero, negative effects are removed from the enemy involved in that interaction.

The effect remains active while Cleaver is alive.

Relic milestone: Level 20

Testing context: This Legendary Relic had not yet been unlocked during the battle testing documented in the original guide. Its complete practical impact was therefore not established by those tests.

LEGENDARY RELIC · LEVEL 30

Carnage — Damage Amplification

Carnage increases Cleaver's damage for each enemy standing near him.

This rewards Cleaver for fighting in close-range situations, particularly when several opponents have been drawn toward the frontline.

Its value increases with the number of nearby enemies, making positioning an important factor in its performance.

Relic milestone: Level 30

Testing context: Carnage had not been unlocked during the original testing, so its full performance was not included in the demonstrated battle results.

Important testing limitation: The original guide's tests did not include the unlocked Legendary Heavyweight and Carnage Relics. Their descriptions are preserved here, but those tests cannot establish their final battle performance.
DEFENSIVE DEVELOPMENT

Cleaver — Best Skin Priority

Cleaver's primary responsibility is surviving on the frontline. Armor, Health and Strength are therefore central to his development. The original guide recommends prioritizing defensive attributes before Physical Attack.

1

Masquerade Skin

Armor. Our first priority for reducing incoming Physical Damage. Especially valuable when facing hard-hitting physical frontline attackers.

2

Inner Demons & Barbarian Skins

Health. Both skins strengthen Cleaver's Health pool. Additional Health improves his frontline survival and contributes to his self-sacrifice-based abilities.

Prioritize these Health investments according to availability and your team's requirements.

3

Default Skin

Strength. Increases Cleaver's primary attribute, supporting both Health and Physical Attack.

4

Lunar Skin

Toughness. Provides another defensive layer, especially useful when Cleaver's Health drops to dangerous levels.

5

Beach Skin

Magic Defense. Helps Cleaver resist magical opponents and maintain his frontline position against Mage-heavy teams.

6

Champion Skin

Physical Attack. Improves his offensive attributes, but we prioritize defensive investment before this skin.

GOA development priority: Armor → Health → Strength → Toughness → Magic Defense → Physical Attack. Match these priorities to the original guide's recommended defensive progression rather than treating skin release order as an upgrade rule.

ARTIFACT DEVELOPMENT

Artifacts — Best Investment Priority

The original Cleaver guide prioritizes permanent defensive stats first, followed by the teamwide Armor bonus from his Weapon and then Strength from his Ring.

1. Artifact Book

Armor & Magic Defense.

Our first priority. The Book improves Cleaver's protection against both physical and magical damage, strengthening his ability to withstand attacks from different types of opponents.

2. Artifact Weapon

Teamwide Armor.

When Cleaver activates Rusty Hook, his Artifact Weapon provides an Armor bonus to his allies.

This adds defensive utility to his displacement mechanic, helping the whole team withstand Physical Damage.

3. Artifact Ring

Strength.

Increases Cleaver's primary attribute, strengthening his Health and contributing to his overall development.

Recommended order: Book → Weapon → Ring.

ADVANCED HERO DEVELOPMENT

Cleaver — Talisman Comparison

Cleaver has two talisman options that support different aspects of his build. One focuses on his traditional frontline durability, while the other provides a combination of offensive and defensive attributes.

First Talisman

Main attribute: Strength.

Additional attribute: Health.

Strength and Health reinforce Cleaver's core identity as a durable frontline Tank.

This combination helps him withstand enemy pressure and continue using his hook, control and area-damage abilities.

Availability: Eternal Frontier

Second Talisman

Main attribute: Crushing.

Additional attribute: Toughness.

This option combines additional damage potential against weakened enemies with improved survivability during dangerous low-Health situations.

Availability: VIP Progression
GOA recommendation: Both Talismans can provide value. The first supports Cleaver's primary tank attributes, while the second creates a more specialized combination of Crushing and Toughness.
CHAOS INTERACTIONS

Cleaver — Hero Synergies

One of the most important parts of Cleaver's rework is the interaction between his self-damage and heroes who benefit from allied Health sacrifice.

Aidan — Chaos Sustain

Putrefaction damages Cleaver himself, creating allied Health sacrifice.

This interaction can provide additional value to Aidan in Chaos compositions, while Aidan's protective capabilities help the team remain active through longer fights.

Key advantage: A valuable combination of self-sacrifice mechanics and team protection.

Xe'Sha — Offensive Sacrifice Synergy

Cleaver's self-damage through Putrefaction can support Xe'Sha's offensive mechanics.

This transforms a mechanic that would normally be considered a disadvantage into a source of potential offensive value for the entire composition.

Key advantage: Turning allied Health sacrifice into additional offensive pressure.

Chaos strategy: Cleaver is not limited to traditional tanking. His self-sacrifice mechanic can create opportunities for partners who benefit from Health loss while still allowing him to disrupt the opposing formation.

COUNTER STRATEGY

How to Counter Cleaver

Cleaver's hook, damage and frontline presence can be dangerous, but several heroes offer mechanics that interfere with his abilities or take advantage of the situations he creates.

HOOK INTERACTION

Dorian

If Cleaver pulls Dorian forward, Dorian's Vampirism Aura may begin supporting frontline allies instead.

This can create an unfavorable interaction for Cleaver by making opposing frontline heroes more difficult to eliminate.

DISPLACEMENT PROTECTION

Andvari

Living Earth prevents protected allies from being displaced, interfering with Cleaver's attempt to pull those heroes through Rusty Hook.

This can preserve the opponent's positioning and prevent Cleaver from exposing vulnerable backline targets.

MAGICAL RETALIATION

Peppy + Aurora

Peppy's shield combined with Aurora's Rainbow Halo can create significant magical retaliation against Cleaver.

This interaction can punish his continued frontline pressure and repeated attacks.

CHARM

Lian

Lian can interrupt Cleaver's hook interaction by Charming him when he targets her.

This can disrupt the timing and effectiveness of Rusty Hook.

TRANSFORMATION

Somna

Somna can transform Cleaver into a sheep when he attempts to hook her.

That transformation can interfere with one of his signature mechanics and prevent the hook from working as intended.

BLIND

Electra von Grave

Electra can apply Blind to Cleaver, causing affected attacks to miss.

This can reduce the offensive contribution of his attacks, depending on the timing and interaction of the abilities involved.

SURVIVABILITY

Martha

Martha can be surprisingly difficult to eliminate even after being pulled forward.

If she survives the positioning disruption, her team may gain enough time to recover and respond to Cleaver's pressure.

ENERGY DENIAL

Jorgen

Torment of Powerlessness can prevent Cleaver from gaining Energy.

Delaying Rusty Hook can reduce his ability to disrupt enemy positioning at important moments of the fight.

Important: No counter guarantees victory. Cleaver's Relic investment, team composition, positioning and ability timing can significantly influence these interactions.
GODS OF ALLIANCE ANALYSIS

Final Verdict — The Reworked Cleaver

Cleaver's rework gives him considerably more depth than a traditional frontline Tank.

He now combines Pure Damage, crowd control, positioning disruption, area pressure and healing reduction while maintaining the durability required to anchor his team.

  • Rusty Hook: Disrupts enemy positioning and exposes protected targets.
  • Putrefaction: Provides repeated area damage and important Chaos sacrifice interactions.
  • Mutilation: Adds close-range control and Pure Damage.
  • Heavyweight: Reinforces Cleaver's primary attribute and durability.
  • Relics: Expand his value through Miasma, healing suppression, cleansing interactions and damage amplification.

The five compositions documented in the original guide demonstrate Cleaver's ability to function in different environments, including aggressive, defensive and control-oriented teams.

The final two Legendary Relics were not unlocked during those original tests, so their full combat performance remains outside the scope of that testing.
