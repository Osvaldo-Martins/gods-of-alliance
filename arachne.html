HERO OVERVIEW

Arachne — Master of Battlefield Control

Arachne is a middle-line Control hero from the Way of Progress, specializing in stuns, disruption, targeted attacks and Pure Damage pressure.

Her job is not simply to trade damage with opponents. She disrupts their ability to act, targets vulnerable enemies and creates opportunities for her teammates to take control of the fight.

Demolition Robot and Underground Strike provide powerful area stuns, while Teleport allows Arachne to reach weakened targets. Neurotoxin adds continuous Pure Damage whenever she damages an enemy.

5s Demolition Robot stun
5s Underground Strike stun
3s Neurotoxin duration
PURE Poison damage type
Arachne's identity: Control the enemy's time. Her value comes from interrupting groups, reaching vulnerable targets and keeping opponents under pressure. Magic Attack strengthens her abilities, while survivability allows her to remain active long enough to influence the battle.
ABILITIES & MECHANICS

Arachne — All Four Skills Explained

Arachne combines repeated control, targeted attacks and Pure Damage. Understanding positioning is especially important because her stuns can affect multiple enemies when their formation is compressed.

Demolition Robot

Arachne summons a machine that charges toward the first enemy it encounters.

When the machine reaches its target, it explodes, damaging enemies in the area and stunning them for 5 seconds.

Control Duration: 5 seconds

One of the most important characteristics of this ability is its area effect. When several enemies are positioned close together, the explosion can interrupt multiple heroes simultaneously.

The ability scales with Magic Attack, making offensive development important even for a hero whose main purpose is battlefield control.

Strategic advantage: Heroes who move or compress the enemy formation can increase the number of opponents affected by Arachne's stun.

Teleport

Arachne jumps toward the enemy with the lowest Health, attacks that opponent with her claws and then returns to her original position.

This gives Arachne the ability to reach a vulnerable target without necessarily having to defeat the enemy frontline first.

The damage scales with Magic Attack, supporting the offensive direction of her development.

Strategic advantage: Teleport can help Arachne pressure already weakened heroes, potentially contributing to their elimination before they recover.

Positioning matters: Arachne temporarily moves toward her target. Enemy abilities that punish nearby opponents can create dangerous interactions during her jump.

Underground Strike

Arachne attacks the nearest enemy, dealing damage and stunning that hero and nearby opponents for 5 seconds.

Control Duration: 5 seconds

Like Demolition Robot, this skill becomes particularly valuable when several enemies are positioned close together.

The ability can disrupt multiple heroes at once, creating opportunities for teammates to activate their own skills while the opposing formation is temporarily unable to respond.

Strategic advantage: Combined with Demolition Robot, Underground Strike gives Arachne repeated opportunities to interrupt the enemy team throughout a battle.

Neurotoxin

Whenever Arachne damages an enemy, that opponent becomes poisoned for 3 seconds.

The poison adds Pure Damage to Arachne's offensive pressure, allowing her to contribute damage even while her primary role remains battlefield control.

Poison Duration: 3 seconds

Pure Damage does not need to overcome Armor or Magic Defense, and Dodge does not avoid it.

Strategic advantage: Neurotoxin complements her repeated attacks and area abilities, helping her maintain pressure on enemies while her stuns interrupt their actions.

Key mechanic: Arachne does not need to be your main damage dealer to contribute significantly. Repeated stuns, vulnerable-target pressure and poison can create the conditions for the rest of your team to win.

TEAM BUILDING

Arachne — Best Hero Synergies

Arachne performs particularly well when teammates help her affect multiple enemies with her control abilities or increase the offensive opportunities created by her stuns.

Dante — Enemy Displacement

Dante can contribute to changes in enemy positioning, creating situations where Arachne's area abilities may affect several opponents.

When enemies are positioned close together, Demolition Robot and Underground Strike become particularly valuable.

Key advantage: Greater opportunities for multi-target disruption.

Qing Mao — Positioning Pressure

Qing Mao can contribute to enemy displacement, creating opportunities for Arachne to affect grouped opponents with her stuns.

Positioning matters because Arachne's control skills are more valuable when several enemies are caught in their area of effect.

Key advantage: Displacement support for area-control abilities.

Judge — Additional Control

Judge adds further control opportunities, supporting Arachne's role in interrupting the opposing team's actions.

Combining control-oriented heroes can create situations where enemies have fewer opportunities to activate important abilities.

Key advantage: Additional disruption and battlefield control.

Polaris — Control Synergy

Polaris provides another control-focused partnership that can complement Arachne's repeated stuns.

Both heroes can contribute to disrupting enemy actions, creating opportunities for their teammates to apply damage while opponents are under pressure.

Key advantage: A lineup centered on controlling the timing of enemy abilities.

Team-building principle: These are examples of synergy rather than one mandatory five-hero lineup. Balance control with protection, damage and the needs of the particular matchup.
COUNTER STRATEGY

How to Counter Arachne

Arachne can disrupt entire formations, but several heroes can challenge her control mechanics, interrupt her skills or punish repeated attacks.

CONTROL PUNISHMENT

Soleil

Soleil can punish enemy control interactions, making him an interesting option against a hero whose main role revolves around repeated stuns.

Counter advantage: Creates pressure against control-heavy compositions.

SILENCE

Thea

Thea can apply Silence, potentially interrupting Arachne's ability to activate important skills at the required time.

Preventing Arachne from casting can reduce the frequency and impact of her control abilities.

Counter advantage: Skill interruption through Silence.

SILENCE

Crow

Crow provides another Silence-based option against Arachne.

Interrupting a Control hero's ability rotation can create important opportunities for the rest of the team.

Counter advantage: Disruption of Arachne's skill timing.

SILENCE

Mushy and Shroom

Mushy and Shroom offer another way to apply Silence and interfere with Arachne's abilities.

Reducing Arachne's ability to use her control skills consistently can help limit her influence during the fight.

Counter advantage: Additional Silence-based disruption.

ALTAR RETALIATION

Corvus

Corvus can punish repeated damage through his Altar of Souls.

Because Arachne can damage enemies through repeated abilities and Neurotoxin, retaliation interactions may create additional pressure against her.

Counter advantage: Punishes repeated damage instances and can threaten Arachne's survivability.

Important: No counter guarantees victory. Positioning, ability timing, team composition and investment still determine the outcome.
GODS OF ALLIANCE ANALYSIS

Final Verdict — Is Arachne Worth Building?

Arachne offers a distinctive combination of control and offensive pressure.

Her primary value is her ability to repeatedly disrupt opponents rather than relying exclusively on raw damage.

  • Demolition Robot: Area damage and a five-second stun.
  • Teleport: Targets the lowest-Health enemy.
  • Underground Strike: Another opportunity for multi-target control.
  • Neurotoxin: Adds Pure Damage pressure through poison.

For development, prioritize Magic Attack and Intelligence before investing more heavily in additional defensive attributes.

When choosing teammates, focus on heroes who complement her control, improve enemy positioning or take advantage of the opportunities she creates.
