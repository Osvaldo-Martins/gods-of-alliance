HERO GUIDE • WAY OF PROGRESS

ARACHNE

CONTROL • MIDDLE LINE

Arachne plays a different game. Her job is not simply to trade damage with the enemy. She disrupts the battle through repeated stuns, targets vulnerable Heroes, applies poison and creates opportunities that the rest of her team can exploit.

ARACHNE'S IDENTITY: control the enemy's time. Her kit can interrupt groups, reach low-Health targets and keep pressure on opponents through Neurotoxin. To do that consistently, she needs both the offensive stats that strengthen her Skills and enough survivability to remain active in the fight.

SKILLS

ARACHNE'S CONTROL KIT

Arachne combines repeated stuns, direct access to vulnerable enemies and Pure Damage pressure.

SKILL 1

Demolition Robot

Arachne summons a machine that charges toward the first enemy it encounters. The machine explodes and stuns enemies in the area for five seconds.

Scales with Magic Attack. Grouping or compressing the enemy formation can allow the stun to affect more Heroes.
SKILL 2

Teleport

Arachne jumps directly toward the enemy with the lowest Health, attacks that target with her claws and then jumps back.

Scales with Magic Attack and gives Arachne a way to reach a vulnerable Hero without necessarily working through the frontline first.
SKILL 3

Underground Strike

Arachne attacks the nearest enemy, dealing damage and stunning that Hero and nearby enemies for five seconds.

Positioning matters. When enemies are close together, one activation can disrupt several Heroes at once.
SKILL 4

Neurotoxin

Any enemy damaged by Arachne becomes poisoned for three seconds. This adds Pure Damage to her pressure.

Pure Damage does not need to pass through Armor or Magic Defense, and Dodge does not avoid it.
DEVELOPMENT

SKINS & GLYPHS

Magic Attack sits at the center of Arachne's development because it repeatedly contributes to the effectiveness of her Skills.

SKIN PRIORITY

  1. Magic Attack
  2. Second Magic Attack
  3. Default Intelligence
  4. Magic Penetration + Super Skin
  5. Health
  6. Armor

The first investments strengthen the stats driving Arachne's Skills. Health then helps her remain active long enough to keep controlling the fight, while Armor comes last.

GLYPH PRIORITY

  1. Magic Attack
  2. Intelligence
  3. Magic Penetration
  4. Health
  5. Armor

Take all five Glyphs to Level 40. Beyond that point, further investment should depend on your development objectives, including whether you are pursuing Skill Class progression.

ARTIFACTS

ARTIFACT PRIORITY

1ST PRIORITY

Book

Provides Magic Attack and Health, strengthening Arachne's Skills while also improving survivability.

2ND PRIORITY

Ring

Provides Intelligence, supporting her Skills and adding some additional Magic Defense.

3RD PRIORITY

Weapon

Provides a teamwide Armor bonus when Arachne activates her first Skill.

GIFT OF THE ELEMENTS

MAX IT

GIFT OF THE ELEMENTS — MAX

Arachne needs solid fundamental stats. Her offensive development keeps her Skills meaningful, while additional survivability gives her more time to continue applying control. Being a Control Hero does not remove the need for strong base development.
