Complete Season Overview

Welcome to Winter's Eye

The Winter's Eye Season begins on November 23, introducing Valdur, the final Elarite Titan, alongside an extensive calendar of events, character upgrades and valuable rewards.

Across four major event groups, players will encounter opportunities to develop their Titans, unlock new hero skins, obtain Talismans and invest in Legendary Relics.

With so many overlapping activities and discounts, resource management will be especially important. This guide brings together the announced calendar, major rewards and strategic considerations for the entire season.

Resource Management

Best Strategy for Winter's Eye

Four consecutive event groups create many opportunities, but also competing demands for the same resources. Free-to-play players and moderate spenders should plan across the entire season rather than treating every event independently.

01 — Define your main objective Decide whether Titan development, Talismans, skins or Legendary Relics are most important to your current account.
02 — Combine quests with discounts Review the announced discount windows before spending Emeralds on chests and spheres. Where possible, complete event requirements during the corresponding discount.
03 — Reserve resources for later weeks Skin Stones, Emeralds, spheres and other resources appear across multiple event groups. Avoid exhausting your reserves on rewards that do not benefit your main teams.
04 — Evaluate Realm objectives carefully Recruitment acceleration and troop losses can carry additional costs. Consider the value of each milestone before committing troops or acceleration resources.
05 — Plan Legendary Relic investments Arachne and Alvanor Relic opportunities appear across multiple weeks, while Tempus Relic Fragments are announced for Bountiful Roads. Compare their relevance to your existing hero investments.
06 — Control Seer's Game spending The return of bad-luck cards means players should account for uncertain outcomes. Set a budget and preserve resources for guaranteed development opportunities.

GOA Season Verdict

A Winter Full of Opportunities

Winter's Eye delivers an extensive calendar of Titan development, Talismans, new skins, Legendary Relics and returning seasonal activities.

Its greatest challenge may not be finding valuable rewards, but deciding which opportunities deserve your resources.

According to information shared during the recent livestream, another festive season is planned afterward, bringing players closer to Christmas and the New Year.

More events mean more decisions. Plan carefully, develop the characters that matter to your account and make every investment count.
