RESOURCE STRATEGY

What Should You Prioritize?

The biggest mistake is looking only at the final totals. Most of Roads Unlocked is built around daily consistency.

  1. Log in every day. Day After Day provides 3,500 Event Coins across the event.
  2. Plan for 2,500 Fairy Dust every day if you want every Hidden Reserves Event Coin milestone.
  3. Start seven Expeditions every day. That is enough for all 10,850 Event Coins from Beyond Horizon.
  4. Use your free Tower Chest openings. The free milestones already contain every Event Coin available in Tower Trophy.
  5. Treat Price of Power separately. Its 7,000 Event Coins require 31,500 Skin Stones across seven days.
  6. Treat Generous Soul separately. Its 7,000 Event Coins require reaching 1,200 VIP Points every day.
  7. Do not include Reward Box outcomes in the guaranteed quest calculation. Reward Box results are random.
  8. Before spending in Caravan Hall, identify which Talismans, Folio Relic Shards or account resources actually support your current priorities.
