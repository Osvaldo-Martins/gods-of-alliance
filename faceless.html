HERO GUIDE

FACELESS

CONTROL • WAY OF ETERNITY • BACK LINE

Faceless is one of the Dominion's most unusual Control Heroes. He can copy the last first Skill used on the battlefield, disrupt enemy positioning, weaken Physical Attack and increase his entire team's Magic Defense.

HERO IDENTITY

What Is Faceless?

Faceless isn't defined by one job. His value comes from versatility and timing. Depending on the battle, he can control, displace, debuff, protect the team from magical pressure or copy a decisive Skill.

SKILLS

Faceless' Skill Kit.

Faceless' greatest strength is not necessarily his own Ultimate. Sometimes, the strongest weapon on the battlefield belongs to someone else.

SKILL 01 • SIGNATURE MECHANIC

Doppelganger

Faceless transforms into the ally or enemy who most recently activated their first Skill and then uses that Skill himself.

The copied Skill is increased to Level 240.

Faceless is not restricted to reproducing one kind of effect. What he provides depends entirely on the Skill he copies.

He can reproduce defensive effects such as Julius' shields and, with the correct copied Skill, can even reproduce resurrection mechanics from Heroes such as Kayla or Aidan.

TIMING MATTERS. The order of first Skills matters. Waiting for the correct activation can be more valuable than using Doppelganger immediately.
SKILL 02

Power Throw

Faceless lifts the nearest enemy into the air and drops that Hero into the center of the opposing team.

The impact deals damage and stuns enemies in the affected area. Its power scales with Faceless' Magic Attack, while the Stun becomes less likely to succeed if the target's level is above 120.

The key mechanic is positioning. Power Throw removes the nearest Hero from their normal position and throws them deeper into their own formation, potentially exposing Heroes that were protected behind the frontline.

IMPORTANT SYNERGY: K'arkh benefits enormously from enemies being knocked into the air, making Power Throw a direct offensive setup for him.
SKILL 03

Chain Lightning

Faceless launches a magical projectile that ricochets between three enemies.

It deals damage while reducing the targets' Physical Attack by 1,800 for four seconds.

The Skill scales with Faceless' Magic Attack and allows him to weaken three targets simultaneously, giving the team another defensive tool against physical pressure.

The negative effect also creates an interesting interaction with Iris, giving her another opportunity to take advantage of enemies affected by debuffs.
SKILL 04 • PASSIVE

Spell Expert

Faceless increases the entire team's Magic Defense by 3,000.

Because this passive depends only on its level, Faceless provides this defensive value without needing another activation.

Against magic-heavy compositions, that additional Magic Defense can help the entire team survive.

ELECTRA INTERACTION: increasing Magic Defense can also strengthen Electra's shell, creating another reason to use Faceless in structures built around her.
DEVELOPMENT

Skins & Glyphs.

Faceless needs to survive long enough to control the battle and reach the right Doppelganger timing. That is why Health comes first.

Skin Priority

  1. Health
    First priority for survivability and control uptime.
  2. Magic Attack
    Strengthens Power Throw and Chain Lightning.
  3. Intelligence
    Supports his magical development while also adding some Magic Defense.
  4. Magic Penetration
    Helps his magical damage bypass enemy Magic Defense.
  5. Magic Defense
    Further improves survivability against magical pressure.
  6. Magical Critical Hit Chance
    Offensive value, but final priority because Faceless' primary responsibility remains control.
Faceless currently has six Skins covering Intelligence, Magic Attack, Health, Magic Defense, Magic Penetration with a Skin+ version, and Magical Critical Hit Chance.

Glyph Priority

  1. Health
  2. Magic Attack
  3. Intelligence
  4. Magic Penetration
  5. Physical Attack
Bring all five Glyphs to Level 40. Beyond Level 40, further investment should depend on your objectives, including Skill Class progression.
ARTIFACTS

Artifact Priority.

Faceless' development begins with the Artifact that directly supplies two of his most important offensive stats: Magic Attack and Magic Penetration.

PRIORITY 01

Artifact Book

Provides Magic Penetration and Magic Attack. Both directly support Power Throw and Chain Lightning.

PRIORITY 02

Artifact Ring

Provides Intelligence, further strengthening Faceless' overall development and complementing the Book.

PRIORITY 03

Artifact Weapon

Activating Doppelganger provides the entire team with a Magic Penetration bonus. Valuable for magic-oriented compositions, but third when prioritizing Faceless' individual development.

SYNERGIES

Heroes That Work With Faceless.

Faceless can perform several jobs, which allows him to create very different interactions depending on the Heroes around him.

K'arkh

One of Faceless' classic interactions. Power Throw knocks an enemy into the air, creating exactly the type of opportunity K'arkh can exploit.

Iris

Chain Lightning applies a negative effect by reducing Physical Attack, creating another opportunity for Iris to take advantage of debuffed enemies.

Electra

Spell Expert increases the team's Magic Defense. That additional Magic Defense can also strengthen Electra's shell.

COUNTERS

How Can Faceless Be Disrupted?

Faceless wants time and control over the flow of the battle. These Heroes provide different ways of interfering with that plan.

Soleil

Soleil's Halo can cause Faceless' Stun to turn against him, while repeated control effects can create additional opportunities for Soleil to punish him.

Iris

Chain Lightning applies a debuff, and that negative effect can make Faceless an interesting target for Iris.

Cornelius

Faceless is Intelligence-based. If his Intelligence becomes sufficiently high while his Armor remains vulnerable, Cornelius can become extremely dangerous against him.

Somna

Somna can create control problems for Faceless when her mechanics are triggered, disrupting a Hero whose own game plan depends heavily on control.

Lian

Lian provides another route for creating control problems for Faceless when her mechanics are triggered.

FINAL ANALYSIS

The Danger of Doppelganger.

Faceless doesn't have one single mechanic that defines everything he does. Sometimes he controls. Sometimes he weakens physical attackers. Sometimes he increases the team's resistance against magic.

He can set up K'arkh, support an Electra structure, interact with Iris, or simply copy one of the most important first Skills on the battlefield.

That is what makes Doppelganger dangerous: the strongest Faceless activation isn't always determined by Faceless. Sometimes, the enemy brings the weapon for him.
