Hero kit

Four skills, one job: control the clock

Every skill scales with Magic Attack, which is why it leads his glyph order.

SKILL 1 · ULTIMATE

Chronostasis

2.5s time stop−Armor / −Magic Defextends debuffs

Stops time for every enemy: no moving, no skills, no dodging, cooldowns frozen.

Why it matters
Dodge teams lose their dodge, opening the door for Xe'Sha and Cascade. Enemy abilities are delayed, which can stop key defensive reactions. The Armor and Magic Defense cuts count as separate debuffs, and Iris feeds on debuffs, so she benefits enormously.
SKILL 2

Withering

6s−Physical Attack−Max Health

Weakens distant enemies in front of him, cutting their damage and their Health pool.

Why it matters
Especially painful for physical damage dealers and heroes that rely on big Health pools; Kayla is one example. Its debuffs also fuel teammates who benefit from debuffed enemies.
SKILL 3

Temporal Anomaly

5s trap25% slow−50 Energy

Traps the enemy with the highest Armor Penetration, slowing, draining and burning them.

Why it matters
It goes straight for physical carries. He doesn't just damage them; slower and starved of Energy, they can't keep up their pressure. Against the right target, those five seconds can decide the fight.
SKILL 4

Time Paradox

4s Clockenemies slowedallies +10% speed

A giant Clock slows enemies and speeds up allies, who also gain maximum Health.

Why it matters
This is where Tempus manipulates both sides at once. The extra maximum Health also interacts with heroes like Aidan, Astaroth and Xe'Sha. Paired with Chronostasis, he sets the pace of the whole battle.

Investment

Build details: the reasoning

Keep Tempus alive first, then make every skill hit harder.

Skins

  1. Lunar: Health. He must survive to keep casting.
  2. Stellar: Magic Attack. Stronger skill effects.
  3. Cybernetic: Magic Attack. More skill scaling.
  4. Default: Intelligence. Magic Attack and Magic Defense.
  5. Angel (Skin+): Armor. Versus physical teams.
  6. Jade Flames: Magic Defense. Versus mages.

Glyphs

  1. Magic Attack. All four skills scale with it.
  2. Health. No control if he dies early.
  3. Intelligence. Magic Attack plus Magic Defense.
  4. Magic Defense. Survive mage burst.
  5. Armor. Versus dangerous physical teams.

Bring all five to level 40 first, unless you're chasing skill-class progression.

Artifacts & Elements

  1. Book: Magic Attack + Health. Power and survival together.
  2. Ring: Intelligence. His main attribute.
  3. Weapon: team Magic Attack. On Chronostasis; great in magic teams.

Gift of the Elements: max it as resources allow.

Team building

Partners & counters

The best Tempus team isn't the one with the most control. You still need damage and survivability to finish the fight.

Pair Tempus with

Iris ★ standout partner

Chronostasis feeds her Pure Soul charges; extended Eternity debuffs keep Inner Fire burning longer and hotter.

Corvus

Frontline protection plus more Eternity debuffs.

Phobos

Extra control and negative effects.

Xe'Sha & Cascade

They punish the moment enemies lose their dodge.

How to counter him

Iris

Punishes enemy debuff applications, and Tempus applies plenty.

Crow

Silence stops his active skills before they land.

Mushy & Shroom

Silence buys a window to hit him first.

Thea & Keira

More Silence options to disrupt his sequence.

Timing is everything: Silence him before Chronostasis lands; it won't undo what's already cast. And a fully-Relic'd Tempus fights back: Time Rebound freezes whoever controls him.
Gods of Alliance analysis

Verdict

THE MASTER OF TIME
"Tempus doesn't need to be your strongest damage dealer. He gives your damage dealers the time and opportunities they need to win."

Strengths

  • Team-wide 2.5s time stop that also extends every debuff
  • Shuts down physical carries (Withering, Temporal Anomaly)
  • Speeds up and protects his own team
  • Best-in-class partner for Iris and Eternity teams
  • Relic adds team Magic Penetration and punishes control

Watch out for

  • Low personal damage; needs real carries beside him
  • Silence before his ultimate cancels his game plan
  • Fragile if under-invested; prioritise Health early
