FINAL VERDICT

Make Every Resource Work Twice.

The strongest part of Bountiful Roads is that it rewards progress you are already making elsewhere in Trade Routes. Roads Unlocked and Grand Caravan therefore have two layers of value: their own rewards and the connector milestones here.

Make the daily progress count, collect the connector rewards and choose additional spending around upgrades your account actually needs.

Reward Boxes can provide additional Event Coins, but they cost Emeralds and their coin rewards depend on random outcomes. For that reason, Reward Box coins are excluded from every total above.

And because No Time to Wait can restart after a complete sequence, 71,850 is not an absolute event maximum. Further speedup repetitions can continue increasing the total.
