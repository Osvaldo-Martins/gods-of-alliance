PEPPY

CONTROL • WAY OF CHAOS • BACK LINE

Control the enemy team's rhythm, punish incoming attacks with Boldness and unlock some of the most explosive interactions available inside Chaos compositions.

Peppy is a Control Hero from the Way of Chaos, positioned on the back line. Her kit combines team-wide disruption, Magic Damage, crowd control and the unique ability to punish enemies for attacking a protected ally.

PEPPY'S KIT

SKILLS

Masquerade

TEAM-WIDE CONTROL

Peppy unleashes a wave of magic that can stun, blind or slow every enemy for six seconds. The power of the ability scales with her Magic Attack.

The randomly selected control effect makes Masquerade unpredictable. A stun can stop enemy initiation, Blind can reduce incoming physical pressure, while Slow can give Peppy's team additional time to generate Energy and act.

Boldness

SHIELD • MAGIC DAMAGE

Peppy surrounds an ally with a magical shield for four seconds. Every time the protected ally takes damage, nearby enemies receive Magic Damage in return.

Boldness scales with Magic Attack and becomes particularly dangerous when placed on heroes who repeatedly take damage, including self-inflicted damage.

Frolic

BURST • STUN

Peppy fires a magical beam at the nearest enemy, dealing heavy Magic Damage and stunning the target for three seconds. The skill scales with her Magic Attack.

The combination of burst damage and reliable crowd control can delay the target's Energy generation and interrupt its offensive rhythm.

Trickster

PASSIVE

Trickster gives Frolic a chance to deal double or quadruple damage. Unlike Peppy's other abilities, this passive depends only on Skill Level.

This creates an unpredictable burst element: the same Frolic cast can range from strong pressure to an extremely damaging hit.

CHAOS COMBINATIONS

BEST SYNERGIES

SIGNATURE INTERACTION

Cleaver + Peppy

Cleaver is one of Peppy's defining partners. When Cleaver pulls an enemy forward with Rusty Hook and activates Putrefaction, his repeated self-inflicted damage can trigger Boldness repeatedly, creating substantial area Magic Damage around him.

Kayla

Kayla jumps into battle and repeatedly damages herself through her own abilities. With Boldness protecting her, those hits can repeatedly trigger Magic Damage against nearby enemies.

Faceless

Power Throw can group enemies together, creating an opportunity for Boldness to pressure multiple targets while an aggressive ally such as Kayla fights among them.

Masquerade can also create a tempo advantage for powerful magical allies such as Lars, Krista, Xe'Sha and Lilith. Disrupting enemy actions gives those heroes additional opportunities to activate their own powerful abilities before the opposition can respond.

STOPPING THE TRICKSTER

COUNTERS & KEY INTERACTIONS

BACK-LINE PRESSURE

Jhu

Jhu's third skill specifically targets back-line heroes, allowing him to reach Peppy and pressure her before she has time to dominate the battle through repeated control.

DAMAGE REDIRECTION

Jorgen

Leper redirects Physical Damage toward the furthest enemy hero. Peppy's back-line position can therefore make her particularly vulnerable to this interaction.

CONTROL PUNISHMENT

Soleil

If Peppy occupies the center position of the enemy team, Soleil can become an effective answer through his ability to deal heavy damage to Control heroes.

MAGIC RESISTANCE

Rufus

Rufus provides a durable frontline answer against Peppy's Magic Damage, absorbing magical pressure while protecting the rest of his team.

VS CLEAVER + PEPPY

Dorian

Against a Cleaver and Peppy combination, Cleaver pulling Dorian into the frontline can place nearby allies inside Dorian's Vampirism Aura, helping them recover through the continuous damage generated by the combination.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

CONTROL THROUGH CHAOS

Peppy's identity is built around control, unpredictability and unusual team interactions.

Masquerade disrupts the entire enemy formation through randomly selected control effects. Frolic combines burst Magic Damage with a stun, while Trickster can dramatically increase Frolic's damage.

But Boldness is what makes many of Peppy's most explosive combinations possible. Heroes such as Cleaver and Kayla can repeatedly trigger its retaliation damage, while Faceless can help group targets into positions where that pressure becomes even more dangerous.

Build her offensive magical stats first, give her enough survivability to remain active, and use her alongside heroes capable of taking advantage of the disruption and unusual mechanics she brings to battle.
