RESOURCE STRATEGY

Know Where the Coins Stop.

Grand Caravan is a good example of why the final milestone and the final Event Coin milestone should not be treated as the same thing.

  1. Use enough of your five free Arena attempts per day to reach 30 battles. You do not need to win.
  2. 4,800 Artifact Coins are enough to collect all 850 Event Coins from Artifacts Empowering. The tab itself continues to 60,000.
  3. 60 Artifact Chests collect all 1,300 Event Coins from Sealed Away. The tab continues to 125 chests.
  4. Wise Exchange gives all 2,000 Event Coins at 80,000 Emeralds spent, together with Alecto's Primordial Skin. The remaining milestones continue to 200,000.
  5. Tactical Losses gives all 5,400 Event Coins by 19,500 Level 6 troops lost. The tab continues to 65,000 troops.
  6. Consider the cost of replacing Realm troops before pursuing Tactical Losses solely for Event Coins.
  7. Generous Soul contains valuable resources but no Event Coins. Judge its VIP milestones according to purchases you already intend to make.
