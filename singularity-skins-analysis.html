DETAILED ANALYSIS

WHAT DOES EACH SKIN BRING?

EVA
HEALTH
SURVIVABILITY UPGRADE

Health is exactly the type of stat Eva benefits from. Her offensive potential is already enormous, while survivability is one of the areas that can limit how much pressure she produces during a battle.

More Health gives Eva additional time to aim, activate her skills and continue applying offensive pressure. It does not change her role — it helps her remain on the battlefield long enough to perform it.

A very useful survivability upgrade for a powerful new Marksman.
NEBULA
HEALTH
STRONG FOR REGULAR USERS

Nebula's survivability relies heavily on Dodge, so additional Health gives her another layer of protection. Keeping Nebula alive is particularly valuable because of how much support she can provide to the entire team.

She can cleanse negative effects, heal, buff allies, increase Movement Speed and Skill Recharge, and provide protection against incoming damage-over-time effects during Disharmony.

Not mandatory for every account, but highly valuable if Nebula is a regular part of your teams.
ISAAC
PHYSICAL ATTACK • SKIN+
USEFUL • NOT URGENT

Physical Attack is a welcome offensive improvement for Isaac because several parts of his kit benefit from the stat. The new skin therefore gives him additional damage potential.

However, Isaac already has access to Physical Attack, Critical Hit Chance and Armor Penetration through his existing skins. For a well-developed Isaac, this is an additional offensive boost rather than a solution to a major weakness.

Good skin and more damage, but it does not need to become an immediate investment if your important Isaac skins are already developed.
PEECH
ARMOR PENETRATION • SKIN+
MAJOR OFFENSIVE UPGRADE

Peech already has the ability to produce a huge amount of physical damage, making Armor Penetration an especially valuable addition to her offensive build.

The new stat gives her another way to push physical damage through enemy Armor and makes her existing damage potential even more threatening.

Armor Penetration is one of the most meaningful new stats introduced among the Singularity Skins.
RESOURCE DECISION

Relic Shards are also available through the event. Consider your own account before choosing: does your Peech benefit more from the new Skin, or would Relic Shards provide greater progress for what you are currently building?

POLARIS
MAGIC PENETRATION
HIGH-VALUE STAT

Magic Penetration fits Polaris extremely well. It helps her magical damage work more effectively against enemy Magic Defense while strengthening the offensive side of a Hero that already provides excellent control and disruption.

Her damage-over-time pressure becomes more threatening, while her damaging abilities gain additional potential against protected targets.

A highly relevant offensive stat if Polaris is an important Hero in your roster.
GINGER
CRITICAL HIT CHANCE
ACCOUNT DEPENDENT

From a pure stat perspective, Critical Hit Chance is exactly the type of offensive improvement Ginger wants. The bigger question is whether Ginger is actually part of your roster.

Eva's arrival creates new possibilities for Marksman teams because her mechanics are designed to work alongside allied Marksmen and help them generate significant offensive pressure.

If Ginger is already developed, or you are testing her alongside Eva, this skin becomes much more interesting. If Ginger has remained unused for a long time, one new skin alone may not justify investing heavily into the Hero from scratch.

Excellent offensive stat for Ginger users — but whether it deserves your resources depends heavily on your account.
META WATCH

ARE MARKSMEN GETTING NEW LIFE?

One of the most interesting parts of Eva's Season is the attention returning to Marksmen. Eva arrives with mechanics specifically designed to empower allied Marksmen, while Ginger receives Critical Hit Chance during the same season. That does not automatically mean Marksmen will dominate the game, but it creates a strong reason to test these Heroes and team structures again and watch how this direction develops.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

INVEST FOR YOUR ACCOUNT

These six skins do not have equal value for every player. Peech's Armor Penetration and Polaris' Magic Penetration are highly relevant offensive additions, while Eva's Health directly addresses the survivability that helps her continue producing damage.

Nebula gains useful extra protection, Isaac receives another offensive boost, and Ginger's Critical Hit Chance becomes particularly interesting for players who already use her or are experimenting with Eva-based Marksman teams.

The most important rule remains simple: do not invest in a skin only because it is new. Look at the Heroes you actually use, the resources available to you, and what will create the most meaningful progress for your own roster.
