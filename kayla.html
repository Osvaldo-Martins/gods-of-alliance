HERO GUIDE

KAYLA

WARRIOR • WAY OF CHAOS • FRONT LINE

Kayla is an explosive Chaos Warrior who dives directly into the enemy formation, creates continuous pressure and becomes increasingly difficult to stop when properly developed. Her build combines Physical Attack, Health and offensive penetration with mechanics that reward her for surviving inside the enemy team.

ABILITIES

SKILLS

SKILL 1

Phoenix's Fury

PHYSICAL ATTACK • HEALTH STEAL • RESURRECTION

Kayla throws her chakram through the battlefield, dealing damage to nearby enemies. Every enemy hit loses a portion of maximum Health based on the damage dealt.

Kayla steals that value for herself, increasing both her current and maximum Health. This allows her to become progressively harder to eliminate as the battle continues.

If Aidan has already fallen, Phoenix's Fury immediately resurrects him with the same amount of Health Kayla has just gained.

Scales with Physical Attack. Increasing Physical Attack strengthens both Kayla's damage and the value she gains from this skill.
SKILL 2

Acrobatics

DIVE • HEALING • CHAOS SYNERGY

If Kayla has more than 40% Health, she jumps directly into the middle of the enemy formation, bypassing the frontline and attacking from inside the opposing team.

When her Health falls below 25%, she begins regenerating Health for several seconds. The healing increases for every allied Chaos Hero fighting beside her.

Once the opportunity appears, Kayla can return to the fight. However, if she was already below 25% Health when Acrobatics activated, she remains in position and focuses on healing.

Scales with Health. More Health improves both Kayla's survivability and the value of one of the defining mechanics in her kit.
SKILL 3

Retaliatory Strike

PHYSICAL DAMAGE • BLIND • RETALIATION

Retaliatory Strike deals Physical Damage to nearby enemies based on the damage Kayla has received since the previous activation.

Every enemy caught by the attack is also Blinded, reducing their ability to successfully land attacks while Kayla continues applying pressure.

Scales with Physical Attack. The combination of retaliatory damage and Blind can punish enemies for focusing Kayla while she is fighting inside their formation.
SKILL 4

Inner Flame

DAMAGE OVER TIME • HEALTH SCALING • SACRIFICE

After Acrobatics activates, Kayla ignites and continuously burns both herself and every nearby enemy.

The effect continues until Kayla's Health falls below 25%. This creates constant pressure while she remains inside the enemy formation.

Scales with Health. Health is therefore not only a defensive stat for Kayla — it also strengthens one of her major offensive mechanics.
BUILD PRIORITIES

SKINS & GLYPHS

Skins

  1. Physical Attack + Super Skin
  2. Default Agility Skin
  3. Armor Penetration
  4. Crushing
  5. Health
  6. Armor

Physical Attack comes first because Phoenix's Fury and Retaliatory Strike scale directly from it. Agility strengthens Kayla's main stat and adds Armor, while Armor Penetration becomes increasingly important against stronger defensive teams.

Glyphs

  1. Health
  2. Physical Attack
  3. Armor
  4. Agility
  5. Magic Defense

Health gives Kayla more time to activate her mechanics and also strengthens her Health-scaling abilities. For most players, bringing every Glyph to Level 40 first provides a strong development foundation.

BUILD PRIORITY

ARTIFACTS

PRIORITY 1

Book

Provides Physical Attack and Armor Penetration — two of Kayla's most important offensive stats. It improves both her abilities and her basic attacks.

PRIORITY 2

Ring

Provides Agility, improving Kayla's main stat and strengthening multiple areas of her overall development.

PRIORITY 3

Weapon

Grants Physical Attack to the entire team whenever Kayla activates Phoenix's Fury. Useful, but maximizing Kayla's personal offensive development comes first.

ADDITIONAL DEVELOPMENT

GIFT OF THE ELEMENTS & TALISMANS

Gift of the Elements

MAX IT.

Kayla fights directly inside the enemy formation. More Health gives her additional survivability, while Physical Attack strengthens her damage and ability scaling.

Talismans

Talisman 1: Agility + Physical Attack.

This is the preferred option in most situations because it strengthens multiple parts of Kayla's kit and provides excellent overall consistency.

Talisman 2: Armor Penetration + Critical Hit Chance.

A strong offensive alternative, particularly against more defensive teams, although it is considerably harder to obtain.

HOW TO FIGHT HER

KAYLA COUNTERS

Somna

Somna can disrupt Kayla after she enters the enemy formation, pushing her away from targets while reducing her Physical Attack and Armor Penetration. This directly reduces Kayla's damage and momentum.

Tempus

Tempus attacks Kayla's core strengths by reducing her Physical Attack and maximum Health while also burning her Energy, making her slower and less threatening.

Guus

Guus can provide enormous healing through his feathers, helping allies survive Kayla's continuous burning pressure and reducing the impact of longer fights.

Miu

Blind can severely disrupt Kayla because of her dependence on Physical Damage. Preventing her from consistently hitting targets limits her ability to do her job.

Drayne

Against a Kayla that has not yet reached very high Health values, Drayne can provide enough damage pressure to eliminate her before she has time to scale.

Corvus

Altar of Souls can punish Kayla's repeated area damage. Corvus may be particularly uncomfortable for her during earlier and mid-game development.

Morrigan

Necromancy prevents Kayla from being resurrected when she is relying on Aidan or Astaroth. Removing resurrection significantly weakens one of the most dangerous parts of the Fire siblings' interaction.

Xe'Sha

If Kayla has the lowest Magic Defense on the battlefield, Xe'Sha can exploit that weakness with massive Magic Damage and potentially eliminate her before she takes control of the battle.

Burst Magic Damage

Kayla needs time to jump into the enemy formation and establish pressure. Rapidly reducing her Health before Acrobatics can do its job can force her into healing mode instead of allowing her to begin her assault.

QUICK SUMMARY

WHY KAYLA IS SO DANGEROUS

Kayla combines explosive Physical Damage, continuous pressure, Health scaling, self-healing and exceptional Chaos synergy. She is designed to enter the enemy formation and become more dangerous the longer she survives. Building her correctly means developing both the offensive stats that power her attacks and the survivability required to keep her active inside enemy lines.
