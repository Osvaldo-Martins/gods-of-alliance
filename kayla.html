HERO GUIDE

KAYLA

WARRIOR • WAY OF CHAOS • FRONT LINE

Kayla is an aggressive front-line Warrior built around mobility, burning pressure and explosive physical damage. Her ability to dive into the enemy formation makes her extremely dangerous, but also means that proper development and survivability are essential.

ABILITIES

SKILLS

SKILL 1

Inferno

DIVE • DAMAGE • BURN

Kayla launches herself into the enemy formation, applying intense pressure directly inside the opposing team.

Her aggressive positioning allows her to threaten vulnerable targets that would normally be protected behind the front line.

Kayla's ability to dive deep into enemy territory is one of her greatest strengths, but it also makes survivability extremely important.
SKILL 2

Overheat

OFFENSIVE PRESSURE

Kayla increases the pressure on enemies affected by her fiery attacks, reinforcing the aggressive damage pattern that defines her playstyle.

Kayla performs best when she can remain active long enough to maintain continuous offensive pressure.
SKILL 3

Flaming Heart

SURVIVABILITY • DAMAGE

Kayla's mechanics reward her for staying engaged in combat, allowing her offensive potential to remain threatening while fighting inside the enemy formation.

Building enough survivability is essential because Kayla often fights much deeper inside the opposing team than traditional front-line Heroes.
SKILL 4

Unquenchable Flame

FIRE • SCALING • PRESSURE

Kayla's final ability reinforces the burning mechanics that define her kit and contributes to the sustained offensive pressure she creates throughout the battle.

Kayla is designed to keep applying pressure rather than simply delivering one isolated attack.

Development Matters

HOW TO FIGHT HER

KAYLA COUNTERS

Control

Control effects can interrupt Kayla's offensive momentum and prevent her from freely applying pressure after entering the enemy formation.

Fast Focus Damage

Because Kayla places herself in dangerous positions, teams that can quickly focus and eliminate her can punish her aggressive playstyle.

Defensive Protection

Strong defensive mechanics can reduce the impact of Kayla's initial pressure and give the defending team time to respond.

Anti-Resurrection

When Kayla is paired with Aidan, resurrection prevention becomes especially valuable because it can stop Phoenix Ritual from repeatedly bringing her back into the battle.

QUICK SUMMARY

HOW TO BUILD KAYLA

Kayla is an aggressive Chaos Warrior who creates pressure by diving directly into the enemy formation. Her build needs to support both sides of that identity: enough offensive development to make her attacks dangerous, and enough survivability to keep her alive while fighting in exposed positions. She becomes particularly threatening when combined with Aidan, whose Phoenix Ritual can bring her back after she falls.
