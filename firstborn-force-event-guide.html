FIRSTBORN FORCE

QUESTS • REWARDS • EVENT COINS • STRATEGY

Ancient Awakening marks the arrival of Metida, the new Elarite Super Titan. The seven-day event contains three connected subevents: Firstborn Force, Unstable Equilibrium and Ancient Awakening.

This guide focuses on the main event systems, Event Shop, Exclusive Bundles, Titan Brawls and all eight tabs of Firstborn Force.

ANCIENT AWAKENING

THE MAIN EVENT

The Ancient Awakening event screen brings the main activities together: event quests, the Event Shop, Exclusive Bundles, Titan Brawls, the Ancients Pass and Emerald offers.

EVENT STRUCTURE

Three Subevents

Firstborn Force, Unstable Equilibrium and Ancient Awakening are connected across the seven-day event.

OUR MAIN COUNT

Event Coins

This guide tracks the Event Coins available from Firstborn Force. Other rewards matter, but they are not added to the Event Coin total.

IMPORTANT

Theoretical Maximums

Daily totals assume every listed milestone can be completed on every event day. They are maximum possibilities, not guaranteed earnings.

SPENDING EVENT COINS

EVENT SHOP

The headline rewards are Metida's Primordial Skin and its Skin+ upgrade. If these are your priority, your Event Coin spending needs to be planned around their combined cost.

METIDA — PRIMORDIAL SKIN PATH

The Primordial Skin costs 100,000 Event Coins. Once Metida is obtained and her Primordial Skin is unlocked, the Skin+ becomes available for another 400,000 Event Coins.

EVENT COINS TOTAL

SKIN+ EFFECT

The Skin+ doubles the skin bonus shown in the shop. At the displayed maximum, the Health bonus rises from 10,000 to 5,255,950. For players seriously developing Metida, this makes the skin path one of the biggest Event Coin decisions of the event.

OTHER SHOP OPTIONS

Titan & Hero Resources

Metida fragments, Pallant and Orm fragments, Cascade and Satori Relic Shards and Titan equipment fragments including the Elarite Crown.

RESOURCE OPTIONS

Development Resources

Titan Skin Stones, Titan Potions, Elemental Spheres, Summoning Spheres and Fairy Dust are also available.

SHOP TIMER

24 Extra Hours

The Event Shop remains open for 24 hours after the event ends, allowing already-earned Event Coins to be spent after the quests close.

ADDITIONAL EVENT SYSTEMS

BUNDLES & TITAN BRAWLS

EXCLUSIVE BUNDLES

Resources & VIP Points

The bundles shown contain different combinations of Rune Stones and VIP Points alongside resources such as Elemental Spheres, Fairy Dust, Bottled Energy, Titan Potions, Summoning Spheres and Gold.

TITAN BRAWLS

Test Metida Maxed

All Titans in the Brawls are maxed. This gives players a chance to observe Metida at maximum development on both attack and defense before deciding how heavily to invest.

BRAWL QUEST

500 Event Coins

The Brawls show a quest rewarding 500 Event Coins for five victories. Watch its reset while Brawls are active. Eternal Marks belong to the Brawls' own shop.

EMERALD OFFERS

The four-times Emerald purchase bonus is scheduled for the second part of the event. Players already planning an Emerald purchase should keep that timing in mind.

DO NOT PLAN AROUND THE MAXIMUM BLINDLY

The maximum requires demanding daily targets involving Fairy Dust, summons, Tower and Artifact Chests, Guild Activity and Raid Leader victories. Calculate the milestones you can realistically complete before treating the theoretical totals as part of your Event Coin budget.

Across the seven tabs outside VIP spending, Firstborn Force provides a theoretical maximum of 235,550 Event Coins. Generous Soul can add another 176,750, bringing the full theoretical total to 412,300 Event Coins if every VIP milestone and every other daily milestone is completed.

WHAT DOES THAT MEAN FOR METIDA?

Metida's Primordial Skin costs 100,000 Event Coins, while obtaining the Skin and Skin+ requires 500,000 Event Coins in total. Firstborn Force alone therefore does not tell us the complete event economy. Unstable Equilibrium and Ancient Awakening can contribute additional Event Coins, and the final decision should consider the totals across all three subevents.
