CROW TALISMAN ANALYSIS: PHYSICAL ATTACK & CRITICAL HIT CHANCE

Crow's second Talisman introduces a fundamentally different approach to his build. Should players prioritize Agility and Armor, or embrace Physical Attack and Critical Hit Chance? We examine both options, their attributes, and how they connect to Crow's abilities.

Two Talismans, Two Different Directions

Crow's two Talismans represent two very different approaches to building the hero.

His original Talisman of Deduction emphasizes defensive protection through Agility and Armor.

His second Talisman of Verdict focuses on offense, providing Physical Attack and Critical Hit Chance.

This is not simply a comparison of Power values. The important question is which combination better complements Crow's abilities and his intended role in battle.

Talisman of Deduction: Defensive Protection

At Level 50, the original Talisman provides 2,000 Agility as its main stat.

All three secondary slots provide Armor. Our current rolls are 4,500, 3,000 and 5,400, totaling 12,900 additional Armor.

That is a substantial defensive investment. Armor protects Crow against Physical Damage, making Deduction especially relevant when physical attackers threaten his survival.

The original Talisman is therefore not useless. It provides a clear defensive alternative for matchups where survivability matters.

Talisman of Verdict: Offensive Potential

Crow's second Talisman provides 8,000 Physical Attack as its main stat.

Its three secondary slots provide Critical Hit Chance. Our current rolls are 1,000, 1,800 and 1,600, totaling 4,400 Critical Hit Chance.

This combination shifts Crow away from additional Armor and toward stronger offensive pressure.

The Physical Attack is particularly important because the damage displayed by Crow's four skills depends on that attribute.

Critical Hit Chance adds another offensive dimension to Crow's build, increasing his potential to deliver critical physical hits when applicable.

The central difference: Deduction invests in keeping Crow alive against Physical Damage, while Verdict invests in helping him deal more damage as he applies pressure through his abilities.

How Physical Attack Connects to Crow's Skills

The strongest argument for Verdict comes from examining Crow's abilities individually.

The damage figures below are the values displayed in our analysis account. They are not universal damage values for every player or battle.

Silent Judgment

Displayed Physical Damage: 191,208

Silent Judgment attacks enemies in the front and applies Silence for eight seconds to enemies whose main stat is Intelligence.

Its displayed damage depends on Physical Attack. This gives Verdict's additional Physical Attack direct relevance to the ability.

The Silence component also demonstrates Crow's role in disrupting Intelligence heroes.

Holy Grenade

Displayed Physical Damage: 100,104

Crow throws a grenade at the enemy with the highest Intelligence, applying Silence for five seconds.

The target and nearby enemies take damage. That damage also depends on Physical Attack.

Holy Grenade reinforces the relationship between Crow's offensive damage and his ability to pressure Intelligence targets.

Swift Retribution

Displayed Physical Damage: 103,104

For twelve seconds, Crow's skills recharge 30% faster, while his basic attacks deliver an additional hit to enemies affected by Silence.

This ability is important because Crow benefits from maintaining Silence and continuing to attack controlled targets.

It contributes to an offensive cycle built around ability usage and repeated pressure rather than a single isolated attack.

Final Verdict

Displayed Physical Damage: 157,656

When Crow applies Silence to an enemy already affected by the same control effect, that enemy takes damage.

The Silence duration is also increased by the duration of the longest Silence already affecting the target.

The displayed damage depends on Physical Attack, creating another connection between Verdict's main stat and Crow's skill mechanics.

Why Silence Is Central to This Build

Crow is not simply a hero who deals Physical Damage. His abilities repeatedly interact with Silence.

Silent Judgment and Holy Grenade apply the effect. Swift Retribution accelerates his skill recharge and provides additional attacks against Silenced enemies. Final Verdict punishes targets when Silence is applied again.

Together, these abilities create a clear offensive identity.

Physical Attack strengthens the damage associated with his skills, while Critical Hit Chance provides additional offensive potential.

Is Losing 12,900 Armor Worth It?

Switching to Verdict means sacrificing the additional Armor from our current Deduction rerolls.

That sacrifice should not be ignored. Against dangerous physical compositions, additional Armor can be valuable.

However, Crow's skills are strongly oriented toward controlling Intelligence targets through Silence.

In matchups where Crow is selected primarily to disrupt these enemies, an offensive Talisman can be a more natural fit for his role.

This does not mean that every Intelligence-based composition lacks Physical Damage, or that Armor becomes irrelevant. The entire enemy team still matters.

The decision is about prioritizing what Crow needs to accomplish in a particular battle.

Physical Attack and Critical Hit Chance

Verdict's offensive identity comes from combining two attributes.

First, its 8,000 Physical Attack directly supports the damage scaling shown in Crow's four abilities.

Second, Critical Hit Chance creates opportunities for critical physical hits, adding offensive potential that the Deduction setup does not provide through its secondary slots.

The exact contribution of critical hits depends on the attacks and damage instances involved. It should not be assumed that every skill damage instance automatically becomes critical.

Nevertheless, the overall direction of Verdict is unmistakable: it is designed to strengthen Crow's offensive capabilities.

Which Crow Talisman Should You Choose?

The Talisman of Deduction remains a valid defensive option.

If Crow is taking heavy Physical Damage and needs additional protection, its Armor rolls may be valuable.

But for a general offensive Crow build, we favor the Talisman of Verdict.

Its Physical Attack directly supports the damage scaling of Crow's abilities, while Critical Hit Chance complements the more aggressive direction of the build.

OUR VERDICT: TALISMAN OF VERDICT

Our preferred general choice is the Talisman of Verdict.

Deduction helps Crow withstand Physical Damage. Verdict focuses on strengthening his offensive contribution.

With Crow's repeated Physical Damage, Silence application, faster skill recharge and punishment of already-Silenced targets, we believe the offensive direction better complements the role we want him to perform.

The original Talisman still deserves consideration in defensive matchups, but Verdict is our preferred overall choice.
