CROW TALISMAN ANALYSIS

DEDUCTION VS VERDICT • PHYSICAL ATTACK • CRITICAL HIT CHANCE

Crow's two Talismans take him in different directions. Talisman of Deduction provides defensive attributes, while Talisman of Verdict focuses on offensive potential. We compare their Level 50 attributes, examine all four skills and explain why the second Talisman is our preferred general choice.

CROW • SECOND TALISMAN

Two Talismans. Two Different Purposes.

Crow's second Talisman is not simply another upgrade. It introduces a different way to develop the hero. Instead of investing his secondary attributes in Armor, players can move toward Physical Attack and Critical Hit Chance.

The important question is not which Talisman looks stronger on the Power screen. It is which set of attributes better supports Crow's abilities, his Silence mechanics and his intended role in battle.

The real trade-off: Switching to Verdict means giving up the additional Armor from Deduction's secondary rolls in exchange for a build more focused on offensive pressure.

THE OFFENSIVE FOUNDATION

Why Physical Attack Matters.

The strongest argument for the Talisman of Verdict comes from Crow's own skill descriptions. The damage values displayed for all four abilities depend on Physical Attack.

This makes Verdict's main attribute particularly relevant. The additional 8,000 Physical Attack contributes to the attribute used in the damage calculations of the skills examined in this analysis.

Critical Hit Chance is a separate offensive benefit. It can increase the likelihood of critical physical hits where critical hits are permitted by the relevant mechanic. However, we should not assume that every individual skill damage instance can critically hit.

Crow's offensive value also depends on his ability to apply Silence, attack controlled enemies and punish repeated Silence applications.

CROW • FOUR SKILL BREAKDOWN

Understanding the Full Kit.

These are the skill damage values displayed in our analysis. They are not guaranteed damage results in every battle. Actual outcomes depend on the combat situation.

SKILL 01 • FRONTLINE PRESSURE

191,208 Displayed Physical Damage

SKILL 02 • INTELLIGENCE TARGETING

Holy Grenade

100,104 Displayed Physical Damage

Crow throws a grenade at the enemy with the highest Intelligence.

The grenade applies Silence for 5 seconds and deals Physical Damage to the target and nearby enemies.

Its displayed damage also depends on Physical Attack, strengthening the case for Verdict's offensive direction.

SKILL 03 • FASTER OFFENSIVE CYCLE

Swift Retribution

103,104 Displayed Physical Damage

Crow's skills recharge 30% faster for 12 seconds.

During this effect, his basic attacks deliver an additional hit to all enemies affected by Silence.

This mechanic connects repeated skill usage, Silence application and continued offensive pressure against controlled targets.

SKILL 04 • SILENCE REAPPLICATION

Final Verdict

157,656 Displayed Physical Damage

When Crow applies Silence to an enemy who is already Silenced, that enemy receives additional damage.

The Silence duration increases by the duration of the longest active Silence affecting the target.

Its damage depends on Physical Attack, providing another direct connection to the second Talisman's main attribute.

THE CORE OF CROW'S MECHANICS

Silence Is More Than Control.

Crow's abilities form a connected system. Silence is not an isolated effect attached to one skill. Multiple abilities interact with it, creating opportunities for continued damage and control.

Silent Judgment

02 / TARGET

Holy Grenade

03 / PRESSURE

Important distinction: Talisman of Verdict does not create a new Silence mechanic. It strengthens Crow's offensive attributes while his existing abilities continue to apply and exploit Silence.

BUILD STRENGTHS AND TRADE-OFFS

What Does Crow Gain — and Lose?

Physical Attack Scaling

Verdict provides 8,000 Physical Attack, an attribute used in the displayed damage calculations of Crow's four abilities.

Critical Hit Potential

The three secondary rolls introduce Critical Hit Chance, supporting an offensive approach where eligible physical hits can benefit from critical strikes.

Less Defensive Armor

Replacing Deduction removes the additional Armor provided by its secondary attributes. This can matter against dangerous physical teams.

Greater Offensive Focus

Verdict places more emphasis on Crow's damage contribution while his skills continue to disrupt and punish Intelligence-based enemies.

THE DEFENSIVE ALTERNATIVE

Is Deduction Still Worth Using?

Yes. The original Talisman still has a purpose. Its Armor can be valuable in matchups where incoming Physical Damage threatens Crow's survival.

Crow needs to remain active long enough to use his abilities. If he is eliminated too quickly, the theoretical benefit of stronger offensive attributes may not translate into practical battle value.

That is where Deduction remains relevant. Against certain physical compositions, its defensive investment can help protect Crow.

Verdict is our preferred general choice, but the entire opposing team still matters. A matchup against Intelligence heroes does not automatically mean Crow will face no significant Physical Damage.

GODS OF ALLIANCE • FINAL ANALYSIS

OUR VERDICT: TALISMAN OF VERDICT.

Our preferred general choice for Crow is the Talisman of Verdict.

Its Physical Attack directly supports the damage scaling displayed in Crow's abilities. Critical Hit Chance adds further offensive potential, while his Silence mechanics continue to provide control and repeated pressure.

The Talisman of Deduction remains a legitimate defensive alternative. When additional Armor is necessary for Crow to survive, the original Talisman may be the better situational choice.

However, when the priority is strengthening Crow's offensive contribution, Verdict offers the more appealing direction for the role we want him to perform.

The first Talisman helps Crow survive the battle. The second Talisman helps Crow do the job we brought him into the battle to do.

This analysis uses the Talisman attributes, secondary rolls and displayed skill values recorded in our comparison. Secondary rolls vary between accounts, and actual combat damage depends on battle conditions.
