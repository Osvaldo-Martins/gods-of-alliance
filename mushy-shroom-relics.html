MUSHY & SHROOM
LEGENDARY RELICS

Four Legendary upgrades. A growing army of Shrooms. Discover how Voracious Spores, Perfect Copy, Wild Growth and Mycelial Echo can work together.

LEGENDARY RELICS · COMPLETE BREAKDOWN

One Mushroom Army. Four Connected Upgrades.

Mushy and Shroom's Legendary Relics don't simply add four unrelated bonuses. They build on the hero's existing summoning and healing mechanics, combining periodic magic damage, Maximum Health steal, additional Magic Penetration and healing triggered by the enemy.

The most interesting possibility is how the later upgrades interact with the earlier ones: active Shrooms can gain Voracious Spores, healing can grant Magic Penetration, and enemy healing can help inactive Shrooms become active.

Important: this is a mechanics-based analysis of the Relic descriptions. Actual performance, uptime and team matchups still require battle testing.

FIRST LEGENDARY RELIC
LEVEL 1
GREEN · NEW ABILITY

Voracious Spores

Five seconds after battle begins, Mushy deals magic damage to the enemy with the highest Maximum Health. The ability activates again every 12 seconds.

Voracious Spores also steals Maximum Health equal to 50% of the damage dealt. Its damage scales with Mushy's Magic Attack.

100,000 EXAMPLE · MAXIMUM HEALTH STOLEN
Why this matters

This is not simply ordinary damage or healing missing Health. It directly interacts with an opponent's Maximum Health, potentially making it interesting against high-Health targets. However, its real effectiveness against different Tanks remains to be tested.

SECOND LEGENDARY RELIC
LEVEL 10
BLUE · PERFECT COPY UPGRADE

Shrooms Gain Voracious Spores

Perfect Copy summons inactive Shrooms with missing Health. They inherit Mushy's starting stats and can receive attacks, offensive spells and healing. Once fully healed for the first time, a Shroom becomes active and can attack and use learned skills, but cannot replenish Energy. Mushy can summon up to five Shrooms.

The Level 10 Legendary Relic changes what happens after activation: an active Shroom receives the Relic and begins using Voracious Spores after five seconds.

The real potential

Level 1's damage and Maximum Health steal are no longer limited to the original Mushy. Multiple active Shrooms can potentially add further Voracious Spores pressure.

Important condition: Shrooms must become active and survive long enough to use the ability. Do not assume all five will always be attacking simultaneously.

THIRD LEGENDARY RELIC
LEVEL 20
PURPLE · WILD GROWTH UPGRADE

Healing Grants Magic Penetration

Wild Growth normally heals inactive Shrooms and Spore Mushrooms. The healing scales with Health and Magic Attack and stacks according to the number of active Shrooms on the battlefield.

The Legendary upgrade adds a new offensive interaction: whenever Mushy or an active Shroom receives healing, they gain additional Magic Penetration for four seconds.

The effect stacks for every active Shroom on the battlefield, and its value depends on Magic Attack. In the example from the analysis, the Magic Penetration bonus is 9,559; this is an example value, not a universal fixed bonus.

Why this matters

Healing now contributes to offensive power, helping magical attacks overcome enemy Magic Defense. The effect also connects to Level 10: active Shrooms may use Voracious Spores while benefiting from Magic Penetration.

Important limitation: the bonus lasts only four seconds. Its consistency in real battles remains to be tested.

FOURTH LEGENDARY RELIC
LEVEL 30
GOLD · NEW ABILITY

Mycelial Echo

Whenever an enemy receives healing, Mushy and every Shroom restore Health equal to 50% of the amount healed. This includes inactive Shrooms.

100,000 EXAMPLE · HEALTH RESTORED TO MUSHY
100,000 EXAMPLE · HEALTH RESTORED TO EACH SHROOM, UP TO ITS HEALTH LIMIT

This creates a remarkable interaction with Perfect Copy: enemy healing can help inactive Shrooms recover the Health needed to become active. Once active, those Shrooms can benefit from the Level 10 upgrade and use Voracious Spores.

Mycelial Echo's healing can also trigger the Level 20 Magic Penetration bonus for Mushy and active Shrooms, because that upgrade activates when they receive healing.

Two critical limitations

First: Mycelial Echo does not trigger when the enemy's healing was itself triggered by a similar effect. It should not be interpreted as an endless healing chain.

Second: this Relic does not steal, cancel or prevent enemy healing. The enemy still receives its healing; Mushy and the Shrooms gain their own healing in response.

HOW THE RELICS CONNECT

The Four-Relic Chain Reaction

The strength of this set is not just the individual bonuses, but the way each upgrade reinforces the next.

LEVEL 1 · GREEN

Introduce the Damage

Voracious Spores periodically deals magic damage to the highest-Maximum-Health enemy and steals Maximum Health based on damage dealt.

LEVEL 10 · BLUE

Multiply the Users

Active Shrooms gain access to Voracious Spores, potentially adding further sources of damage and Maximum Health steal.

LEVEL 20 · PURPLE

Turn Healing into Penetration

Healing grants Mushy and active Shrooms a four-second Magic Penetration bonus, stacking according to active Shrooms.

LEVEL 30 · GOLD

Respond to Enemy Healing

Enemy healing restores Mushy and every Shroom, potentially activating inactive summons and triggering the Level 20 bonus.

The standout interaction

An enemy healer restores Health → Mycelial Echo heals Mushy and the Shrooms → inactive Shrooms may reach activation → active Shrooms can use Voracious Spores → healing received by Mushy and active Shrooms can activate Magic Penetration.

This is a potential sequence of interactions, not a guarantee that every stage will occur in every battle.

BATTLE TESTING · OPEN QUESTIONS

What Still Needs to Be Proven?

How reliably can inactive Shrooms reach full Health and become active?
How long do active Shrooms survive against high-damage teams?
How much total damage does Voracious Spores add across a complete battle?
How effective is Mycelial Echo against teams with different amounts of healing?
How consistently can the four-second Magic Penetration bonus be maintained?
Which matchups benefit most from the Maximum Health steal and healing interactions?

These are important questions for real testing. The descriptions show promising potential, but they do not establish that Mushy and Shroom are unbeatable or automatically counter every healing-based team.

GODS OF ALLIANCE · FINAL VERDICT

Level 30 Is the Most Fascinating Upgrade

Level 1 creates a new offensive threat. Level 10 extends it to active Shrooms. Level 20 converts healing into additional Magic Penetration. And Level 30 creates a new healing source that can reinforce the other upgrades.

Mycelial Echo stands out not merely because it restores Health, but because that healing can help inactive Shrooms become active and interact with Wild Growth's Legendary bonus.

The full set offers exciting potential against teams that depend heavily on healing, but the true impact must be established through battle testing.
