01 / FORT WARFARE OVERVIEW

A New Battle for Realm Territory.

Forts introduce a new guild-focused Realm mode built around territory control, coordinated attacks, player-controlled Garrisons and Guild-wide bonuses.

Unlike a simple boss encounter, capturing a Fort requires preparation, multiple battles and contributions from participating guild members. A guild must first overcome the defending Garrison before engaging the Fort Guards.

Multiple guilds can compete for the same Fort. The winner is determined by total Capture Points when the final Fort Guard is defeated.

This guide covers the mechanics presented in our first look at Forts. Advanced tactics and optimal Garrison compositions will be explored as further testing becomes available.

02 / SELECT YOUR TARGET

Find a Fort. Declare War.

Every siege begins on the Realm map. The Guild Master or a General must find an available Fort before declaring war.

01

Locate a Fort

Search the Realm map and identify a potential target for your guild.

02

Check Availability

Some Forts are occupied, protected or already under siege. Confirm that your target is eligible for an attack.

03

Declare War

The Guild Master or a General declares war, triggering the preparation countdown.

Important: Not every Fort can be attacked at any moment. Fort status must be checked before committing your guild to an operation.
03 / THE COUNTDOWN

30 Minutes to Prepare.

Declaring war does not begin combat immediately. It activates a 30-minute preparation phase designed for guild organization and coordination.

01

Gather Your Guild

Bring participating players together around the target Fort and make sure they know when the siege begins.

02

Assign Roles and Attack Orders

Discuss who attacks first, how players coordinate and which squads will be used during the siege.

03

Prepare Squads

Check that your participating members have suitable squads ready before the preparation timer expires.

04

Confirm the Plan

Use the remaining preparation time to coordinate participation and avoid confusion once the battle opens.

The key principle: Preparation is part of the battle. A guild that organizes during these 30 minutes enters the siege with a clear plan.
04 / SIEGE MECHANICS

Break the Garrison. Conquer the Fort.

Fort battles have two distinct combat stages. The attacking guild must first defeat the defending players' squads before it can engage the Fort Guards.

The Siege Route

STAGE 01 ATTACKING GUILD

Participating players enter the siege and launch their attacks.

→
STAGE 02 GARRISON

Defeat the real squads stationed by the defending guild.

→
STAGE 03 FORT GUARDS

Once all defending squads fall, engage the Guards and complete the siege.

OFFENSIVE STAGE ONE

The Garrison

The Garrison contains real player squads deployed by the guild currently controlling the Fort. Attackers must overcome every defending squad before reaching the Guards.

  • Player-controlled defensive squads
  • First obstacle during the siege
  • Must be cleared before the Guards
OFFENSIVE STAGE TWO

The Fort Guards

After the Garrison has been eliminated, attacking guilds can engage the Fort Guards. Defeating the final Guard triggers the comparison of participating guilds' Capture Point totals.

  • Accessible after Garrison elimination
  • Contributes to Capture Points
  • Final Guard defeat resolves ownership
05 / HOW VICTORY IS MEASURED

Capture Points Decide Everything.

Winning individual fights matters, but the Fort is awarded according to the total Capture Points accumulated by each participating guild.

How Your Guild Earns Capture Points

01

Defeating Garrison Squads

Eliminating defending player squads contributes to the guild's total.

02

Defeating Fort Guards

Eliminating Guards also contributes toward Capture Points.

03

Dealing Damage

Damage inflicted during the siege contributes toward the guild's overall score.

Every contribution counts. The system rewards guild-wide participation, not just the player who finishes the final battle.
06 / COMPETING GUILDS

One Fort. Multiple Challengers.

Several guilds may declare war on the same Fort. Each attacking guild has its own preparation countdown and attack window.

A

Guild Alpha

Declares war, prepares and begins earning Capture Points.

B

Guild Bravo

May also challenge the same Fort with its own preparation and attack timing.

★

Highest Score Wins

When the final Guard is defeated, the guild with the highest total Capture Points becomes the owner.

Critical distinction: The Fort does not automatically belong to whichever guild lands the final attack. Ownership is determined by the highest total Capture Points when the siege concludes.
07 / DEFENDING YOUR TERRITORY

Capture the Fort. Build the Garrison.

Once your guild captures a Fort, it becomes responsible for defending it. Guild members can place their own squads inside the Garrison to protect the objective.

THE DEFENDING GUILD

Build Your Defensive Wall

Station member squads inside the Fort. These squads become the first line of defense that future attackers must defeat before reaching the Fort Guards.

  • Organize guild member defenses
  • Prepare strong Garrison squads
  • Protect the route to the Guards
THE ATTACKING GUILD

Break Through the Defense

Rival guilds must overcome the Garrison before attacking the Guards. A well-organized defense can force attackers to commit additional effort and coordinate more carefully.

  • Fight through player squads
  • Clear the defensive line
  • Reach the Fort Guards
Capturing a Fort wins the battle. Defending it protects your guild's position.
08 / WHY FORTS MATTER

Rewards. Territory. Guild Bonuses.

Forts are valuable because they provide rewards and benefits to guilds that participate in and control them.

🏰

Fort Rewards

Different Forts offer different rewards. More powerful Forts provide more valuable prizes and become attractive strategic targets.

⚔️

Neutral Fort Participation

Capturing your first Neutral Fort can unlock special participation rewards. Players must actively contribute and earn Capture Points to qualify.

👑

Guild-wide Bonuses

A captured Fort provides a Guild Bonus to every guild member while the guild controls it. Lose the Fort, and the bonus is lost.

Participation matters: Simply belonging to a guild is not enough to qualify for the special participation rewards. Players need to contribute Capture Points during the siege.

GODS OF ALLIANCE • FINAL VERDICT

Conquering Is Only the Beginning.

Realm Forts introduces a continuous cycle of attack, defense and territorial competition. Guilds must organize their members, defeat Garrison squads, eliminate Fort Guards and earn enough Capture Points to secure ownership.

The rewards create a reason to fight, but the Guild Bonuses create a reason to defend. Every successful siege becomes the beginning of another strategic challenge.

The strongest guilds are built through preparation, coordination and strategy. Master the mechanics. Lead your guild. Conquer the Realm.
