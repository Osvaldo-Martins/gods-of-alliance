FULL COST

The complete resource reference for Titan Levels, evolution, Artifacts and Skins — with Metida as a practical full-build example.

How many resources does it actually take to fully upgrade a Titan in Hero Wars Alliance? This guide separates every major progression system so you can see exactly what each total includes — and what it does not.
Titan Level Titan Stars Artifacts Titan Skins
01 • TITAN LEVEL

Level 1 → Level 120

Titan level progression uses Titan Potions. The complete total below covers the progression from Level 1 through Level 120.

COMPLETE LEVELING COST
686,760
Titan Potions
Level 1 → Level 120. Soul Stones, Artifacts and Skins are separate progression systems and are not included in this number.
IMPORTANT

If your Titan is already partially leveled, 686,760 is not your remaining cost. Level costs are not evenly distributed, so the full total cannot simply be divided by the number of levels.
