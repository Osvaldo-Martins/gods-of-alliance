01 / RESEARCH OVERVIEW

The Foundation of Long-Term Power.

The Realm Research Center is one of the most important progression buildings. It provides upgrades for Realm growth, resource economy, troop strength and Hero combat statistics.

Your Realm can have two Research Centers, both upgradeable to level 25. Once the second research queue is unlocked, you can conduct two researches simultaneously.

Both buildings open the same technology menu. At the bottom of that menu, you can monitor both active researches and their remaining time.

Research technologies form connected trees. Each technology can have multiple levels, and progression may require earlier upgrades, a minimum Research Center level, Realm resources or Honor Marks.

12 / STRATEGIC DEVELOPMENT

Research With Purpose.

Efficient Realm development comes from keeping research active, meeting unlock requirements and investing in technologies that support your account's goals.

01

Keep both research queues active. Once unlocked, simultaneous research reduces wasted development time.

02

Invest in Research Speed. Research Center building bonuses and Tool Improvement help future progression.

03

Plan your PRO unlocks. Troops PRO needs 40% Troops progress, while Growth PRO and Economy PRO each require 80% progress in their corresponding tree, alongside Research Center level 15.

04

Collect Honor Marks through Guild Versus. They are essential for advanced technologies and class research.

05

Prioritize Hero improvements deliberately. Universal Hero research benefits every Hero, while Tactical and Combat Classes target specific roles.

06

Collect research vial rewards. Completed research contributes vials toward the research chest, which provides additional rewards.

07

Check My Bonuses. Compare active improvements with maximum level-table values so you understand what your account has actually gained.

GODS OF ALLIANCE • FINAL VERDICT

Knowledge Is Power. Research Makes It Permanent.

The Research Center is not simply a place to spend Realm resources. It is a long-term progression system that strengthens your economy, troops, marches and Heroes.
