LILITH

MAGE • WAY OF CHAOS • BACK LINE

Survive, scale and overwhelm the enemy. Lilith combines area Magic Damage, permanent Health growth and Magic Defense reduction with powerful Chaos interactions.

Lilith is a Chaos Mage positioned on the back line. Her strength comes from scaling throughout the battle: increasing her Health, sacrificing Health for Magic Attack and creating growing pressure as the fight continues.

LILITH'S KIT

SKILLS

Underworld Seal

HEALTH SCALING

Lilith places a demonic seal on the central enemy. It explodes, dealing Magic Damage to the target and nearby enemies. For every enemy hit and every allied Chaos hero on the battlefield, Lilith permanently increases both her current and maximum Health.

Demonic Fury

SUSTAINED DAMAGE

For 10 seconds, Lilith attacks twice with every basic attack. Her strikes also target the central enemy while dealing additional area Magic Damage, increasing her sustained battlefield pressure.

Fire Breath

MAGIC DEFENSE REDUCTION

Lilith breathes flames toward the central enemy, damaging nearby opponents while reducing their Magic Defense for 8 seconds. This creates an important offensive window for allied Magic Damage dealers.

Sacrifice

SCALING ENGINE

Whenever Lilith's Health is above 25%, she sacrifices 5% of her maximum Health before casting a skill. In return, she gains a temporary Magic Attack bonus based on the Health sacrificed. The bonus lasts 10 seconds.

HOW LILITH SCALES

THE LATE-BATTLE ENGINE

Lilith is designed around surviving long enough for her mechanics to become increasingly difficult to contain.

Underworld Seal permanently increases her current and maximum Health.
More Health makes Lilith harder to eliminate and strengthens the value of her Health-based mechanics.
Sacrifice converts part of her maximum Health into temporary Magic Attack.
Fire Breath reduces enemy Magic Defense.
Demonic Fury increases her sustained pressure across the enemy formation.

CHAOS INTERACTIONS

SYNERGIES & MECHANICS

Xe'Sha

One of Lilith's strongest interactions. Fire Breath lowers enemy Magic Defense while Xe'Sha's Deadly Ray naturally targets heroes with the lowest Magic Defense.

Xe'Sha + Sacrifice

Lilith's sacrifices activate Xe'Sha's passive, granting Xe'Sha permanent Magic Attack. Spiritual Power can then share growing Magic Attack back to the team, strengthening Lilith further.

Cleaver

Pulling enemies toward the frontline can group targets together, allowing Underworld Seal to hit multiple enemies more consistently.

Lars

His vortex can gather the enemy team, creating stronger opportunities for Underworld Seal to hit several targets.

Aidan

Aidan is highlighted as one of the heroes that can be paired with Lilith inside Chaos compositions built around longer scaling battles.

Chaos Heroes

Underworld Seal gains additional Health-growth value for every allied Chaos hero on the battlefield, reinforcing Lilith's natural connection to Chaos compositions.

STOP THE SNOWBALL

COUNTERS

ENERGY CONTROL

Jorgen

His ability to steal Energy can delay Lilith's Ultimate, slowing down the snowball mechanics that make her more threatening as the battle continues.

ANTI-MAGIC

Rufus

Rufus can absorb the overwhelming majority of Lilith's Magic Damage, forcing Chaos compositions into longer and less efficient fights.

CHARM

Lian

When Lilith attacks Lian with an offensive ability, Lian's Charm can interrupt Lilith's aggression and break her offensive rhythm.

POSITIONING

Cleaver

Cleaver can pull Lilith from the safety of the back line directly toward the frontline, exposing her before she has enough time to scale.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

SURVIVE. SCALE. OVERWHELM.

Lilith is built around becoming stronger as a battle lasts. She is not designed around immediate elimination of the enemy.

Every cast of Underworld Seal can increase her Health. Sacrifice turns part of that Health into offensive power. Fire Breath weakens enemy Magic Defense, while Demonic Fury increases her sustained pressure.

Her interaction with Xe'Sha pushes that scaling even further, while Chaos allies can increase the value Lilith receives from Underworld Seal.

If you build Lilith around survivability, scaling and Magic Damage, she can become increasingly difficult to stop as the fight progresses.
