METIDA'S KIT

SKILLS

Crystal Vortex

FIRST SKILL

Crystal Vortex traps the central enemy in a whirlwind for five seconds. It deals damage, moves that Titan around the battlefield and prevents it from moving, attacking or using skills while trapped.

Supreme Harmony

SECOND SKILL

Supreme Harmony empowers allies for nine seconds. Its bonuses depend on the surviving Titans in your team.

FIRE Adds healing.
WATER Increases Physical Attack.
EARTH Increases speed.
ELARITE Strengthens an ally's damage, prioritising high Attack.

MAXED TESTING

FIRST BATTLE RESULTS

The training results immediately showed why Metida should not be judged only by her individual power. Some combinations won all ten attempts. Others lost all ten.

Simply placing a powerful Supertitan into a team does not guarantee a strong formation. We then moved to tests against maxed teams, with four battles standing out.

BATTLE 1

Fire with Metida vs Earth

Araji • Asherona • Ignis • Alecto • Metida

Crystal Vortex interrupts the enemy formation while the Fire team maintains pressure. Araji leads the damage, while Metida adds substantial damage and healing.

EARLY ANALYSIS

FIRST IMPRESSIONS

Metida is a very promising Supertitan. Her control is useful, her bonuses open several team combinations, and these first tests already show serious damage and healing in the right battles.

The losing tests matter just as much. Her performance depends on the lineup and the opponent. These results are a starting point, not a universal answer to every matchup.

There is also a major piece still missing: the fifth Elarite Titan. Once that Titan arrives, the capabilities of a complete Elarite team could change significantly.

Metida will also need to be revisited when her Primordial Skin becomes available.
